# な-adjectives — descriptive adjectives needing な/です

**JLPT level:** N5

Adjectives that take な before a noun and use だ/です (not くない) to negate; grammatically behave more like nouns than い-adjectives do.

## Example
- **JP:** 彼女は親切な人です。
- **Reading:** kanojo wa shinsetsu na hito desu.
- **EN:** She is a kind person.
