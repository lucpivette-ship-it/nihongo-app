# Grammar — N2 Batch 5 (20 points)

## naku wa nai / naku mo nai — it's not that; I may (double negative)
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb/adjective + くはない/くもない to softly concede a partial possibility, a double-negative hedge.

**JP:** 行きたくなくはないですが、時間がありません。
**Reading:** Ikitakunaku wa nai desu ga, jikan ga arimasen.
**EN:** It's not that I don't want to go, but I don't have time.

## nakute sumu — get by without doing
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb + なくて済む to describe avoiding a necessary but undesirable action.

**JP:** 幸い、手術しなくて済みました。
**Reading:** Saiwai, shujutsu shinakute sumimashita.
**EN:** Fortunately, I got by without needing surgery.

## nani mo~nai — nothing; (not) ~ at all; there's no need to
**JLPT:** N2

Pairs 何も with a negative predicate for total denial or dismissal.

**JP:** 何も心配することはありません。
**Reading:** Nani mo shinpai suru koto wa arimasen.
**EN:** There's nothing to worry about at all.

## nao — still; yet; furthermore; in addition
**JLPT:** N2

A formal adverb or conjunction adding emphasis to a continuing state, or introducing supplementary information.

**JP:** 年を取ってもなお、彼は元気に働いています。
**Reading:** Toshi o tottemo nao, kare wa genki ni hataraite imasu.
**EN:** Even now that he's aged, he still works energetically.

## neba naranai — have to do; must; should
**JLPT:** N2

A literary suffix attaching to the negative stem of a verb + ねばならない to express strong obligation.

**JP:** 明日までにレポートを出さねばなりません。
**Reading:** Ashita made ni repooto o dasaneba narimasen.
**EN:** I must submit the report by tomorrow.

## ni atatte — at the time; on the occasion of
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/verb + にあたって to mark a significant, formal occasion for an action.

**JP:** 新しい仕事を始めるにあたって、目標を立てました。
**Reading:** Atarashii shigoto o hajimeru ni atatte, mokuhyou o tatemashita.
**EN:** On the occasion of starting a new job, I set some goals.

## ni hoka naranai — nothing but; none other than
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + にほかならない to assert emphatically that something is precisely and only one thing.

**JP:** 彼の成功は努力の結果にほかなりません。
**Reading:** Kare no seikou wa doryoku no kekka ni hoka narimasen.
**EN:** His success is nothing other than the result of his effort.

## ni kagirazu — not just; not only... but also
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + に限らず to state that a fact applies beyond just the specific case mentioned.

**JP:** 日本人に限らず、多くの外国人もこの祭りが好きです。
**Reading:** Nihonjin ni kagirazu, ooku no gaikokujin mo kono matsuri ga suki desu.
**EN:** Not only Japanese people, but many foreigners also love this festival.

## ni kagiru — is best; nothing is better than
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + に限る to state a strong personal preference as the definitively best option.

**JP:** 疲れた時は、ゆっくり休むに限ります。
**Reading:** Tsukareta toki wa, yukkuri yasumu ni kagirimasu.
**EN:** When you're tired, there's nothing better than resting properly.

## ni kagitte — only; in particular
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + に限って to note an ironic exception where something unexpectedly bad happens only in a specific case.

**JP:** 忙しい時に限って、お客さんが来ます。
**Reading:** Isogashii toki ni kagitte, okyakusan ga kimasu.
**EN:** It's always exactly when I'm busy that customers show up.

## ni kakawarazu — in spite of; regardless of
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + に関わらず to state that an outcome is unaffected by a given condition.

**JP:** 年齢に関わらず、誰でも参加できます。
**Reading:** Nenrei ni kakawarazu, dare demo sanka dekimasu.
**EN:** Regardless of age, anyone can participate.

## ni kakawaru — to relate to; to have to do with; relating to
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + に関わる to describe a serious matter that directly concerns or affects something important.

**JP:** これは命に関わる重大な問題です。
**Reading:** Kore wa inochi ni kakawaru juudai na mondai desu.
**EN:** This is a serious matter that concerns life itself.

## ni kimatte iru — certainly; I'm sure/certain that; it must be the case that
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a plain-form clause + に決まっている to express strong personal conviction about an outcome.

**JP:** そんなことをしたら、怒られるに決まっています。
**Reading:** Sonna koto o shitara, okorareru ni kimatte imasu.
**EN:** If you do something like that, you're definitely going to get scolded.

## ni koshita koto wa nai — it's best that, there's nothing better than
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a plain-form clause + に越したことはない to state that a given option is ideal, though not strictly mandatory.

**JP:** 早く準備するに越したことはありません。
**Reading:** Hayaku junbi suru ni koshita koto wa arimasen.
**EN:** It's best to prepare early.

## ni kotaete — in response to
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + に応えて to describe an action taken to fulfill a request, expectation, or demand.

**JP:** ファンの声援に応えて、アンコールを演奏しました。
**Reading:** Fan no seien ni kotaete, ankooru o ensou shimashita.
**EN:** In response to the fans' cheers, they played an encore.

## ni kuwaete — in addition
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + に加えて to add an additional element on top of what was previously stated.

**JP:** 英語に加えて、中国語も勉強しています。
**Reading:** Eigo ni kuwaete, chuugokugo mo benkyou shite imasu.
**EN:** In addition to English, I'm also studying Chinese.

## ni motozuite — based on; on the basis of
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + に基づいて to describe an action, judgment, or work grounded in a specific source or standard.

**JP:** この小説は実話に基づいています。
**Reading:** Kono shousetsu wa jitsuwa ni motozuite imasu.
**EN:** This novel is based on a true story.

## ni mukatte — to face; to go towards; to head to
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + に向かって to describe physically or figuratively moving or facing in a certain direction.

**JP:** 彼は夢に向かって努力しています。
**Reading:** Kare wa yume ni mukatte doryoku shite imasu.
**EN:** He's working hard toward his dream.

## ni oujite — depending on; in accordance with
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + に応じて to describe an action or condition that varies appropriately based on circumstances.

**JP:** 収入に応じて、税金の額が変わります。
**Reading:** Shuunyuu ni oujite, zeikin no gaku ga kawarimasu.
**EN:** The amount of tax changes depending on your income.

## ni saishite — on the occasion of; at the time of
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + に際して to mark a formal, significant occasion for an action, similar to にあたって.

**JP:** 卒業に際して、一言お祝いを申し上げます。
**Reading:** Sotsugyou ni saishite, hitokoto oiwai o moushiagemasu.
**EN:** On the occasion of your graduation, allow me to offer a word of congratulations.
