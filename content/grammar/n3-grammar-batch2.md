# Grammar — N3 Batch 2 (20 points)

## doushitemo — no matter what; at any cost; after all
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb expressing a strong, unshakeable will or an unavoidable circumstance.

**JP:** どうしても彼に会いたいです。
**Reading:** Doushitemo kare ni aitai desu.
**EN:** I want to see him no matter what.

## furi o suru — to pretend; to act as if
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb or noun + ふりをする to describe feigning a state or action.

**JP:** 彼は知らないふりをしました。
**Reading:** Kare wa shiranai furi o shimashita.
**EN:** He pretended not to know.

## futo — suddenly; unexpectedly; unintentionally
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb describing a thought or action that arises spontaneously, without prior intention.

**JP:** ふと昔のことを思い出しました。
**Reading:** Futo mukashi no koto o omoidashimashita.
**EN:** I suddenly remembered something from the past.

## gachi — tend to; tendency to; often
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb or a noun + がち to describe a recurring, often undesirable, tendency.

**JP:** 最近、遅刻がちです。
**Reading:** Saikin, chikokugachi desu.
**EN:** I've been tending to be late recently.

## gatai — very difficult to; hard to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + がたい to describe something emotionally or practically hard to do.

**JP:** その話は信じがたいです。
**Reading:** Sono hanashi wa shinjigatai desu.
**EN:** That story is hard to believe.

## gimi — -like; tending to; a bit ~
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + 気味 to describe a slight tendency toward a state, often physical.

**JP:** 最近、風邪気味です。
**Reading:** Saikin, kazegimi desu.
**EN:** I've been feeling a bit like I have a cold lately.

## goto ni — each; every; at intervals of
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + ごとに to describe something occurring at regular intervals or for each unit.

**JP:** このバスは一時間ごとに来ます。
**Reading:** Kono basu wa ichijikan goto ni kimasu.
**EN:** This bus comes every hour.

## hodo — degree; extent; to the point that
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches after a clause to describe the extent or degree of a state, often "to the point that."

**JP:** 声が出ないほど疲れました。
**Reading:** Koe ga denai hodo tsukaremashita.
**EN:** I got so tired I could barely speak.

## hodo~nai — is not as... as
**JLPT:** N3

Pairs ほど with a negative predicate to compare two things, saying one doesn't reach the degree of the other.

**JP:** 今日は昨日ほど寒くないです。
**Reading:** Kyou wa kinou hodo samukunai desu.
**EN:** Today isn't as cold as yesterday.

## ichido ni — all at once
**JLPT:** N3

An adverbial phrase describing multiple things happening simultaneously in a single instance.

**JP:** 質問を一度にしないでください。
**Reading:** Shitsumon o ichido ni shinaide kudasai.
**EN:** Please don't ask all your questions at once.

## ikura~temo — no matter how (much)
**JLPT:** N3

Pairs いくら with a ても-form clause to emphasize that no amount or degree changes the result.

**JP:** いくら食べても太りません。
**Reading:** Ikura tabetemo futorimasen.
**EN:** No matter how much I eat, I don't gain weight.

## ippou da — more and more; continue to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + 一方だ to describe a trend continuing in one direction, often negative.

**JP:** 物価は上がる一方です。
**Reading:** Bukka wa agaru ippou desu.
**EN:** Prices just keep going up and up.

## ittai — what on earth; what in the world
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb of emphasis used before a question word to express surprise, confusion, or frustration.

**JP:** 一体何が起きたのですか。
**Reading:** Ittai nani ga okita no desu ka.
**EN:** What on earth happened?

## janai — right?; I bet; isn't it (confirmation)
**JLPT:** N3

A casual tag used to seek agreement, express surprise, or state a likely conclusion.

**JP:** 明日は休みじゃない？
**Reading:** Ashita wa yasumi janai?
**EN:** Tomorrow's a day off, right?

## ka nani ka — or something
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches after a noun + か何か to give a vague, non-specific example.

**JP:** お茶か何か飲みますか。
**Reading:** Ocha ka nanika nomimasu ka.
**EN:** Would you like some tea or something?

## kakeru — half-done; in the middle of
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + かける to describe an action left unfinished or just beginning.

**JP:** 食べかけのケーキがあります。
**Reading:** Tabekake no keeki ga arimasu.
**EN:** There's a half-eaten cake.

## kara~ni kakete — through; from [A] to [B]
**JLPT:** N3

Pairs から and にかけて to describe a broad, somewhat vague range of time or place.

**JP:** 今夜から明日にかけて雨が降るでしょう。
**Reading:** Kon'ya kara ashita ni kakete ame ga furu deshou.
**EN:** It will likely rain from tonight through tomorrow.

## kawari ni — instead of; in exchange for
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/clause + 代わりに to describe a substitution or reciprocal exchange.

**JP:** 妹の代わりに私が行きます。
**Reading:** Imouto no kawari ni watashi ga ikimasu.
**EN:** I'll go instead of my younger sister.

## kekka — as a result of; after
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches after a clause + 結果 to describe an outcome following some process or effort.

**JP:** 一生懸命練習した結果、試合に勝ちました。
**Reading:** Isshoukenmei renshuu shita kekka, shiai ni kachimashita.
**EN:** As a result of practicing hard, I won the match.

## kekkyoku — after all; eventually; in the end
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb summarizing a final outcome, often one different from what was initially expected.

**JP:** 結局、旅行には行きませんでした。
**Reading:** Kekkyoku, ryokou ni wa ikimasen deshita.
**EN:** In the end, I didn't go on the trip.
