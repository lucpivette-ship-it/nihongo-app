# Grammar — N4 Batch 1 (20 points)

## aida — while; during; between
**JLPT:** N4

Marks the entire duration of a state or ongoing action, used with stative or continuous verbs (unlike あいだに, it does not mark a single point in time).

**JP:** 子供が寝ている間、本を読みました。
**Reading:** Kodomo ga nete iru aida, hon o yomimashita.
**EN:** I read a book while the child was sleeping.

## aida ni — while; during ~ something happened
**JLPT:** N4

Marks a shorter action or event that occurs at some point within a longer background period.

**JP:** 子供が寝ている間に、掃除をしました。
**Reading:** Kodomo ga nete iru aida ni, souji o shimashita.
**EN:** While the child was sleeping, I did the cleaning.

## amari~nai — not very, not much ~
**JLPT:** N4

Pairs あまり with a negative predicate to soften a statement, meaning "not very" or "not really."

**JP:** この映画はあまり面白くなかったです。
**Reading:** Kono eiga wa amari omoshirokunakatta desu.
**EN:** This movie wasn't very interesting.

## ato de — after~; later
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the plain past form of a verb, or a noun + の, to mean "after doing [A]."

**JP:** 仕事が終わった後で、飲みに行きましょう。
**Reading:** Shigoto ga owatta ato de, nomi ni ikimashou.
**EN:** Let's go for a drink after work is finished.

## ba — conditional form; If [A] then [B]
**JLPT:** N4

A conditional form made by changing a verb/adjective's final sound, expressing a general or hypothetical condition.

**JP:** 天気がよければ、公園へ行きます。
**Reading:** Tenki ga yokereba, kouen e ikimasu.
**EN:** If the weather is good, I'll go to the park.

## baai wa — in the event of; in the case that ~
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches after a verb (plain form) or noun + の to describe what happens in a hypothetical situation or case.

**JP:** 雨が降った場合は、試合は中止です。
**Reading:** Ame ga futta baai wa, shiai wa chuushi desu.
**EN:** In the event that it rains, the match will be cancelled.

## bakari — only; nothing but ~
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a noun, or the て-form of a verb, to emphasize that something is exclusively or repeatedly the case.

**JP:** 彼はゲームばかりしています。
**Reading:** Kare wa geemu bakari shite imasu.
**EN:** He does nothing but play games.

## dake de — just by; just by doing
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a noun or verb to indicate that a single action alone is enough to bring about a result.

**JP:** 見るだけで、味が想像できます。
**Reading:** Miru dake de, aji ga souzou dekimasu.
**EN:** Just by looking, I can imagine the taste.

## dasu — to begin to; to burst into; ... out
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb to mean the sudden start of an action, or physically moving something outward.

**JP:** 赤ちゃんが急に泣き出しました。
**Reading:** Akachan ga kyuu ni nakidashimashita.
**EN:** The baby suddenly burst into tears.

## de gozaimasu — to be (honorific)
**JLPT:** N4

The very formal, humble equivalent of です, commonly used by service staff.

**JP:** こちらがメニューでございます。
**Reading:** Kochira ga menyuu de gozaimasu.
**EN:** This is the menu (formal/polite).

## demo — ...or something; how about~
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a noun to suggest one example among several possible options, softening the suggestion.

**JP:** お茶でも飲みませんか。
**Reading:** Ocha demo nomimasen ka.
**EN:** How about some tea or something?

## dewa nai ka — right?; isn't it?
**JLPT:** N4

A rhetorical tag used to seek agreement or point out something the listener should already realize.

**JP:** もう遅いではないか。
**Reading:** Mou osoi dewa nai ka.
**EN:** It's already late, isn't it?

## ga hitsuyou — need; necessary
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a noun + が必要 to state that something is required.

**JP:** この仕事には経験が必要です。
**Reading:** Kono shigoto ni wa keiken ga hitsuyou desu.
**EN:** Experience is necessary for this job.

## ga suru — to smell; hear; taste
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches after a sensory noun (匂い, 音, 味, 気) + がする to describe a sensation one perceives.

**JP:** いいにおいがしますね。
**Reading:** Ii nioi ga shimasu ne.
**EN:** That smells nice, doesn't it?

## gari — personality (tends to; has a tendency to ~)
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the stem of an emotion/sensation adjective to describe someone's general personality trait.

**JP:** 妹は寒がりです。
**Reading:** Imouto wa samugari desu.
**EN:** My little sister is sensitive to the cold.

## garu / gatteiru — to show signs of; to appear; to feel ~
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the stem of an emotion adjective to describe a third person's observed feelings, since emotion adjectives normally only describe the speaker directly.

**JP:** 子供が犬を怖がっています。
**Reading:** Kodomo ga inu o kowagatte imasu.
**EN:** The child is showing fear of the dog.

## gozaimasu — to be, to exist (polite)
**JLPT:** N4

The very polite/humble form of いる／ある, common in service and formal contexts.

**JP:** お手洗いはあちらにございます。
**Reading:** Otearai wa achira ni gozaimasu.
**EN:** The restroom is over there (polite).

## hajimeru — to start; to begin to ~
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + 始める to describe the start of a continuing action.

**JP:** 雨が降り始めました。
**Reading:** Ame ga furihajimemashita.
**EN:** It started raining.

## hazu da — it must be; it should be (expectation)
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a plain-form clause to express a confident expectation based on reasoning, not direct evidence.

**JP:** 彼はもう着いているはずです。
**Reading:** Kare wa mou tsuite iru hazu desu.
**EN:** He should have already arrived.

## hazu ga nai — cannot be (impossible)
**JLPT:** N4

The negative of はずだ, used to strongly deny that something is likely or possible.

**JP:** そんな話が本当のはずがない。
**Reading:** Sonna hanashi ga hontou no hazu ga nai.
**EN:** There's no way such a story could be true.
