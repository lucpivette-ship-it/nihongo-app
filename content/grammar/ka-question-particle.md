# か — question particle

**JLPT level:** N5

Placed at the end of a sentence to turn a statement into a question. Also used inside a sentence to mean "or" between choices (see か～か).

## Example
- **JP:** これは本ですか。
- **Reading:** kore wa hon desu ka.
- **EN:** Is this a book?
