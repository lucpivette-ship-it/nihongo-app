# だけ — only / just / as much as

**JLPT level:** N5

Attaches to nouns, verbs, or adjectives to limit something to a single item, amount, or action.

## Example
- **JP:** 少しだけ食べました。
- **Reading:** sukoshi dake tabemashita.
- **EN:** I ate just a little.
