# Grammar — N1 Batch 7 (20 points)

## ni sakigakete — prior to; to be the first (pioneer); being ahead of
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + に先駆けて to describe doing something before anyone else, or ahead of a scheduled event.

**JP:** 発売に先駆けて、記者発表が行われた。
**Reading:** Hatsubai ni sakigakete, kisha happyou ga okonawareta.
**EN:** Ahead of the release, a press announcement was made.

## ni shinobinai — cannot bring oneself (to do); unable to bear
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + に忍びない to describe being emotionally unable to carry out an unpleasant action.

**JP:** これ以上苦しむ姿を見るに忍びない。
**Reading:** Kore ijou kurushimu sugata o miru ni shinobinai.
**EN:** I can't bear to watch him suffer any longer.

## ni shita tokoro de / to shita tokoro de — even if; even supposing that
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a plain-form clause + にしたところで to concede a hypothetical premise while denying it changes the outcome.

**JP:** 今から急いだところで、間に合わない。
**Reading:** Ima kara isoida tokoro de, ma ni awanai.
**EN:** Even if we hurry now, we won't make it in time.

## ni shite — at / on / under certain conditions (time, position, age, number of times)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun (often age, position, or number) + にして to emphasize the exceptional nature of achieving something at that specific point.

**JP:** 五十歳にして、初めて海外に行った。
**Reading:** Gojussai ni shite, hajimete kaigai ni itta.
**EN:** It was only at age fifty that I went abroad for the first time.

## ni sokushite — according to; in keeping with; to be based on
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + に即して to describe an action taken in accordance with actual facts or a specific standard.

**JP:** 現実に即して計画を立てる。
**Reading:** Genjitsu ni sokushite keikaku o tateru.
**EN:** We make plans in keeping with reality.

## ni taeru / ni taenai — worth doing; can do; cannot bear doing
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + に耐える to state something is worth enduring, or with a negative, unbearable/inappropriate.

**JP:** これは鑑賞に耐える作品だ。
**Reading:** Kore wa kanshou ni taeru sakuhin da.
**EN:** This is a work worthy of appreciation.

## ni taranai / ni tarinai — cannot; not worthy; not worth doing
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun/clause + に足らない to dismiss something as not significant enough to merit attention.

**JP:** それは恐れるに足らない。
**Reading:** Sore wa osoreru ni taranai.
**EN:** That's not something worth fearing.

## ni taru / ni tariru — can do; worthy; worth doing
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun/clause + に足る to state that something meets the standard of being worthy or sufficient.

**JP:** 彼は信頼するに足る人物だ。
**Reading:** Kare wa shinrai suru ni taru jinbutsu da.
**EN:** He's a person worthy of trust.

## ni terashite — according to; in view of; in light of
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + に照らして to describe evaluating something against an established standard or precedent.

**JP:** 法律に照らして判断する。
**Reading:** Houritsu ni terashite handan suru.
**EN:** We make the judgment in light of the law.

## ni todomarazu — not limited to; not only… but also
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + にとどまらず to state that an effect or scope extends beyond the stated boundary.

**JP:** 影響は国内にとどまらず、世界中に広がった。
**Reading:** Eikyou wa kokunai ni todomarazu, sekaijuu ni hirogatta.
**EN:** The impact wasn't limited to the country — it spread worldwide.

## ni wa muri ga aru — difficult to do; is unreasonable / unrealistic / impossible
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a clause + には無理がある to point out that a proposed plan or explanation is fundamentally unreasonable.

**JP:** その説明には無理がある。
**Reading:** Sono setsumei ni wa muri ga aru.
**EN:** That explanation doesn't really hold up.

## ni yorazu — regardless of
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + によらず to state that a category or standard doesn't determine or restrict the outcome.

**JP:** 年齢によらず、誰でも参加できます。
**Reading:** Nenrei ni yorazu, dare demo sanka dekimasu.
**EN:** Regardless of age, anyone can participate.

## ni~o kasanete — success after continuous (effort, work, adjustments, etc.)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + を重ねて to describe an outcome achieved only after repeated effort accumulated over time.

**JP:** 実験に実験を重ねて、ついに成功した。
**Reading:** Jikken ni jikken o kasanete, tsui ni seikou shita.
**EN:** After experiment upon experiment, we finally succeeded.

## nimo hodo ga aru — there is a limit to what is acceptable; to go too far
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun/na-adjective + にもほどがある to criticize something as having gone well beyond a reasonable limit.

**JP:** 冗談にもほどがある。
**Reading:** Joudan ni mo hodo ga aru.
**EN:** That joke has gone too far.

## nimo mashite — more than…; above
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + にも増して to state that something now exceeds even a previously high standard.

**JP:** 今年は去年にも増して暑い。
**Reading:** Kotoshi wa kyonen ni mo mashite atsui.
**EN:** This year is even hotter than last year.

## niwa ataranai — it's not worth; there's no need to; does not correspond to
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + には当たらない to dismiss an extreme reaction as unwarranted for the situation.

**JP:** それほど驚くには当たらない。
**Reading:** Sore hodo odoroku ni wa ataranai.
**EN:** It's not something worth being that surprised about.

## niwa oyobanai — there is no need to; unnecessary; no match for
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + には及ばない to state that a stronger response isn't necessary.

**JP:** わざわざ来るには及びません。
**Reading:** Wazawaza kuru ni wa oyobimasen.
**EN:** There's no need to go out of your way to come.

## no itari — utmost; extremely
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + の至り to express that an emotion has reached its absolute peak, formal register.

**JP:** 光栄の至りです。
**Reading:** Kouei no itari desu.
**EN:** It's an utmost honor.

## no kiwami — utmost; extremely
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + の極み to express that something has reached an extreme degree, formal/literary register.

**JP:** 贅沢の極みだ。
**Reading:** Zeitaku no kiwami da.
**EN:** It's the height of luxury.

## no nan notte — extremely (cannot be expressed in words)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to an adjective/verb + のなんのって to emphasize an experience so extreme that words fail to capture it, colloquial register.

**JP:** 痛いのなんのって、涙が出た。
**Reading:** Itai no nan notte, namida ga deta.
**EN:** It hurt so much I couldn't even describe it — I ended up in tears.
