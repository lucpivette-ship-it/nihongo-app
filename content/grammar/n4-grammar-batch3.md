# Grammar — N4 Batch 3 (20 points)

## made ni — by; by the time; time limit
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a noun or verb (dictionary form) to mark a deadline — the action must be complete at or before that point.

**JP:** 明日までにレポートを出してください。
**Reading:** Ashita made ni repooto o dashite kudasai.
**EN:** Please submit the report by tomorrow.

## mama — as it is; current state; without changing
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a past-tense verb or noun + の to describe something left unchanged from its current state.

**JP:** 靴を履いたまま部屋に入らないでください。
**Reading:** Kutsu o haita mama heya ni hairanaide kudasai.
**EN:** Please don't enter the room with your shoes still on.

## matawa — or; both; otherwise
**JLPT:** N4

A formal conjunction connecting two alternatives, similar to または in written or official contexts.

**JP:** ペンまたは鉛筆を使ってください。
**Reading:** Pen matawa enpitsu o tsukatte kudasai.
**EN:** Please use a pen or a pencil.

## mitai da — like; similar to; resembling
**JLPT:** N4

A casual equivalent of ようだ, attaches to a plain-form clause or noun to draw a comparison or guess.

**JP:** 外は寒いみたいです。
**Reading:** Soto wa samui mitai desu.
**EN:** It seems cold outside.

## mitai na — like, similar to (noun-modifying)
**JLPT:** N4

The noun-modifying form of みたいだ, placed directly before a noun.

**JP:** 彼はモデルみたいな人です。
**Reading:** Kare wa moderu mitai na hito desu.
**EN:** He's a person like a model.

## mitai ni — like; similar to (adverbial)
**JLPT:** N4

The adverbial form of みたいだ, used to modify a verb or describe the manner of an action.

**JP:** 鳥みたいに飛びたいです。
**Reading:** Tori mitai ni tobitai desu.
**EN:** I want to fly like a bird.

## mo — as many as; as much as; up to; nearly
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches after a number or quantity to emphasize that the amount is surprisingly large.

**JP:** 一時間も待ちました。
**Reading:** Ichijikan mo machimashita.
**EN:** I waited for as long as an hour.

## na — don't ~ (negative command)
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches directly to the dictionary form of a verb to form a blunt, forceful command not to do something.

**JP:** ここに入るな。
**Reading:** Koko ni hairu na.
**EN:** Don't enter here.

## nado — such as; things like ~
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches after a noun or list of nouns to give examples without being exhaustive.

**JP:** りんごやみかんなどが好きです。
**Reading:** Ringo ya mikan nado ga suki desu.
**EN:** I like things like apples and mandarins.

## nagara — while; during; simultaneously
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb to describe two actions performed by the same person at the same time.

**JP:** 音楽を聞きながら勉強します。
**Reading:** Ongaku o kikinagara benkyou shimasu.
**EN:** I study while listening to music.

## nakanaka~nai — not easy to; struggling to; not able to
**JLPT:** N4

Pairs なかなか with a negative predicate to say something isn't happening as readily or easily as expected.

**JP:** 日本語がなかなか上手にならない。
**Reading:** Nihongo ga nakanaka jouzu ni naranai.
**EN:** My Japanese isn't improving as easily as I'd like.

## nakereba ikenai — must do something; have to
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb to express obligation, slightly softer in tone than なければならない.

**JP:** 薬を飲まなければいけません。
**Reading:** Kusuri o nomanakereba ikemasen.
**EN:** I have to take the medicine.

## nara — if; in the case that
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a plain-form clause or noun to set a hypothetical condition, often about a topic just mentioned by the listener.

**JP:** 東京に行くなら、新幹線が便利です。
**Reading:** Toukyou ni iku nara, shinkansen ga benri desu.
**EN:** If you're going to Tokyo, the shinkansen is convenient.

## nasai — do this (firm command)
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb to give a firm but not harsh command, often used by parents/teachers.

**JP:** 早く寝なさい。
**Reading:** Hayaku nesai.
**EN:** Go to sleep early.

## nasaru — to do (honorific)
**JLPT:** N4

The honorific equivalent of する, used to speak respectfully about someone else's actions.

**JP:** 社長は何をなさいますか。
**Reading:** Shachou wa nani o nasaimasu ka.
**EN:** What will the president do? (honorific)

## ni ki ga tsuku — to notice; to realize
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a noun + に気がつく to describe suddenly becoming aware of something.

**JP:** 間違いに気がつきました。
**Reading:** Machigai ni ki ga tsukimashita.
**EN:** I noticed the mistake.

## ni mieru — to look; to seem; to appear
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a な-adjective/noun (+ に) or plain form to describe a visual impression.

**JP:** 彼女は元気に見えます。
**Reading:** Kanojo wa genki ni miemasu.
**EN:** She looks well.

## ni suru — to make something ~
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a な-adjective/noun + にする to describe deliberately causing a change of state.

**JP:** 部屋をきれいにしました。
**Reading:** Heya o kirei ni shimashita.
**EN:** I made the room clean.

## nikui — difficult to do
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb to describe something inherently hard or awkward to do.

**JP:** この靴は歩きにくいです。
**Reading:** Kono kutsu wa arukinikui desu.
**EN:** These shoes are hard to walk in.

## no naka de — in; among
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches after a noun (often a group or category) + の中で to single out one item from within it.

**JP:** クラスの中で彼が一番背が高い。
**Reading:** Kurasu no naka de kare ga ichiban se ga takai.
**EN:** Among the class, he is the tallest.

## noni [1] — although; in spite of; even though
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a plain-form clause to connect two contrasting statements, often with a nuance of surprise or complaint.

**JP:** 頑張ったのに、失敗しました。
**Reading:** Ganbatta noni, shippai shimashita.
**EN:** Even though I tried hard, I failed.
