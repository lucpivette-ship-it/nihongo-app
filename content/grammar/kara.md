# から — because / since / from

**JLPT level:** N5

Attaches to a clause to give a reason ("because"), or to a noun to mark a starting point in time or space ("from"). More direct/casual than ので.

## Example
- **JP:** 疲れたから、早く寝ます。
- **Reading:** tsukareta kara, hayaku nemasu.
- **EN:** Because I'm tired, I'll go to bed early.
