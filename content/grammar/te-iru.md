# ている — ongoing action / current state

**JLPT level:** N5

Attaches to the て-form of a verb to show an action in progress, an ongoing state, or a habitual action.

## Example
- **JP:** 今、テレビを見ています。
- **Reading:** ima, terebi o mite imasu.
- **EN:** I'm watching TV right now.
