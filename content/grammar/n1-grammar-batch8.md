# Grammar — N1 Batch 8 (20 points)

## no yara / mono yara / koto yara — I wonder...; unsure; I don't know
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a plain-form clause + のやら to express vague uncertainty about a fact or outcome.

**JP:** 彼が来るのやら、来ないのやら分からない。
**Reading:** Kare ga kuru no yara, konai no yara wakaranai.
**EN:** I have no idea whether he's coming or not.

## no yara~no yara — or ~ (I don't know)
**JLPT:** N1

Repeats のやら after two contrasting possibilities to express uncertainty about which is true.

**JP:** 嬉しいのやら悲しいのやら、複雑な気持ちだ。
**Reading:** Ureshii no yara kanashii no yara, fukuzatsu na kimochi da.
**EN:** I don't know if I'm happy or sad — it's a complicated feeling.

## o fumaete — to be based on; to take into account
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + を踏まえて to describe making a decision or statement grounded in prior facts or information.

**JP:** これまでの結果を踏まえて、方針を決めます。
**Reading:** Kore made no kekka o fumaete, houshin o kimemasu.
**EN:** We'll decide the policy based on the results so far.

## o hete — through; by way of; after; via
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + を経て to describe passing through a stage, place, or period before reaching a result.

**JP:** 長い訓練を経て、プロになった。
**Reading:** Nagai kunren o hete, puro ni natta.
**EN:** After long training, he became a professional.

## o hikaete — to be soon; the time has come to; nearby; ahead of
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + を控えて to describe an upcoming event that is drawing near.

**JP:** 試験を控えて、緊張している。
**Reading:** Shiken o hikaete, kinchou shite iru.
**EN:** With the exam coming up soon, I'm nervous.

## o ii koto ni — to take advantage of
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + をいいことに to describe exploiting a convenient circumstance, often with a critical nuance.

**JP:** 親切をいいことに、無理を言う。
**Reading:** Shinsetsu o ii koto ni, muri o iu.
**EN:** Taking advantage of his kindness, she makes unreasonable demands.

## o kaerimizu / mo kaerimizu — despite; without considering; not worrying about; regardless of
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + を顧みず to describe acting without regard for a normally important consideration, formal register.

**JP:** 危険を顧みず、救助に向かった。
**Reading:** Kiken o kaerimizu, kyuujo ni mukatta.
**EN:** Disregarding the danger, he went to help.

## o kagiri ni — starting from; the last time (ending a continued action)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun (usually a time point) + を限りに to mark a definitive end point for a continuing situation.

**JP:** 今日を限りに禁煙します。
**Reading:** Kyou o kagiri ni kin'en shimasu.
**EN:** Starting today, I'm quitting smoking for good.

## o kanete — also for the purpose of
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + を兼ねて to describe an action that serves two purposes at once.

**JP:** 散歩を兼ねて、買い物に行った。
**Reading:** Sanpo o kanete, kaimono ni itta.
**EN:** I went shopping, combining it with a walk.

## o kawakiri ni — one after another; starting with
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + を皮切りに to mark the starting point of a series of continuing events.

**JP:** 東京公演を皮切りに、全国ツアーが始まる。
**Reading:** Tokyo kouen o kawakiri ni, zenkoku tsuaa ga hajimaru.
**EN:** Starting with the Tokyo performance, the national tour begins.

## o ki ni — as an opportunity/chance to
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + を機に to describe an event that becomes the turning point for a subsequent change.

**JP:** 卒業を機に、一人暮らしを始めた。
**Reading:** Sotsugyou o ki ni, hitorigurashi o hajimeta.
**EN:** Taking graduation as an opportunity, I started living alone.

## o kinji enai — can't help but; can't refrain from
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + を禁じ得ない to describe an emotional reaction that cannot be suppressed.

**JP:** その光景に涙を禁じ得なかった。
**Reading:** Sono koukei ni namida o kinji enakatta.
**EN:** I couldn't hold back tears at that sight.

## o mono tomo sezuni — in defiance of; not losing to / afraid / worrying about
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + をものともせずに to describe overcoming an obstacle without being deterred by it.

**JP:** 悪天候をものともせずに、登山を続けた。
**Reading:** Akutenkou o mono tomo sezu ni, tozan o tsuzuketa.
**EN:** Undeterred by the bad weather, they continued the climb.

## o motte / o mochimashite — by means of; with; on / at / as of (time)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + をもって to state a formal means, method, or effective date.

**JP:** 本日をもって閉店いたします。
**Reading:** Honjitsu o motte heiten itashimasu.
**EN:** As of today, we will be closing the store.

## o oite~nai — only; can only be; there is no alternative, only
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + をおいて with a negative predicate to state that no other option exists besides the one named.

**JP:** 彼をおいて適任者はいない。
**Reading:** Kare o oite tekininsha wa inai.
**EN:** There's no one suitable for this other than him.

## o oshite / o oshikitte — to overcome (opposition); to push past
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + を押して to describe proceeding despite significant opposition or difficulty.

**JP:** 反対を押して、計画を進めた。
**Reading:** Hantai o oshite, keikaku o susumeta.
**EN:** He pushed the plan forward despite the opposition.

## o sakai ni — since ~ (indicates a large change before & after)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun (usually a time point) + を境に to mark a clear turning point after which things noticeably changed.

**JP:** 引っ越しを境に、生活が一変した。
**Reading:** Hikkoshi o sakai ni, seikatsu ga ippen shita.
**EN:** Since the move, my life has completely changed.

## o yogi naku sareru — to be forced to do something because one has no other choice
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + を余儀なくされる to state that circumstances left no choice but to accept an undesirable outcome.

**JP:** 台風のため、試合は中止を余儀なくされた。
**Reading:** Taifuu no tame, shiai wa chuushi o yoginaku sareta.
**EN:** Due to the typhoon, the match was forced to be cancelled.

## o yoso ni — despite; without regards to
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + をよそに to describe ignoring or disregarding a relevant concern.

**JP:** 周囲の心配をよそに、彼は平然としていた。
**Reading:** Shuui no shinpai o yoso ni, kare wa heizen to shite ita.
**EN:** Disregarding everyone's worry, he remained perfectly calm.

## o zentei toshite — with the intention to; on the condition / assumption
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + を前提として to establish a base condition or premise for what follows.

**JP:** 全員参加を前提として計画を立てる。
**Reading:** Zen'in sanka o zentei toshite keikaku o tateru.
**EN:** We'll make the plan on the assumption that everyone will attend.
