# Grammar — N1 Batch 11 (20 points)

## to ii~to ii — both ... and; not only ... also; whether it be ... or
**JLPT:** N1

Repeats といい after two aspects of the same subject to praise or describe it from multiple angles.

**JP:** 味といい、値段といい、大満足だ。
**Reading:** Aji to ii, nedan to ii, daimanzoku da.
**EN:** Both the taste and the price are completely satisfying.

## to ittara nai — extremely; very; nothing more ... than this; as ... as it could possibly be
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to an adjective + といったらない to emphasize an extreme degree beyond words, colloquial register.

**JP:** あの時の恥ずかしさといったらなかった。
**Reading:** Ano toki no hazukashisa to ittara nakatta.
**EN:** The embarrassment I felt at that moment was beyond words.

## to iu — all; every single ~ (no exceptions)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a repeated noun + という to emphasize totality, that every single instance within a category applies.

**JP:** 会う人という人が皆優しかった。
**Reading:** Au hito to iu hito ga mina yasashikatta.
**EN:** Every single person I met was kind.

## to iu ka~to iu ka — or rather; or perhaps I should say; I mean
**JLPT:** N1

Repeats というか between two descriptive words to hedge while searching for the most accurate expression.

**JP:** 勇敢というか、無謀というか、彼らしい行動だ。
**Reading:** Yuukan to iu ka, mubou to iu ka, kare rashii koudou da.
**EN:** Brave, or maybe reckless — either way, it's just like him.

## to iu mono — during; for; since; over a period of time
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a duration + というもの to emphasize that an entire stretch of time has passed in a certain continuous state.

**JP:** ここ一年というもの、休みなく働いた。
**Reading:** Koko ichinen to iu mono, yasumi naku hataraita.
**EN:** For this whole past year, I've worked without a single break.

## to iu tokoro da / to itta tokoro da — at the most; no more than; at best; roughly; about
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to an estimate + というところだ to state that a rough figure represents the realistic upper limit.

**JP:** せいぜい五分というところだ。
**Reading:** Seizei gofun to iu tokoro da.
**EN:** Five minutes at the most, I'd say.

## to iu wake da — that's why; no wonder; this means; it is the case that
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a clause + というわけだ to draw a natural, confirming conclusion from preceding information.

**JP:** つまり、彼は嘘をついていたというわけだ。
**Reading:** Tsumari, kare wa uso o tsuite ita to iu wake da.
**EN:** In other words, that means he was lying.

## to iu wake dewa nai — it's not that; it doesn't mean that
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a clause + というわけではない to soften or deny a conclusion that might otherwise be assumed.

**JP:** 嫌いというわけではないが、あまり食べない。
**Reading:** Kirai to iu wake dewa nai ga, amari tabenai.
**EN:** It's not that I dislike it, but I don't eat it much.

## to iwazu~to iwazu — both; not only A or B, but (overall)
**JLPT:** N1

Repeats といわず after two examples to state that the described condition applies to the whole, not just those parts.

**JP:** 手といわず足といわず、泥だらけだ。
**Reading:** Te to iwazu ashi to iwazu, doro darake da.
**EN:** Both his hands and feet — really, his whole body — was covered in mud.

## to kiteiru — because of
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun/clause + ときている to describe an inherent, often frustrating, characteristic contributing to a situation.

**JP:** 頑固ときているから、意見を変えない。
**Reading:** Ganko to kite iru kara, iken o kaenai.
**EN:** Since he's so stubborn, he won't change his opinion.

## to miru to — as soon as one realizes ..., then
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a clause + とみると to describe an immediate reaction following a perceived realization.

**JP:** 危ないとみると、すぐに助けに入った。
**Reading:** Abunai to miru to, sugu ni tasuke ni haitta.
**EN:** As soon as he saw it was dangerous, he immediately stepped in to help.

## to miru ya — at the sight of; upon seeing; after confirming
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a clause + と見るや to describe an instantaneous reaction the moment something is observed, literary register.

**JP:** 相手が引くと見るや、一気に攻めた。
**Reading:** Aite ga hiku to miru ya, ikki ni semeta.
**EN:** The moment he saw the opponent retreat, he attacked all at once.

## to naru to / to nareba — when it comes to; in such a case; if that happens
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun/clause + となると to shift focus to the significant implications of a hypothetical case actually happening.

**JP:** いざ本番となると、緊張してしまう。
**Reading:** Iza honban to naru to, kinchou shite shimau.
**EN:** When it actually comes down to the real thing, I get nervous.

## to sareru — is considered to; it is said that
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a plain-form clause + とされる to state a widely-held view or classification in formal, objective register.

**JP:** この薬は効果があるとされている。
**Reading:** Kono kusuri wa kouka ga aru to sarete iru.
**EN:** This medicine is said to be effective.

## tokitara — when it comes to; concerning
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + ときたら to introduce a topic with an exasperated or critical tone, colloquial register.

**JP:** うちの猫ときたら、いつも寝てばかりだ。
**Reading:** Uchi no neko to kitara, itsumo nete bakari da.
**EN:** As for my cat, honestly, all he does is sleep.

## tokoro o — although (it is a certain time/condition); even though normally
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun/clause + ところを to acknowledge an inconvenient circumstance while expressing gratitude or apology.

**JP:** お忙しいところをお越しいただき、ありがとうございます。
**Reading:** Oisogashii tokoro o okoshi itadaki, arigatou gozaimasu.
**EN:** Thank you for coming despite your busy schedule.

## tomo arou mono ga — of all people (expression showing surprise at a high standing person's misbehavior)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a title/position + ともあろうものが to express shock that someone of high standing acted inappropriately.

**JP:** 大臣ともあろう者が、そんなミスをするとは。
**Reading:** Daijin tomo arou mono ga, sonna misu o suru to wa.
**EN:** A minister of all people, making such a mistake?

## tomo naku / tomo nashi ni — somehow; without knowing; without thinking; unconsciously
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + ともなく to describe an action performed vaguely, without clear intention.

**JP:** どこからともなく、いい匂いがしてきた。
**Reading:** Doko kara tomo naku, ii nioi ga shite kita.
**EN:** A nice smell drifted in from somewhere or other.

## tomo sureba — apt to (do); tend to; liable to; prone to
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a clause + ともすれば to describe a tendency toward a particular, often undesirable, outcome.

**JP:** ともすれば、諦めてしまいがちだ。
**Reading:** Tomo sureba, akiramete shimaigachi da.
**EN:** I'm prone to giving up if I'm not careful.

## tomo~tomo — unable to draw a conclusion; unable to judge
**JLPT:** N1

Repeats とも after two contrasting possibilities to state that one cannot decide between them.

**JP:** 賛成とも反対とも言えない。
**Reading:** Sansei tomo hantai tomo ienai.
**EN:** I can't say whether I'm for it or against it.
