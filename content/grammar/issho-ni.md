# いっしょに — together

**JLPT level:** N5

Adverb meaning "together," often paired with a companion marked by と.

## Example
- **JP:** 友達と一緒に映画を見ました。
- **Reading:** tomodachi to issho ni eiga o mimashita.
- **EN:** I watched a movie together with a friend.
