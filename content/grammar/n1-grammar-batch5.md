# Grammar — N1 Batch 5 (20 points)

## mono o — if only; I wish; I'm sorry it's not actually (regret)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a plain-form clause + ものを to express regret or reproach that things didn't turn out as they should have.

**JP:** 早く言ってくれれば良かったものを。
**Reading:** Hayaku itte kurereba yokatta mono o.
**EN:** If only you'd told me sooner (but you didn't).

## mono to omowareru / mono to mirareru — to think; to suppose; it is believed/expected that
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a plain-form clause + ものと思われる to state a reasoned, somewhat formal conjecture, common in news/reports.

**JP:** 事故の原因は整備不良によるものと思われる。
**Reading:** Jiko no gen'in wa seibi furyou ni yoru mono to omowareru.
**EN:** The cause of the accident is believed to be poor maintenance.

## mono to suru — shall; to assume; understood as
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a plain-form clause + ものとする to formally stipulate a rule or assumption, common in contracts and regulations.

**JP:** 会費は毎月末に支払うものとする。
**Reading:** Kaihi wa maitsuki matsu ni shiharau mono to suru.
**EN:** Membership fees shall be paid at the end of each month.

## mono toshite — to assume; to suppose; on the presumption that
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a plain-form clause + ものとして to establish a premise or assumption for the following statement.

**JP:** 参加者は全員来るものとして準備を進めます。
**Reading:** Sankasha wa zen'in kuru mono toshite junbi o susumemasu.
**EN:** We'll proceed with preparations assuming all participants will attend.

## moshikuwa — or; otherwise
**JLPT:** N1

A formal conjunction connecting two alternative options, common in official documents.

**JP:** 電話もしくはメールでご連絡ください。
**Reading:** Denwa moshikuwa meeru de gorenraku kudasai.
**EN:** Please contact us by phone or email.

## n bakari ni — as if; as though
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb + んばかりに to describe an intense state or emotion that seems just short of manifesting outright.

**JP:** 泣かんばかりに頼んできた。
**Reading:** Nakan bakari ni tanonde kita.
**EN:** He begged me as if he were about to cry.

## n ga tame ni — in order to
**JLPT:** N1

A classical literary suffix attaching to the negative stem of a verb + んがために to state a strong, deliberate purpose.

**JP:** 生きんがために働く。
**Reading:** Ikin ga tame ni hataraku.
**EN:** I work in order to survive.

## nagara ni / nagara no — while; during; without change; performed in this condition
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + ながらに to describe a state maintained continuously, unchanged, during an action.

**JP:** 涙ながらに謝罪した。
**Reading:** Namida nagara ni shazai shita.
**EN:** He apologized in tears.

## nai made mo — not to say; although it does not reach the level of
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb/adjective + ないまでも to concede that a lesser standard was met, even if not the ideal one.

**JP:** 完璧とは言わないまでも、良い出来だ。
**Reading:** Kanpeki to wa iwanai made mo, yoi deki da.
**EN:** It's not perfect, but it's a good result.

## nai mono demo nai — is not entirely impossible
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb + ないものでもない to concede a small, hedge possibility, formal register.

**JP:** 考え直せば、賛成しないものでもない。
**Reading:** Kangaenaoseba, sansei shinai mono demo nai.
**EN:** If I reconsider, it's not impossible that I'd agree.

## nai mono ka / nai mono darou ka — isn't there; can't we…?; can't I…?
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb + ないものか to express a hopeful wish for a solution or possibility.

**JP:** 何とかならないものか。
**Reading:** Nantoka naranai mono ka.
**EN:** Isn't there some way to make this work?

## nai tomo kagiranai — not necessarily; I can't say that; maybe; might
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb + ないとも限らない to state that a possibility, however unlikely, cannot be ruled out.

**JP:** 明日雨が降らないとも限らない。
**Reading:** Ashita ame ga furanai tomo kagiranai.
**EN:** It's not impossible that it might rain tomorrow.

## nakushite wa — cannot do without
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + なくしては to state that something is an indispensable prerequisite for a following negative outcome.

**JP:** 努力なくしては成功はあり得ない。
**Reading:** Doryoku nakushite wa seikou wa arienai.
**EN:** Without effort, success is impossible.

## nami — average; ordinary; same level as; equal to; on par with
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + 並み to describe something as being at the same standard or level as the referenced thing.

**JP:** 彼はプロ並みの腕前だ。
**Reading:** Kare wa puro nami no udemae da.
**EN:** His skill is on par with a professional's.

## nan to iu / nanto / nante — how (beautiful, etc.); what a
**JLPT:** N1

An exclamatory adverb introducing surprise or strong emotion about a following quality.

**JP:** なんときれいな景色だろう。
**Reading:** Nanto kirei na keshiki darou.
**EN:** What a beautiful view!

## nani shiro — at any rate; after all; anyhow; anyway; in any case; because; as you know
**JLPT:** N1

A conjunction introducing a reason that the listener should already understand, to justify a following statement.

**JP:** 何しろ初めてのことなので、緊張します。
**Reading:** Nani shiro hajimete no koto na node, kinchou shimasu.
**EN:** After all, it's my first time, so I'm nervous.

## nara dewa — distinctive of; uniquely applying to; special to
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + ならでは to praise something as a quality unique to, and only possible because of, the stated subject.

**JP:** これは職人ならではの技術だ。
**Reading:** Kore wa shokunin naradewa no gijutsu da.
**EN:** This is a skill unique to a true craftsman.

## nara iza shirazu / wa iza shirazu — I don't know about ... but; it may be possible for ... but
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + ならいざしらず to set aside one case as possibly exceptional before contrasting it with the main point.

**JP:** 昔ならいざしらず、今はそんなことは許されない。
**Reading:** Mukashi nara iza shirazu, ima wa sonna koto wa yurusarenai.
**EN:** It might have been fine in the past, but that's not permitted now.

## nari — as soon as; right after
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + なり to describe a second action occurring the instant the first one happens.

**JP:** 家に帰るなり、寝てしまった。
**Reading:** Ie ni kaeru nari, nete shimatta.
**EN:** As soon as I got home, I fell asleep.

## nari ni / nari no — suitable; in one's own way/style
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + なりに to describe doing something in a manner fitting to one's own limited ability or circumstances.

**JP:** 自分なりに努力しました。
**Reading:** Jibun nari ni doryoku shimashita.
**EN:** I made an effort in my own way.
