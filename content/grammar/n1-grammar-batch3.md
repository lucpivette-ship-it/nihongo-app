# Grammar — N1 Batch 3 (20 points)

## ikani mo — indeed; really; truly; just (like); very; extremely
**JLPT:** N1

An adverb emphasizing that something is exactly, unmistakably, or extremely as described.

**JP:** いかにも彼らしいやり方だ。
**Reading:** Ikani mo kare rashii yarikata da.
**EN:** That's just so typical of him.

## izure ni shitemo / izure ni shiro / izure ni seyo — anyhow; anyway; either way; in any case
**JLPT:** N1

A conjunction conceding multiple possibilities while asserting the conclusion holds regardless of which is true.

**JP:** いずれにしても、明日には結果が出る。
**Reading:** Izure ni shitemo, ashita ni wa kekka ga deru.
**EN:** Either way, the result will be known tomorrow.

## jimita — to become; to appear like; to look like; tainted with
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun stem + じみた to describe something that has taken on an unwanted, often negative, quality.

**JP:** 冗談じみた話だ。
**Reading:** Joudan jimita hanashi da.
**EN:** It sounds like a joke (in a dismissive way).

## ka ina ka — whether or not
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a plain-form clause + か否か to formally pose a yes/no question within a larger sentence.

**JP:** 参加するか否か、早めに知らせてください。
**Reading:** Sanka suru ka ina ka, hayame ni shirasete kudasai.
**EN:** Please let me know soon whether or not you'll participate.

## ka to omoikiya — contrary to expectations; or so we thought, but
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a clause + かと思いきや to describe a surprising reversal of what was initially expected.

**JP:** 晴れるかと思いきや、急に雨が降り出した。
**Reading:** Hareru ka to omoikiya, kyuu ni ame ga furidashita.
**EN:** We thought it would clear up, but it suddenly started raining.

## kagiri da — to feel strongly
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to an adjective stem + 限りだ to express an intense personal feeling at its peak.

**JP:** 羨ましい限りだ。
**Reading:** Urayamashii kagiri da.
**EN:** I'm so envious (as much as one can be).

## kai mo naku — despite; even though
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun/clause + 甲斐もなく to describe an effort that yielded no result despite being made.

**JP:** 看病の甲斐もなく、彼は亡くなった。
**Reading:** Kanbyou no kai mo naku, kare wa nakunatta.
**EN:** Despite all the nursing care, he passed away.

## kanousei ga aru — may/might; there's a possibility that
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a plain-form clause + 可能性がある to state that an outcome is a genuine, if uncertain, possibility.

**JP:** 明日は雪が降る可能性がある。
**Reading:** Ashita wa yuki ga furu kanousei ga aru.
**EN:** There's a possibility it will snow tomorrow.

## kara aru / kara suru / kara no — at least; as much as; as many as
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a quantity + からある to emphasize that an amount is impressively large.

**JP:** 百キロからある荷物を運んだ。
**Reading:** Hyaku kiro kara aru nimotsu o hakonda.
**EN:** I carried a load weighing at least 100 kilos.

## kare~kare — sooner or later; more or less
**JLPT:** N1

Repeats かれ between two opposite adjective stems to state a middling or eventual outcome regardless of specifics.

**JP:** 遅かれ早かれ、真実は明らかになる。
**Reading:** Osokare hayakare, shinjitsu wa akiraka ni naru.
**EN:** Sooner or later, the truth will come out.

## katagata — while; at the same time; incidentally; for the purpose of
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + かたがた to describe doing something incidentally, for the additional stated purpose, formal register.

**JP:** お礼かたがたご挨拶に伺いました。
**Reading:** Orei katagata goaisatsu ni ukagaimashita.
**EN:** I came to pay my respects, and at the same time to thank you.

## katawara — while; besides; at the same time; in addition
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun/clause + かたわら to describe a secondary, ongoing activity carried out alongside a main one.

**JP:** 会社員のかたわら、小説を書いている。
**Reading:** Kaishain no katawara, shousetsu o kaite iru.
**EN:** Besides being a company employee, he also writes novels.

## katsute — once; before; formerly; ever; former; ex-
**JLPT:** N1

A formal literary adverb referring to a point or period in the past.

**JP:** かつてこの街は栄えていた。
**Reading:** Katsute kono machi wa sakaete ita.
**EN:** This town once used to prosper.

## kirai ga aru — bad habit; to have a tendency to
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a plain-form clause + 嫌いがある to point out a mildly negative recurring tendency.

**JP:** 彼は物事を大げさに言う嫌いがある。
**Reading:** Kare wa monogoto o oogesa ni iu kirai ga aru.
**EN:** He has a tendency to exaggerate things.

## kiri ga nai — endless; boundless; going on forever; there's no end to
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a clause + 切りがない to state that something, if continued, would never reach a natural stopping point.

**JP:** 文句を言い出したら切りがない。
**Reading:** Monku o iidashitara kiri ga nai.
**EN:** If I start complaining, there's no end to it.

## kitte no — the most / greatest … of all
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a place/group + きっての to describe someone as the top example within that entire scope.

**JP:** 彼は町きっての物知りだ。
**Reading:** Kare wa machi kitte no monoshiri da.
**EN:** He's the most knowledgeable person in the whole town.

## kiwamaru / kiwamarinai — extremely; very
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a na-adjective stem + 極まる/極まりない to intensify a description to its utmost extreme, formal/literary register.

**JP:** 失礼極まりない態度だ。
**Reading:** Shitsurei kiwamarinai taido da.
**EN:** It's an extremely rude attitude.

## koso are — although; even though
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun/clause + こそあれ to concede a point while asserting a stronger contrasting fact, formal/literary register.

**JP:** 困難はあったこそあれ、目標は達成した。
**Reading:** Konnan wa atta koso are, mokuhyou wa tassei shita.
**EN:** Although there were difficulties, the goal was achieved.

## koso sure — and; although; but
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + こそすれ to affirm one thing strongly while denying its opposite, formal register.

**JP:** 感謝こそすれ、恨む理由はない。
**Reading:** Kansha koso sure, uramu riyuu wa nai.
**EN:** I only feel gratitude — there's no reason to resent it.

## koso~ga/keredo — but; although (emphasis)
**JLPT:** N1

Emphasizes a conceded point using こそ before but; although (emphasis) contrasting result with が/けれど, formal/literary register.

**JP:** 若さこそあれ、経験は誰にも負けない。
**Reading:** Wakasa koso are, keiken wa dare ni mo makenai.
**EN:** Although I'm young, my experience is second to none.
