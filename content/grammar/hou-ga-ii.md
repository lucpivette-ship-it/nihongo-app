# ほうがいい — had better / should

**JLPT level:** N5

Gives advice or a recommendation: attaches to the plain past form for a positive suggestion, or the ない form for a negative one.

## Example
- **JP:** 早く寝たほうがいいです。
- **Reading:** hayaku neta hou ga ii desu.
- **EN:** You'd better go to bed early.
