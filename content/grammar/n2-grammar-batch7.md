# Grammar — N2 Batch 7 (20 points)

## nuki ni shite / nuki de — without; leaving out; cutting out
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + 抜きにして/抜きで to describe proceeding without a normally-expected element.

**JP:** 冗談抜きで、真剣に話しましょう。
**Reading:** Joudan nuki de, shinken ni hanashimashou.
**EN:** Joking aside, let's talk seriously.

## nuku — to do something from beginning to end; completely, extremely
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + 抜く to emphasize completing a difficult action all the way through, often under hardship.

**JP:** 苦しい練習に耐え抜きました。
**Reading:** Kurushii renshuu ni taenukimashita.
**EN:** I endured the grueling practice all the way through.

## o keiki ni — as a good opportunity/chance to; as a result of; make the best of
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + を契機に to describe an event that becomes the turning point for a subsequent change.

**JP:** 病気を契機に、生活習慣を見直しました。
**Reading:** Byouki o keiki ni, seikatsu shuukan o minaoshimashita.
**EN:** Taking my illness as an opportunity, I reviewed my lifestyle habits.

## o megutte — concerning; in regard to
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + をめぐって to describe a debate, conflict, or discussion revolving around a particular topic.

**JP:** その問題をめぐって、激しい議論が続いています。
**Reading:** Sono mondai o megutte, hageshii giron ga tsuzuite imasu.
**EN:** A heated debate continues concerning that issue.

## o moto ni — based on; derived from; building on; beginning with
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + をもとに to describe creating or deciding something using a given source as its foundation.

**JP:** アンケート結果をもとに、新しい商品を開発しました。
**Reading:** Ankeeto kekka o moto ni, atarashii shouhin o kaihatsu shimashita.
**EN:** We developed a new product based on the survey results.

## o nozoite — except; with the exception of; excluding
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + を除いて to exclude a specific item from an otherwise general statement.

**JP:** 日曜日を除いて、毎日営業しています。
**Reading:** Nichiyoubi o nozoite, mainichi eigyou shite imasu.
**EN:** We're open every day except Sunday.

## o towazu — regardless of; irrespective of; no matter
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + を問わず to state that a distinction or category doesn't affect the applicability of a statement.

**JP:** 経験の有無を問わず、応募できます。
**Reading:** Keiken no umu o towazu, oubo dekimasu.
**EN:** You can apply regardless of whether you have experience or not.

## o~negau — please do; could you please…; I ask of you to
**JLPT:** N2

Sandwiches a noun between お and 願う to make a very formal, polite request.

**JP:** ご確認をお願いいたします。
**Reading:** Gokakunin o onegai itashimasu.
**EN:** Please could you kindly confirm this.

## omake ni — to make matters worse; besides; what's more; in addition; on top of that
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb adding an additional, often negative, point on top of a preceding statement.

**JP:** 電車が遅れて、おまけに雨まで降ってきました。
**Reading:** Densha ga okurete, omake ni ame made futte kimashita.
**EN:** The train was late, and on top of that, it even started raining.

## osoraku — perhaps; likely; probably; I dare say
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb of speculation expressing a fairly confident guess about an outcome.

**JP:** 恐らく、彼は今日来ないでしょう。
**Reading:** Osoraku, kare wa kyou konai deshou.
**EN:** He probably won't come today.

## osore ga aru — it is feared that; to be in danger of; to be liable to
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + 恐れがある to describe the risk of a negative event occurring, often in formal warnings.

**JP:** 台風が上陸する恐れがあります。
**Reading:** Taifuu ga jourikusuru osore ga arimasu.
**EN:** There is a risk that the typhoon will make landfall.

## oyobi — and; as well as
**JLPT:** N2

A formal conjunction connecting nouns, commonly seen in official documents and lists.

**JP:** 氏名及び住所を記入してください。
**Reading:** Shimei oyobi juusho o kinyuu shite kudasai.
**EN:** Please fill in your name and address.

## roku ni~nai — not well; not enough; improperly; insufficiently; unsatisfactorily
**JLPT:** N2

Pairs ろくに with a negative predicate to criticize an action as insufficient or poorly done.

**JP:** 忙しくて、ろくに食事もできません。
**Reading:** Isogashikute, roku ni shokuji mo dekimasen.
**EN:** I'm so busy I can't even eat properly.

## saiwai na koto ni — fortunately; luckily; thankfully
**JLPT:** N2

An adverbial phrase prefacing a statement to express relief that things turned out well.

**JP:** 幸いなことに、誰も怪我をしませんでした。
**Reading:** Saiwai na koto ni, dare mo kega o shimasen deshita.
**EN:** Fortunately, no one was injured.

## sei ka — perhaps because
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + せいか to tentatively attribute a result to a possible, uncertain cause.

**JP:** 疲れているせいか、頭が痛いです。
**Reading:** Tsukarete iru sei ka, atama ga itai desu.
**EN:** Maybe it's because I'm tired, but my head hurts.

## sekkaku — especially; (thank you for) troubling to, going to great pains for
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb acknowledging a special effort was made, often introducing regret that it went unrewarded.

**JP:** せっかく来たのに、彼は留守でした。
**Reading:** Sekkaku kita noni, kare wa rusu deshita.
**EN:** Even though I made a special trip, he wasn't home.

## semete — at least; at most
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb expressing the minimum acceptable amount or degree in a given situation.

**JP:** せめて連絡ぐらいしてください。
**Reading:** Semete renraku gurai shite kudasai.
**EN:** Please at least give me a call.

## shidai — as soon as, dependent upon
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + 次第 to describe an action performed immediately once a condition is met.

**JP:** 到着次第、ご連絡いたします。
**Reading:** Touchaku shidai, gorenraku itashimasu.
**EN:** I'll contact you as soon as I arrive.

## shidai de — depending on; so
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + 次第で to state that an outcome varies based on a particular factor.

**JP:** 努力次第で、結果は変わります。
**Reading:** Doryoku shidai de, kekka wa kawarimasu.
**EN:** The result changes depending on your effort.

## shidai ni — gradually (progress into a state); in sequence; in order
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb describing a change that unfolds slowly and steadily over time.

**JP:** 空は次第に明るくなってきました。
**Reading:** Sora wa shidai ni akaruku natte kimashita.
**EN:** The sky gradually grew brighter.
