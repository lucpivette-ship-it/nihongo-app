# Grammar — N2 Batch 8 (20 points)

## shikamo — moreover; furthermore; and yet; what's more
**JLPT:** N2

A conjunction adding an additional, often intensifying, point to a preceding statement.

**JP:** このケーキは安い、しかもおいしいです。
**Reading:** Kono keeki wa yasui, shikamo oishii desu.
**EN:** This cake is cheap, and what's more, it's delicious.

## sono ue — besides; in addition; furthermore
**JLPT:** N2

A conjunction introducing an additional point, similar to しかも but slightly more neutral.

**JP:** 彼は頭がいい。その上、努力家です。
**Reading:** Kare wa atama ga ii. Sono ue, doryokuka desu.
**EN:** He's smart. On top of that, he's hardworking.

## sore na noni — and yet; despite this; but even so
**JLPT:** N2

A conjunction expressing frustration or surprise that a result contradicts what was just stated.

**JP:** 一生懸命勉強しました。それなのに、試験に落ちました。
**Reading:** Isshoukenmei benkyou shimashita. Sore na noni, shiken ni ochimashita.
**EN:** I studied really hard. And yet, I failed the exam.

## sore nara — if that's the case; if so
**JLPT:** N2

A conjunction proposing a conclusion or suggestion based on a preceding statement.

**JP:** それなら、明日もう一度会いましょう。
**Reading:** Sore nara, ashita mou ichido aimashou.
**EN:** If that's the case, let's meet again tomorrow.

## sore ni shitemo — nevertheless; at any rate; even so; be that as it may
**JLPT:** N2

A conjunction conceding a preceding point while still adding a strong personal reaction.

**JP:** それにしても、今日は寒すぎます。
**Reading:** Sore ni shitemo, kyou wa samusugimasu.
**EN:** Even so, it's way too cold today.

## sou ieba — come to think of it; now that you mention it
**JLPT:** N2

A conjunction used to introduce a related thought that suddenly comes to mind.

**JP:** そう言えば、彼女最近見かけませんね。
**Reading:** Sou ieba, kanojo saikin mikakemasen ne.
**EN:** Come to think of it, I haven't seen her lately.

## sou suru to — having done that; if that is done; if it is done in that way
**JLPT:** N2

A conjunction introducing a natural consequence following a previously described action.

**JP:** ボタンを押しました。そうすると、ドアが開きました。
**Reading:** Botan o oshimashita. Sou suru to, doa ga hirakimashita.
**EN:** I pressed the button. Then, the door opened.

## sue ni — finally; after; following; at the end/ conclusion of
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + 末に to describe an outcome reached after a long, often difficult, process.

**JP:** 長い議論の末に、結論が出ました。
**Reading:** Nagai giron no sue ni, ketsuron ga demashita.
**EN:** After a long discussion, a conclusion was reached.

## sukoshi mo~nai — not one bit; not even a little
**JLPT:** N2

Pairs 少しも with a negative predicate for strong, complete denial.

**JP:** 少しも後悔していません。
**Reading:** Sukoshi mo koukai shite imasen.
**EN:** I don't regret it one bit.

## sukunaku tomo — at least
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb setting a minimum bound on a quantity or degree.

**JP:** 少なくとも三日は必要です。
**Reading:** Sukunaku tomo mikka wa hitsuyou desu.
**EN:** We need at least three days.

## tadachi ni — at once; immediately; directly; in person; automatically
**JLPT:** N2

A formal adverb describing an action taken without any delay whatsoever.

**JP:** 事故が起きたら、直ちに警察に連絡してください。
**Reading:** Jiko ga okitara, tadachi ni keisatsu ni renraku shite kudasai.
**EN:** If an accident occurs, please contact the police immediately.

## tamae — do~; order somebody to do something
**JLPT:** N2

An old-fashioned, masculine imperative suffix attaching to the ます-stem of a verb, used by a superior to a subordinate.

**JP:** 一緒に来たまえ。
**Reading:** Issho ni kitamae.
**EN:** Come with me. (commanding, dated tone)

## te bakari wa irarenai — can't keep doing
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + ばかりはいられない to say a continuous action can no longer be justified given the circumstances.

**JP:** いつまでも泣いてばかりはいられません。
**Reading:** Itsu made mo naite bakari wa iraremasen.
**EN:** I can't keep crying forever.

## te demo — even if I have to; by all means
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + でも to express strong determination to do something regardless of the cost.

**JP:** 借金してでも、この店を続けます。
**Reading:** Shakkin shite demo, kono mise o tsuzukemasu.
**EN:** Even if I have to go into debt, I'll keep this shop running.

## te irai — since; henceforth
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + 以来 to mark a point in the past after which a state has continued.

**JP:** 日本に来て以来、ずっと忙しいです。
**Reading:** Nihon ni kite irai, zutto isogashii desu.
**EN:** I've been busy ever since I came to Japan.

## te ite wa — if one keeps doing
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + いては to describe a negative consequence that follows from continuing a habitual action.

**JP:** そんなに怠けていては、成功できません。
**Reading:** Sonna ni namakete ite wa, seikou dekimasen.
**EN:** If you keep slacking off like that, you won't succeed.

## te koso — now that; since (something happened)
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + こそ to emphasize that only after a certain event does something become true or possible.

**JP:** 失敗してこそ、成長できるのです。
**Reading:** Shippai shite koso, seichou dekiru no desu.
**EN:** It's precisely by failing that you can grow.

## te made — even; will go far so as to
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + まで to emphasize an extreme lengths taken to accomplish something.

**JP:** 学校を休んでまで、旅行に行きました。
**Reading:** Gakkou o yasunde made, ryokou ni ikimashita.
**EN:** I went as far as skipping school to go on the trip.

## te naranai — can't help but; dying to; extremely
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the て-form of an adjective/verb + ならない to express an uncontrollable feeling.

**JP:** 彼のことが心配でなりません。
**Reading:** Kare no koto ga shinpai de narimasen.
**EN:** I can't help but worry about him.

## te tamaranai — can't help but; dying to; extremely
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the て-form of an adjective/verb + たまらない to express an intense, hard-to-suppress feeling.

**JP:** のどが渇いてたまりません。
**Reading:** Nodo ga kawaite tamarimasen.
**EN:** I'm dying of thirst.
