# たり〜たり — do such things as A and B

**JLPT level:** N5

Lists example actions out of a larger, unstated set, using the た-form of each verb + り; often paired with する.

## Example
- **JP:** 週末は本を読んだり、映画を見たりします。
- **Reading:** shuumatsu wa hon o yondari, eiga o mitari shimasu.
- **EN:** On weekends, I do things like read books and watch movies.
