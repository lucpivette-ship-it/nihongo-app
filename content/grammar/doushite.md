# どうして — why / for what reason

**JLPT level:** N5

Asks the reason behind something; usable interchangeably with なぜ in most casual contexts.

## Example
- **JP:** どうして遅れたんですか。
- **Reading:** doushite okuretan desu ka.
- **EN:** Why were you late?
