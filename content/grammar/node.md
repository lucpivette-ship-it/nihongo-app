# ので — because / since

**JLPT level:** N5

Attaches to a clause to give a reason. Sounds softer and more polite than から, and is common in explanations.

## Example
- **JP:** 雨なので、行きません。
- **Reading:** ame na node, ikimasen.
- **EN:** Since it's raining, I won't go.
