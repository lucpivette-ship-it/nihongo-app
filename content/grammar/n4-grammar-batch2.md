# Grammar — N4 Batch 2 (20 points)

## hitsuyou ga aru — need to; it is necessary to
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + 必要がある to state that doing something is required.

**JP:** もっと勉強する必要があります。
**Reading:** Motto benkyou suru hitsuyou ga arimasu.
**EN:** It's necessary to study more.

## ikou kei — volitional form; let's do ~
**JLPT:** N4

The volitional conjugation of a verb (e.g. 行こう from 行く), used to suggest doing something or express one's own intention.

**JP:** そろそろ出かけようか。
**Reading:** Sorosoro dekakeyou ka.
**EN:** Shall we head out soon?

## irassharu — to be; to come; to go (polite)
**JLPT:** N4

An honorific verb covering いる／来る／行く, used to speak respectfully about someone else's presence or movement.

**JP:** 先生は教室にいらっしゃいます。
**Reading:** Sensei wa kyoushitsu ni irasshaimasu.
**EN:** The teacher is in the classroom (polite).

## itashimasu — to do (humble)
**JLPT:** N4

The humble form of する, used to lower oneself when describing one's own actions toward someone of higher status.

**JP:** 私がご案内いたします。
**Reading:** Watashi ga goannai itashimasu.
**EN:** I will show you the way (humble).

## janai ka — right?; isn't it?; let's~
**JLPT:** N4

Casual equivalent of ではないか, used to seek agreement, express surprise, or make a suggestion.

**JP:** いいアイデアじゃないか。
**Reading:** Ii aidea janai ka.
**EN:** That's a good idea, isn't it?

## ka dou ka — whether or not ~
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a plain-form clause to express uncertainty about whether something is or isn't the case.

**JP:** 彼が来るかどうか分かりません。
**Reading:** Kare ga kuru ka dou ka wakarimasen.
**EN:** I don't know whether he'll come or not.

## ka shira — I wonder (feminine)
**JLPT:** N4

A softer, often feminine-associated way of expressing wonder or uncertainty, similar to かな.

**JP:** 明日は晴れるかしら。
**Reading:** Ashita wa hareru ka shira.
**EN:** I wonder if it will be sunny tomorrow.

## kai — turns a sentence into a yes/no question
**JLPT:** N4

A casual, often masculine question particle attached to the plain form, softer than か alone.

**JP:** もう食べたかい。
**Reading:** Mou tabeta kai.
**EN:** Have you eaten yet?

## kamo shirenai — might; perhaps; indicates possibility
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a plain-form clause to express that something is possible but uncertain.

**JP:** 明日は雨が降るかもしれません。
**Reading:** Ashita wa ame ga furu kamo shiremasen.
**EN:** It might rain tomorrow.

## kana — I wonder; should I?
**JLPT:** N4

A casual particle used to express wondering or musing to oneself, gender-neutral.

**JP:** どこに置いたかな。
**Reading:** Doko ni oita kana.
**EN:** I wonder where I put it.

## kara tsukuru — made from; made with
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a noun + から作る to describe the raw material something is made from (often implying a transformation).

**JP:** このワインはぶどうから作られます。
**Reading:** Kono wain wa budou kara tsukuraremasu.
**EN:** This wine is made from grapes.

## kitto — surely; undoubtedly; almost certainly
**JLPT:** N4

An adverb expressing strong confidence about a future or unverified event.

**JP:** 彼はきっと来ます。
**Reading:** Kare wa kitto kimasu.
**EN:** He will surely come.

## koro / goro — around; about; when
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a time expression to mean "around" that time, or on its own to mean "the time when ~."

**JP:** 子供の頃、よく公園で遊びました。
**Reading:** Kodomo no koro, yoku kouen de asobimashita.
**EN:** When I was a child, I often played at the park.

## koto — verb nominalizer
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb to turn the action into an abstract noun.

**JP:** 泳ぐことが好きです。
**Reading:** Oyogu koto ga suki desu.
**EN:** I like swimming.

## koto ga aru — there are times when
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb to describe something that occasionally happens.

**JP:** たまに遅刻することがあります。
**Reading:** Tamani chikoku suru koto ga arimasu.
**EN:** There are times when I'm late.

## koto ga dekiru — can; able to
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + ことができる to express general ability or possibility.

**JP:** 私はピアノを弾くことができます。
**Reading:** Watashi wa piano o hiku koto ga dekimasu.
**EN:** I can play the piano.

## koto ni naru — it has been decided that; it turns out that
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb to describe a decision or arrangement made by circumstances rather than the speaker.

**JP:** 来月、大阪に引っ越すことになりました。
**Reading:** Raigetsu, Oosaka ni hikkosu koto ni narimashita.
**EN:** It has been decided that I'll move to Osaka next month.

## koto ni suru — to decide on
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb to describe a decision the speaker personally makes.

**JP:** 毎朝走ることにしました。
**Reading:** Maiasa hashiru koto ni shimashita.
**EN:** I've decided to run every morning.

## ku suru — to make something ~
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the adverbial (く) form of an い-adjective + する to describe deliberately changing something's state.

**JP:** 部屋を明るくしました。
**Reading:** Heya o akaruku shimashita.
**EN:** I made the room brighter.

## kyuu ni — quickly; suddenly; abruptly
**JLPT:** N4

An adverb describing something happening abruptly or without warning.

**JP:** 急に雨が降ってきました。
**Reading:** Kyuu ni ame ga futte kimashita.
**EN:** It suddenly started raining.
