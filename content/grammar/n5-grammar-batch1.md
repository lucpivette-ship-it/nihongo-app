# Grammar — N5 Batch 1 (20 points)

## cha ikenai / ja ikenai — must not do (spoken)
**JLPT:** N5

Casual, spoken contraction of ~てはいけない (must not do). ちゃ is the contraction of ては after most verbs; じゃ is used after で-forms (e.g., 遊んじゃ from 遊んで).

**JP:** 遊んじゃいけない。
**Reading:** Asonja ikenai.
**EN:** You mustn't play around.

## ka~ka — or
**JLPT:** N5

Connects two nouns, verbs, or phrases meaning "or," presenting a choice between the two options.

**JP:** コーヒーか紅茶かどちらがいいですか。
**Reading:** Koohii ka koucha ka dochira ga ii desu ka.
**EN:** Which is better, coffee or tea?

## kata — the way of doing; how to ~
**JLPT:** N5

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb to mean "the way of doing [verb]" or "how to [verb]."

**JP:** 漢字の書き方を教えてください。
**Reading:** Kanji no kakikata o oshiete kudasai.
**EN:** Please teach me how to write kanji.

## mada~te imasen — have not yet done ~
**JLPT:** N5

Combines まだ (still/not yet) with the て-form + いません to say an action has not been completed yet.

**JP:** まだ宿題をやっていません。
**Reading:** Mada shukudai o yatte imasen.
**EN:** I haven't done my homework yet.

## mae ni — before ~
**JLPT:** N5

Attaches after a verb in dictionary form, or a noun + の, to mean "before doing ~" or "before [noun]."

**JP:** 寝る前に歯を磨きます。
**Reading:** Neru mae ni ha o migakimasu.
**EN:** I brush my teeth before going to bed.

## masen ka — would you like to; won't you ~
**JLPT:** N5

Polite negative question form used to invite someone to do something together.

**JP:** 一緒に映画を見ませんか。
**Reading:** Issho ni eiga o mimasen ka.
**EN:** Would you like to watch a movie together?

## mashou — let's ~
**JLPT:** N5

Volitional polite form used to suggest doing something together.

**JP:** そろそろ帰りましょう。
**Reading:** Sorosoro kaerimashou.
**EN:** Let's head home soon.

## mashouka — shall I ~?
**JLPT:** N5

Used to offer to do something for the listener, or to suggest doing something together while checking their opinion.

**JP:** 荷物を持ちましょうか。
**Reading:** Nimotsu o mochimashou ka.
**EN:** Shall I carry your luggage?

## naa — sentence-ending particle (admiration / wish)
**JLPT:** N5

Casual sentence-ending particle expressing admiration, wish, or a mild exclamation to oneself.

**JP:** いい天気だなあ。
**Reading:** Ii tenki da naa.
**EN:** What nice weather!

## naide — without doing ~
**JLPT:** N5

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb (drop the い from the ~ない form) to mean "without doing [A]," often followed by another clause.

**JP:** 朝ごはんを食べないで学校へ行きました。
**Reading:** Asagohan o tabenaide gakkou e ikimashita.
**EN:** I went to school without eating breakfast.

## naide kudasai — please don't ~
**JLPT:** N5

Polite request not to do something, attaching to the negative stem of a verb.

**JP:** ここで写真を撮らないでください。
**Reading:** Koko de shashin o toranaide kudasai.
**EN:** Please don't take photos here.

## naito ikenai — must do; have to ~
**JLPT:** N5

Colloquial form meaning "must do ~," a shortened version of ~なくてはいけない.

**JP:** もう行かないといけない。
**Reading:** Mou ikanaito ikenai.
**EN:** I have to go now.

## naku temo ii — don't have to; it's fine not to ~
**JLPT:** N5

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb to say that doing something is not necessary.

**JP:** 明日は来なくてもいいです。
**Reading:** Ashita wa konakute mo ii desu.
**EN:** You don't have to come tomorrow.

## nakucha — must do; gotta ~ (casual)
**JLPT:** N5

Casual, spoken contraction of ~なくては(いけない), meaning "must do ~."

**JP:** もう寝なくちゃ。
**Reading:** Mou nenakucha.
**EN:** I've gotta go to sleep now.

## nakute wa ikenai — must do ~
**JLPT:** N5

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb or adjective to express obligation, "must do ~."

**JP:** 薬を飲まなくてはいけない。
**Reading:** Kusuri o nomanakute wa ikenai.
**EN:** I have to take the medicine.

## nakute wa naranai — must do ~ (formal)
**JLPT:** N5

Similar to ~なくてはいけない but slightly more formal in tone; expresses obligation.

**JP:** 規則を守らなくてはならない。
**Reading:** Kisoku o mamoranakute wa naranai.
**EN:** One must follow the rules.

## naru — to become
**JLPT:** N5

Verb meaning "to become." Used after な-adjectives (+ に), い-adjectives (drop い, add く), or nouns (+ に).

**JP:** 来月、二十歳になります。
**Reading:** Raigetsu, hatachi ni narimasu.
**EN:** I will turn twenty next month.

## ndesu — explanatory emphasis
**JLPT:** N5

Attaches to plain-form sentences to add explanatory nuance or emphasis, often used when explaining a reason or seeking clarification.

**JP:** 頭が痛いんです。
**Reading:** Atama ga itai n desu.
**EN:** (The thing is,) I have a headache.

## ni iku — go (somewhere) to do ~
**JLPT:** N5

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + に行く to express going somewhere in order to do an action.

**JP:** 友達に会いに行きます。
**Reading:** Tomodachi ni ai ni ikimasu.
**EN:** I'm going to meet my friend.

## ni suru — to decide on ~
**JLPT:** N5

Attaches to a noun + にする to indicate a choice or decision.

**JP:** 私はこれにします。
**Reading:** Watashi wa kore ni shimasu.
**EN:** I'll go with this one.
