# Grammar — N3 Batch 9 (20 points)

## ue de — upon; after; when; for; in order to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the plain past form of a verb + 上で to describe an important step to complete before proceeding, often in formal contexts.

**JP:** よく考えた上で、決めてください。
**Reading:** Yoku kangaeta ue de, kimete kudasai.
**EN:** Please decide after thinking it over carefully.

## ue ni — as well; besides; in addition to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a clause + 上に to add an additional, often compounding, point to a preceding statement.

**JP:** 頭がいい上に、性格もいいです。
**Reading:** Atama ga ii ue ni, seikaku mo ii desu.
**EN:** Not only is he smart, but he also has a good personality.

## wa betsu toshite — aside from; apart from
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + は別として to set aside one point before discussing another.

**JP:** 値段は別として、デザインはとても気に入りました。
**Reading:** Nedan wa betsu toshite, dezain wa totemo ki ni irimashita.
**EN:** Aside from the price, I really liked the design.

## wa mochiron — not to mention; not only... but also
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + はもちろん to state an obvious point before adding an even more notable one.

**JP:** 平日はもちろん、週末も忙しいです。
**Reading:** Heijitsu wa mochiron, shuumatsu mo isogashii desu.
**EN:** Not to mention weekdays, weekends are busy too.

## wa~de yuumei — famous for
**JLPT:** N3

Combines は and で有名 to describe what a place or person is well known for.

**JP:** 京都は紅葉で有名です。
**Reading:** Kyouto wa kouyou de yuumei desu.
**EN:** Kyoto is famous for its autumn leaves.

## wake da — for that reason; no wonder; so you mean
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a plain-form clause + わけだ to state a logical conclusion derived from given information.

**JP:** 彼はアメリカ育ちなんですね。だから英語が上手なわけです。
**Reading:** Kare wa amerika sodachi nan desu ne. Dakara eigo ga jouzu na wake desu.
**EN:** He grew up in America. That's why (it makes sense that) he's good at English.

## wake dewa nai — it doesn't mean that; it's not the case that
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a plain-form clause + わけではない to soften or deny an implication that might be wrongly assumed.

**JP:** 嫌いなわけではありません。
**Reading:** Kirai na wake dewa arimasen.
**EN:** It's not that I dislike it.

## wake ga nai — there is no way that; it is impossible
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a plain-form clause + わけがない to firmly deny a possibility as logically implausible.

**JP:** そんな高い物、買えるわけがありません。
**Reading:** Sonna takai mono, kaeru wake ga arimasen.
**EN:** There's no way I could afford something that expensive.

## wake niwa ikanai — must not; cannot afford to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + わけにはいかない to describe an obligation that can't be avoided due to social or moral reasons.

**JP:** 約束したので、休むわけにはいきません。
**Reading:** Yakusoku shita node, yasumu wake ni wa ikimasen.
**EN:** Since I made a promise, I can't afford to take a day off.

## wari ni — considering; for; despite
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/clause + 割に to note that a result doesn't match what a given standard would suggest.

**JP:** 値段の割に、おいしいです。
**Reading:** Nedan no wari ni, oishii desu.
**EN:** It's delicious considering the price.

## wazato — on purpose; intentionally
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb describing a deliberate action, often one with a slightly negative or mischievous connotation.

**JP:** わざと負けてあげました。
**Reading:** Wazato makete agemashita.
**EN:** I lost on purpose (to let them win).

## wazawaza — to go to the trouble of
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb emphasizing that someone made a special, often unnecessary, effort to do something.

**JP:** わざわざ迎えに来てくれました。
**Reading:** Wazawaza mukae ni kite kuremashita.
**EN:** They went out of their way to come pick me up.

## yorimo — in comparison to; rather than
**JLPT:** N3

An emphatic version of より, used to strengthen a comparison between two things.

**JP:** 去年よりも今年の方が暑いです。
**Reading:** Kyonen yorimo kotoshi no hou ga atsui desu.
**EN:** This year is hotter than last year.

## you ga nai — there is no way to; it's impossible to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + ようがない to say there's no method available to accomplish something.

**JP:** 連絡先が分からないので、連絡のしようがありません。
**Reading:** Renrakusaki ga wakaranai node, renraku no shiyou ga arimasen.
**EN:** I don't know their contact info, so there's no way to reach them.

## you na ki ga suru — have a feeling that; seems like
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a plain-form clause + ような気がする to express a vague, intuitive impression rather than a certainty.

**JP:** 何かいいことが起きるような気がします。
**Reading:** Nanika ii koto ga okiru you na ki ga shimasu.
**EN:** I have a feeling something good is going to happen.

## you ni — in order to; so that
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the dictionary or potential form of a verb + ように to express a goal or hoped-for outcome that isn't directly controllable.

**JP:** 忘れないようにメモしました。
**Reading:** Wasurenai you ni memo shimashita.
**EN:** I took notes so I wouldn't forget.

## you ni mieru — to look; to seem; to appear
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a plain-form clause + ように見える to describe a visual impression or apparent state.

**JP:** 彼は疲れているように見えます。
**Reading:** Kare wa tsukarete iru you ni miemasu.
**EN:** He looks tired.

## you to shinai — not try to; not make an effort to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the volitional form of a verb + としない to describe a refusal or lack of effort to do something.

**JP:** 彼は話を聞こうとしません。
**Reading:** Kare wa hanashi o kikou to shimasen.
**EN:** He won't even try to listen.

## you to suru — try to; attempt to; be about to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the volitional form of a verb + とする to describe an attempt at, or the verge of, an action.

**JP:** ドアを開けようとした時、電話が鳴りました。
**Reading:** Doa o akeyou to shita toki, denwa ga narimashita.
**EN:** Just as I was about to open the door, the phone rang.

## zuni — without doing
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb + ずに to describe doing one action without performing another, similar to ないで.

**JP:** 朝ご飯を食べずに、学校に行きました。
**Reading:** Asagohan o tabezu ni, gakkou ni ikimashita.
**EN:** I went to school without eating breakfast.
