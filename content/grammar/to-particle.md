# と — and / with (connecting particle)

**JLPT level:** N5

Connects nouns into an exhaustive list ("A and B," a complete set) or marks a companion ("with").

## Example
- **JP:** 友達と映画を見ます。
- **Reading:** tomodachi to eiga o mimasu.
- **EN:** I'll watch a movie with a friend.
