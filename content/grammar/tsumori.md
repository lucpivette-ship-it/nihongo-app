# つもり — plan to / intend to

**JLPT level:** N5

Follows a verb's plain (dictionary) form to express intention or a plan.

## Example
- **JP:** 来年、留学するつもりです。
- **Reading:** kurainen, ryuugaku suru tsumori desu.
- **EN:** I plan to study abroad next year.
