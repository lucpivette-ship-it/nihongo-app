# Grammar — N4 Batch 7 (9 points)

## yotei da — plan to, intend to
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + 予定だ to describe a scheduled or planned event.

**JP:** 来月、旅行に行く予定です。
**Reading:** Raigetsu, ryokou ni iku yotei desu.
**EN:** I'm planning to go on a trip next month.

## you da — appears; seems; looks as if
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to a plain-form clause to express a judgment based on the speaker's own observation or inference.

**JP:** 誰もいないようです。
**Reading:** Dare mo inai you desu.
**EN:** It seems no one is here.

## you ni / you na — like; as; similar to
**JLPT:** N4

The adverbial (ように) and noun-modifying (ような) forms of ようだ, used to describe similarity or manner.

**JP:** 彼女は歌手のように歌います。
**Reading:** Kanojo wa kashu no you ni utaimasu.
**EN:** She sings like a professional singer.

## you ni naru — to reach the point that; to come to be that
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the dictionary/potential form of a verb + ようになる to describe a gradual change in ability or state over time.

**JP:** 漢字が読めるようになりました。
**Reading:** Kanji ga yomeru you ni narimashita.
**EN:** I've come to be able to read kanji.

## you ni suru — to try to; to make sure that
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the dictionary or negative form of a verb + ようにする to describe making a conscious effort to maintain a habit.

**JP:** 毎日運動するようにしています。
**Reading:** Mainichi undou suru you ni shite imasu.
**EN:** I make sure to exercise every day.

## you to omou — thinking of doing; planning to
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the volitional form of a verb + と思う to express an intention that's still somewhat tentative.

**JP:** 来年、留学しようと思います。
**Reading:** Rainen, ryuugaku shiyou to omoimasu.
**EN:** I'm thinking of studying abroad next year.

## zehi — by all means; certainly; definitely
**JLPT:** N4

An adverb emphasizing a strong wish or invitation, often paired with a request or invitation verb.

**JP:** ぜひ遊びに来てください。
**Reading:** Zehi asobi ni kite kudasai.
**EN:** Please do come visit, by all means.

## zenzen~nai — (not) at all
**JLPT:** N4

Pairs 全然 with a negative predicate to emphatically deny something entirely.

**JP:** この問題は全然分かりません。
**Reading:** Kono mondai wa zenzen wakarimasen.
**EN:** I don't understand this problem at all.

## zurai — difficult to do
**JLPT:** N4

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb, similar to にくい but often implying an emotional or circumstantial difficulty rather than a physical one.

**JP:** その話は言いづらいです。
**Reading:** Sono hanashi wa iizurai desu.
**EN:** That's a hard thing to say (emotionally difficult).
