# Grammar — N1 Batch 13 (13 points)

## yare yare — good grief; oh boy; here we go
**JLPT:** N1

An interjection of exasperation, relief, or resignation, often used at the start or end of a sentence.

**JP:** やれやれ、また残業か。
**Reading:** Yare yare, mata zangyou ka.
**EN:** Good grief, overtime again?

## you ga / you to — whether; no matter (how); it doesn't matter if
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the volitional form of a verb/adjective + が/と to state that regardless of a condition, the outcome does not change.

**JP:** 何を言われようが、気にしない。
**Reading:** Nani o iwareyou ga, ki ni shinai.
**EN:** No matter what people say to me, I won't worry about it.

## you ga~you ga / you to~you to — whether ~ or ~; regardless of
**JLPT:** N1

Repeats the volitional-form pattern with two contrasting verbs to state that either way, the result is unaffected.

**JP:** 賛成しようが反対しようが、決定は変わらない。
**Reading:** Sansei shiyou ga hantai shiyou ga, kettei wa kawaranai.
**EN:** Whether you agree or disagree, the decision won't change.

## you ni yotte wa / you dewa — depending on how
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a verb + ようによっては to state that the outcome depends heavily on the manner or approach taken.

**JP:** 考えようによっては、これはチャンスだ。
**Reading:** Kangaeyou ni yotte wa, kore wa chansu da.
**EN:** Depending on how you look at it, this is a chance.

## you nimo~nai — cannot even if one wanted to
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the volitional-stem + うにも + a potential-negative verb to express that an action is physically impossible to carry out.

**JP:** 疲れすぎて、動こうにも動けない。
**Reading:** Tsukaresugite, ugokou nimo ugokenai.
**EN:** I'm so exhausted I can't even move even if I wanted to.

## you to~mai to / you ga~mai ga — whether or not
**JLPT:** N1

Pairs the volitional form with its negative counterpart (まい) to state that an outcome holds regardless of whether something happens.

**JP:** 雨が降ろうと降るまいと、試合は行われる。
**Reading:** Ame ga furou to furumai to, shiai wa okonawareru.
**EN:** Whether or not it rains, the match will be held.

## yue ni — therefore; for that reason; because of
**JLPT:** N1

A formal, literary conjunction attaching to a noun/clause to indicate cause and effect.

**JP:** 経験不足ゆえに、失敗も多かった。
**Reading:** Keiken busoku yue ni, shippai mo ookatta.
**EN:** Because of my lack of experience, I made many mistakes.

## zo / ze — sentence-ending particles for emphasis (masculine, casual)
**JLPT:** N1

Sentence-final particles adding blunt, assertive emphasis, associated with casual masculine speech.

**JP:** もう行くぞ。
**Reading:** Mou iku zo.
**EN:** I'm leaving now, you hear?

## zu jimai — ended up not (doing something), despite intending to
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb + ずじまい to express regret that an intended action was never carried out.

**JP:** 結局、彼にお礼を言えずじまいだった。
**Reading:** Kekkyoku, kare ni orei o iezu jimai datta.
**EN:** In the end, I never got around to thanking him.

## zu niwa okanai / nai dewa okanai — cannot help but; will inevitably
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb + ずにはおかない to express that a strong feeling or reaction is unavoidable.

**JP:** この映画は観客を感動させずにはおかない。
**Reading:** Kono eiga wa kankyaku o kandou sasezu ni wa okanai.
**EN:** This movie is bound to move its audience.

## zu niwa sumanai / nai dewa sumanai — cannot avoid; must inevitably
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb + ずには済まない to state that a certain consequence or obligation cannot be avoided.

**JP:** これだけ迷惑をかけたら、謝らずには済まない。
**Reading:** Kore dake meiwaku o kaketara, ayamarazu ni wa sumanai.
**EN:** After causing this much trouble, I can't get away without apologizing.

## zu tomo — even without; even if not
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb + ずとも to state that a certain result still holds even without a given condition, literary register.

**JP:** 言わずとも、彼の気持ちは分かる。
**Reading:** Iwazu tomo, kare no kimochi wa wakaru.
**EN:** Even without him saying it, I understand how he feels.

## zukume — nothing but; all
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to certain nouns + ずくめ to describe a situation entirely filled with one quality, often all-good or all-bad.

**JP:** 今年はいいことずくめだった。
**Reading:** Kotoshi wa ii koto zukume datta.
**EN:** This year was nothing but good things.
