# Grammar — N2 Batch 2 (20 points)

## eru / uru — can; to be able to; is possible to
**JLPT:** N2

A literary suffix attaching to the ます-stem of a verb + 得る to state a possibility exists, the positive counterpart of えない.

**JP:** それは十分にあり得ることです。
**Reading:** Sore wa juubun ni ari uru koto desu.
**EN:** That is a thing that can quite possibly happen.

## futatabi — again; once more
**JLPT:** N2

A formal adverb meaning "again," used mostly in writing or formal speech in place of また.

**JP:** 彼は再びこの町に戻ってきました。
**Reading:** Kare wa futatabi kono machi ni modotte kimashita.
**EN:** He returned to this town once again.

## fuu ni — this way; that way; in such a way; how
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches after a demonstrative or clause + ふうに to describe a manner or way of doing something.

**JP:** こういうふうに書いてください。
**Reading:** Kou iu fuu ni kaite kudasai.
**EN:** Please write it this way.

## ga kikkake de / o kikkake ni — with… as a start; as a result of; taking advantage of
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + がきっかけで or をきっかけに to describe the triggering event behind a change.

**JP:** 友人の紹介がきっかけで、この会社に入りました。
**Reading:** Yuujin no shoukai ga kikkake de, kono kaisha ni hairimashita.
**EN:** A friend's introduction was what led me to join this company.

## ge — looks like; seems like; appears to
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to an adjective stem + げ to describe an outward appearance of a feeling or state.

**JP:** 彼女は寂しげな顔をしていました。
**Reading:** Kanojo wa sabishige na kao o shite imashita.
**EN:** She had a lonely-looking expression.

## gyaku ni — conversely; on the contrary
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb introducing a statement that reverses or contrasts with the preceding one.

**JP:** 忙しい時ほど、逆に集中できます。
**Reading:** Isogashii toki hodo, gyaku ni shuuchuu dekimasu.
**EN:** The busier I am, the more, conversely, I can concentrate.

## hanmen — while, although; on the other hand
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + 反面 to present a contrasting aspect alongside a previously stated fact.

**JP:** この仕事は給料がいい反面、とても忙しいです。
**Reading:** Kono shigoto wa kyuuryou ga ii hanmen, totemo isogashii desu.
**EN:** This job pays well, but on the other hand it's very busy.

## hatashite — as was expected; sure enough; really; actually
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb emphasizing whether an anticipated outcome actually occurred, often used with a question.

**JP:** 果たして彼の計画はうまくいくのでしょうか。
**Reading:** Hatashite kare no keikaku wa umaku iku no deshou ka.
**EN:** Will his plan really go well, I wonder?

## ichiou — more or less; pretty much; roughly; tentatively
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb describing an action done to a provisional or minimal degree of completeness.

**JP:** 一応、準備はできています。
**Reading:** Ichiou, junbi wa dekite imasu.
**EN:** Preparations are more or less done.

## igai — with the exception of; excepting
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + 以外 to exclude one item while implying a statement applies to everything else.

**JP:** 彼以外は全員来ました。
**Reading:** Kare igai wa zen'in kimashita.
**EN:** Everyone except him came.

## ijou ni — more than; not less than; beyond
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + 以上に to indicate a degree exceeding a comparison point.

**JP:** 思った以上に難しかったです。
**Reading:** Omotta ijou ni muzukashikatta desu.
**EN:** It was more difficult than I expected.

## ijou wa — because; since; seeing that
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + 以上は to state a firm conclusion that follows logically from an established fact.

**JP:** 約束した以上は、必ず守ります。
**Reading:** Yakusoku shita ijou wa, kanarazu mamorimasu.
**EN:** Since I made a promise, I will definitely keep it.

## ikinari — abruptly; suddenly; all of a sudden; without warning
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb describing an action that occurs unexpectedly, without any preceding buildup.

**JP:** いきなり雨が降り出しました。
**Reading:** Ikinari ame ga furidashimashita.
**EN:** It suddenly started to rain.

## ikki ni — in one go; without stopping; all at once; immediately; instantly; right away
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb describing an action completed in a single continuous burst.

**JP:** ジュースを一気に飲み干しました。
**Reading:** Juusu o ikki ni nomihoshimashita.
**EN:** I drank the juice all in one go.

## ippou de — on one hand, on the other hand; although
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + 一方で to contrast two related but different aspects of a situation.

**JP:** 都会は便利な一方で、人が多くて疲れます。
**Reading:** Tokai wa benri na ippou de, hito ga ookute tsukaremasu.
**EN:** The city is convenient, but on the other hand it's tiring with so many people.

## iwayuru — what is called; as it is called; the so-called; so to speak
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb preceding a term to note it is a commonly-used or established label for something.

**JP:** 彼はいわゆる天才です。
**Reading:** Kare wa iwayuru tensai desu.
**EN:** He is what you'd call a genius.

## iyoiyo — at last; finally; beyond doubt
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb marking the imminent arrival of a long-anticipated moment.

**JP:** いよいよ試験の日がやってきました。
**Reading:** Iyoiyo shiken no hi ga yatte kimashita.
**EN:** At last, the day of the exam has arrived.

## jou — for the sake of; from the standpoint of; as a matter of; in terms of
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + 上 to specify the perspective or category from which a statement is being made.

**JP:** 健康上の理由で、お酒をやめました。
**Reading:** Kenkoujou no riyuu de, osake o yamemashita.
**EN:** For health reasons, I quit drinking alcohol.

## ka no you ni — as if; just like
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a plain-form clause + かのように to describe an appearance or behavior resembling something untrue.

**JP:** 彼は何も知らないかのように振る舞いました。
**Reading:** Kare wa nani mo shiranai ka no you ni furumaimashita.
**EN:** He behaved as if he knew nothing.

## ka to omottara — just when; no sooner than
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + かと思ったら to describe a surprising or immediate follow-up event right after the first.

**JP:** 晴れたかと思ったら、すぐにまた雨が降りました。
**Reading:** Hareta ka to omottara, sugu ni mata ame ga furimashita.
**EN:** Just when it cleared up, it started raining again right away.
