# Grammar — N3 Batch 8 (20 points)

## to iu to — speaking of; when you talk of
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/clause + と言うと to pick up a keyword and elaborate or ask about it further.

**JP:** 京都と言うと、清水寺を思い出します。
**Reading:** Kyouto to iu to, Kiyomizudera o omoidashimasu.
**EN:** Speaking of Kyoto, it makes me think of Kiyomizu Temple.

## to iu yori — rather than
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/clause + というより to correct or refine a preceding characterization with a more accurate one.

**JP:** 忙しいというより、疲れているんです。
**Reading:** Isogashii to iu yori, tsukarete iru n desu.
**EN:** It's less that I'm busy, more that I'm tired.

## to mieru / to miete — it seems that
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a plain-form clause + とみえる/とみえて to describe a judgment based on visible evidence.

**JP:** 疲れているとみえて、すぐに寝てしまいました。
**Reading:** Tsukarete iru to miete, sugu ni nete shimaimashita.
**EN:** He seemed tired, and fell asleep right away.

## to sureba / to shitara / to suru to — assuming; if A then B
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a plain-form clause + とすれば/としたら/とすると to set up a hypothetical premise and reason from it.

**JP:** もし宝くじが当たったとしたら、家を買います。
**Reading:** Moshi takarakuji ga atatta to shitara, ie o kaimasu.
**EN:** If I were to win the lottery, I'd buy a house.

## to tomo ni — together with; as well as
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + と共に to describe two things happening or existing together.

**JP:** 時代の変化と共に、生活も変わりました。
**Reading:** Jidai no henka to tomo ni, seikatsu mo kawarimashita.
**EN:** Along with the changing times, our lifestyle has also changed.

## tochuu de / tochuu ni — on the way; in the middle of
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/clause + 途中で/途中に to describe something happening partway through a journey or process.

**JP:** 帰る途中で、雨に降られました。
**Reading:** Kaeru tochuu de, ame ni furaremashita.
**EN:** I got caught in the rain on my way home.

## tokoro de — by the way
**JLPT:** N3

A conjunction used to abruptly change the subject in a conversation.

**JP:** ところで、来週の予定は決まりましたか。
**Reading:** Tokoro de, raishuu no yotei wa kimarimashita ka.
**EN:** By the way, have you decided on next week's plans?

## tokoro ga — even so; however
**JLPT:** N3

A conjunction introducing an unexpected result that contradicts what was just stated.

**JP:** 晴れると思っていました。ところが、雨が降りました。
**Reading:** Hareru to omotte imashita. Tokoro ga, ame ga furimashita.
**EN:** I thought it would be sunny. However, it rained.

## toori ni — in the same way as; as
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/clause + とおりに to describe an action performed exactly as instructed or shown.

**JP:** 先生が言ったとおりにやりました。
**Reading:** Sensei ga itta toori ni yarimashita.
**EN:** I did it exactly as the teacher said.

## toosu — to do until the end; through
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + 通す to describe carrying an action through to completion, or maintaining a state throughout.

**JP:** 最後まで自分の意見を言い通しました。
**Reading:** Saigo made jibun no iken o ii tooshimashita.
**EN:** I asserted my opinion all the way through to the end.

## toshite — as; in the role of
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + として to specify the capacity or role in which someone or something acts.

**JP:** 彼は先生として働いています。
**Reading:** Kare wa sensei toshite hataraite imasu.
**EN:** He works as a teacher.

## totemo~nai — cannot possibly be; hardly
**JLPT:** N3

Pairs とても with a negative predicate to emphasize that something is absolutely impossible.

**JP:** これはとても食べられません。
**Reading:** Kore wa totemo taberaremasen.
**EN:** There's no way I could possibly eat this.

## towa kagiranai — not necessarily so
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a plain-form clause + とは限らない to qualify a general statement, noting exceptions exist.

**JP:** 高いものがいいとは限りません。
**Reading:** Takai mono ga ii to wa kagirimasen.
**EN:** Expensive things aren't necessarily good.

## tsui — accidentally; unintentionally; by mistake
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb describing an action done without conscious intent, often out of habit.

**JP:** ついお菓子を食べ過ぎてしまいました。
**Reading:** Tsui okashi o tabesugite shimaimashita.
**EN:** I accidentally ate too many snacks.

## tsui ni — finally; at last; in the end
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb marking the culmination of a long process, often with emotional weight.

**JP:** ついに夢がかないました。
**Reading:** Tsui ni yume ga kanaimashita.
**EN:** My dream finally came true.

## tsuide ni — while; incidentally; on the way
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a clause + ついでに to describe doing something extra while already doing another action.

**JP:** 買い物のついでに、郵便局にも寄りました。
**Reading:** Kaimono no tsuide ni, yuubinkyoku ni mo yorimashita.
**EN:** While out shopping, I also stopped by the post office.

## tsumari — in other words; in summary
**JLPT:** N3

A conjunction used to summarize or restate a preceding point more concisely.

**JP:** つまり、彼は嘘をついていたのです。
**Reading:** Tsumari, kare wa uso o tsuite ita no desu.
**EN:** In other words, he was lying.

## tsumori datta — I thought I~; was planning to~
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + つもりだった to describe a past intention, often one that didn't pan out.

**JP:** 早く着くつもりだったのに、遅れました。
**Reading:** Hayaku tsuku tsumori datta noni, okuremashita.
**EN:** I was planning to arrive early, but I ended up being late.

## tsumori de — with the intention of doing
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + つもりで to describe acting under a certain intention or mindset.

**JP:** 冗談のつもりで言いました。
**Reading:** Joudan no tsumori de iimashita.
**EN:** I said it with the intention of it being a joke.

## uchi ni — while; before
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/clause + うちに to describe doing something within a limited window before a state changes.

**JP:** 温かいうちに食べてください。
**Reading:** Atatakai uchi ni tabete kudasai.
**EN:** Please eat it while it's still warm.
