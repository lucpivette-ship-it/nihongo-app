# Grammar — N1 Batch 6 (20 points)

## nari tomo — at least; even just a little
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + なりとも to state a minimal, modest degree that is still acceptable or hoped for.

**JP:** 少しなりともお役に立てれば幸いです。
**Reading:** Sukoshi nari tomo oyaku ni tatereba saiwai desu.
**EN:** I'd be glad if I could be of even a little help.

## nari~nari — [A] or [B] or something; for instance
**JLPT:** N1

Repeats なり after two example nouns to suggest either option as an example among possible choices.

**JP:** 電話なりメールなり、連絡してください。
**Reading:** Denwa nari meeru nari, renraku shite kudasai.
**EN:** Please get in touch, whether by phone or email or whatever works.

## nashi ni / nashi de — without; without doing
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + なしに to state that an action was done, or should be done, without a certain accompanying element.

**JP:** 断りなしに部屋に入らないでください。
**Reading:** Kotowari nashi ni heya ni hairanaide kudasai.
**EN:** Please don't enter the room without permission.

## ni — and; in addition to; the combination of
**JLPT:** N1

A formal usage of に connecting parallel items to indicate an accumulating combination, distinct from the basic particle use.

**JP:** 朝食にパンにコーヒー、それだけです。
**Reading:** Choushoku ni pan ni koohii, sore dake desu.
**EN:** Breakfast is just bread and coffee, that's all.

## ni atai suru — to be worth; to be worthy of; to deserve; to merit
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + に値する to state that something fully merits a particular evaluation or reward.

**JP:** この作品は評価に値する。
**Reading:** Kono sakuhin wa hyouka ni atai suru.
**EN:** This work deserves recognition.

## ni atte — at; on; during; in the condition of
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + にあって to describe a formal circumstance or situation in which something takes place.

**JP:** 非常時にあって、冷静さを保つことが大切だ。
**Reading:** Hijouji ni atte, reisei-sa o tamotsu koto ga taisetsu da.
**EN:** In an emergency, it's important to stay calm.

## ni hikikae — compared to; in contrast to; unlike; while; whereas
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + に引き換え to draw a sharp contrast between two situations or people.

**JP:** 兄は真面目なのに引き換え、弟は遊んでばかりだ。
**Reading:** Ani wa majime na no ni hikikae, otouto wa asonde bakari da.
**EN:** Unlike his serious older brother, the younger one does nothing but play.

## ni itaru / ni itatta — leads to; come to a conclusion
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun/clause + に至る to describe a process finally reaching a significant conclusion or point.

**JP:** 長い交渉の末、合意に至った。
**Reading:** Nagai koushou no sue, goui ni itatta.
**EN:** After long negotiations, we finally reached an agreement.

## ni itaru made — as far as; up until; everything from ... to
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + に至るまで to emphasize an extreme range, "all the way up to and including."

**JP:** 細かい点に至るまで確認した。
**Reading:** Komakai ten ni itaru made kakunin shita.
**EN:** I checked everything, down to the smallest details.

## ni itattemo — even if; even though; although something reached the point
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun/clause + に至っても to describe a situation persisting even after reaching an extreme point.

**JP:** この期に至っても、彼はまだ諦めない。
**Reading:** Kono ki ni itattemo, kare wa mada akiramenai.
**EN:** Even at this stage, he still hasn't given up.

## ni ittate wa — when it comes to; as for
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + に至っては to single out an extreme example within a larger set for special comment.

**JP:** 三男に至っては、全く勉強しない。
**Reading:** San'nan ni ittate wa, mattaku benkyou shinai.
**EN:** As for the youngest son, he doesn't study at all.

## ni iwasereba — if you ask; if one may say
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a person + に言わせれば to introduce that person's personal, often blunt, opinion.

**JP:** 私に言わせれば、それは間違っている。
**Reading:** Watashi ni iwasereba, sore wa machigatte iru.
**EN:** If you ask me, that's wrong.

## ni kagitta koto dewa nai — not limited to only
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + に限ったことではない to state that a phenomenon extends well beyond the specific case mentioned.

**JP:** これは今回に限ったことではない。
**Reading:** Kore wa konkai ni kagitta koto dewa nai.
**EN:** This isn't limited to just this time.

## ni kakatte wa / ni kakattara / ni kakaru to / kakareba — when handled by (N), becomes a completely different result
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a person + にかかっては to describe how a situation transforms entirely depending on who handles it.

**JP:** 彼にかかっては、どんな難問も簡単に解けてしまう。
**Reading:** Kare ni kakatte wa, donna nanmon mo kantan ni tokete shimau.
**EN:** When it's in his hands, even the hardest problem gets solved easily.

## ni kakatteiru — depending on; to depend on; whether or not
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + にかかっている to state that an outcome hinges entirely on a certain factor.

**JP:** 成功は準備にかかっている。
**Reading:** Seikou wa junbi ni kakatte iru.
**EN:** Success depends on preparation.

## ni kako tsukete — to use as a pretext; to use as an excuse
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + にかこつけて to describe using a convenient reason as cover for a different real motive.

**JP:** 出張にかこつけて、旅行を楽しんだ。
**Reading:** Shutchou ni kakotsukete, ryokou o tanoshinda.
**EN:** Using the business trip as a pretext, he enjoyed some sightseeing.

## ni kamakete — to be too busy; to focus / concentrate only on
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + にかまけて to describe neglecting other duties because of being absorbed in one thing.

**JP:** 仕事にかまけて、家族との時間を減らしてしまった。
**Reading:** Shigoto ni kamakete, kazoku to no jikan o herashite shimatta.
**EN:** Being too absorbed in work, I ended up reducing time with my family.

## ni katakunai — easy to do; it's not hard to (imagine, understand, guess)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + に難くない to state that something (usually imagining or understanding) is easily done given the circumstances.

**JP:** 彼の苦労は想像に難くない。
**Reading:** Kare no kurou wa souzou ni katakunai.
**EN:** It's not hard to imagine how much he struggled.

## ni matsuwaru — to be related to; to concern; associated with
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + にまつわる to describe something connected to or surrounding a particular topic.

**JP:** この土地にまつわる伝説がある。
**Reading:** Kono tochi ni matsuwaru densetsu ga aru.
**EN:** There's a legend associated with this land.

## ni nottotte — based on; according to; in accordance with
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + に則って to describe following an established rule, law, or precedent.

**JP:** 規則に則って手続きを行う。
**Reading:** Kisoku ni nottotte tetsuzuki o okonau.
**EN:** We carry out the procedure in accordance with the rules.
