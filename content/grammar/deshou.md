# でしょう — probably / I think / right?

**JLPT level:** N5

Attaches to the end of a sentence to express a guess, a soft assertion, or to seek agreement.

## Example
- **JP:** 明日は晴れるでしょう。
- **Reading:** ashita wa hareru deshou.
- **EN:** It will probably be sunny tomorrow.
