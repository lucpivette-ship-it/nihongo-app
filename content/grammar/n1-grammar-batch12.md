# Grammar — N1 Batch 12 (20 points)

## toriwake — especially; above all
**JLPT:** N1

A formal adverb singling out one item as notably more so than the rest of a group.

**JP:** 今年はとりわけ寒い。
**Reading:** Kotoshi wa toriwake samui.
**EN:** This year is especially cold.

## toshita koto ga — of all people, who would have thought?
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a person + としたことが to express surprise that someone normally reliable made an uncharacteristic mistake.

**JP:** 私としたことが、うっかりしていた。
**Reading:** Watashi to shita koto ga, ukkari shite ita.
**EN:** I can't believe I of all people was so careless.

## tossa ni — at once; right away; promptly
**JLPT:** N1

An adverb describing an instantaneous, instinctive reaction with no time to think.

**JP:** とっさに手を伸ばして支えた。
**Reading:** Tossa ni te o nobashite sasaeta.
**EN:** I instantly reached out and caught it.

## tote — even; even if/though
**JLPT:** N1

A classical literary particle attaching to a noun/clause to concede a point similarly to でも or としても.

**JP:** 子供とて、責任がないわけではない。
**Reading:** Kodomo tote, sekinin ga nai wake dewa nai.
**EN:** Even being a child doesn't mean he has no responsibility.

## towa — I didn't know; I was surprised that; the fact that
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a plain-form clause + とは to express strong surprise or shock at a fact just learned.

**JP:** まさか彼が犯人だとは。
**Reading:** Masaka kare ga hannin da to wa.
**EN:** I can't believe he was the culprit!

## towa ie — though; although; be that as it may; nonetheless
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a clause + とはいえ to concede a preceding point while still asserting an important qualification.

**JP:** 経験者とはいえ、油断は禁物だ。
**Reading:** Keikensha to wa ie, yudan wa kinmotsu da.
**EN:** Even though he's experienced, carelessness is still not permitted.

## towa kurabemono ni naranai — can't compare with
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + とは比べものにならない to state that the difference between two things is so vast that comparison is meaningless.

**JP:** 昔とは比べものにならないほど便利になった。
**Reading:** Mukashi to wa kurabemono ni naranai hodo benri ni natta.
**EN:** It's become so much more convenient it's not even comparable to the past.

## towa utte kawatte / to wa utte kawari — unlike; totally different; very different from
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + とは打って変わって to describe a dramatic, sudden change from a previous state.

**JP:** 昨日とは打って変わって、今日は晴天だ。
**Reading:** Kinou to wa utte kawatte, kyou wa seiten da.
**EN:** Completely unlike yesterday, today is bright and sunny.

## tsu~tsu — and ~ (indicates two contrasting actions)
**JLPT:** N1

Repeats つ between two opposite verb stems to describe alternating or simultaneous contrasting actions, literary register.

**JP:** 行きつ戻りつ、悩んでいた。
**Reading:** Ikitsu modoritsu, nayande ita.
**EN:** Going back and forth, I was troubled over the decision.

## tsukusu — to use up (completely without anything remaining); to exhaust; to run out of
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + 尽くす to describe doing an action to its absolute fullest extent.

**JP:** 力を尽くして戦った。
**Reading:** Chikara o tsukushite tatakatta.
**EN:** I fought giving it everything I had.

## tteba / ttara — speaking of; I told you already; come on (annoyance / dissatisfaction)
**JLPT:** N1

A colloquial emphatic particle attaching to a noun/clause to express mild frustration or insistence.

**JP:** もう、お父さんったら。
**Reading:** Mou, otousan ttara.
**EN:** Honestly, Dad, come on.

## uchi ni hairanai — not really; can't be regarded as
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + うちに入らない to dismiss something as not really qualifying as a genuine instance of that category.

**JP:** これくらいは苦労のうちに入らない。
**Reading:** Kore kurai wa kurou no uchi ni hairanai.
**EN:** Something this minor doesn't even count as real hardship.

## wa — feminine sentence ending particle
**JLPT:** N1

A sentence-final particle traditionally associated with soft, feminine speech, adding a gentle assertive tone.

**JP:** 私、行くわ。
**Reading:** Watashi, iku wa.
**EN:** I'll go.

## wa dou de are — however; whatever
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + はどうであれ to state that regardless of a certain factor, the conclusion remains the same.

**JP:** 結果はどうであれ、努力は認めるべきだ。
**Reading:** Kekka wa dou de are, doryoku wa mitomeru beki da.
**EN:** Whatever the result, the effort should be recognized.

## wa oroka — let alone; not to mention; needless to say
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + はおろか to dismiss a lesser case before making an even stronger point about a related, larger one.

**JP:** 漢字はおろか、ひらがなも読めない。
**Reading:** Kanji wa oroka, hiragana mo yomenai.
**EN:** Let alone kanji, he can't even read hiragana.

## wa sateoki — setting aside
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + はさておき to temporarily set a topic aside before moving to a different point.

**JP:** 冗談はさておき、本題に入りましょう。
**Reading:** Joudan wa sateoki, hondai ni hairimashou.
**EN:** Joking aside, let's get to the main topic.

## wa socchinoke de / o socchinoke de — ignoring (one thing) for (another)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + はそっちのけで to describe neglecting an expected duty in favor of something else.

**JP:** 宿題はそっちのけで、ゲームばかりしている。
**Reading:** Shukudai wa socchinoke de, geemu bakari shite iru.
**EN:** Ignoring his homework entirely, all he does is play games.

## wa~wade — and (listing negative things happening at the same time)
**JLPT:** N1

Repeats わ between clauses to list multiple negative or troublesome things occurring together, colloquial register.

**JP:** 雨は降るわ、電車は遅れるわで、散々だった。
**Reading:** Ame wa furu wa, densha wa okureru wa de, sanzan datta.
**EN:** It was a terrible day — it rained, the train was late, everything went wrong.

## ya ina ya — as soon as; the moment
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + や否や to describe a second event happening the instant the first occurs.

**JP:** ドアを開けるや否や、猫が飛び出した。
**Reading:** Doa o akeru ya ina ya, neko ga tobidashita.
**EN:** The moment I opened the door, the cat bolted out.

## ya shinai — should do, but don't; absolutely not; there's no way
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + やしない to emphatically deny that something will happen, colloquial register.

**JP:** そんなの信じやしない。
**Reading:** Sonna no shinji ya shinai.
**EN:** There's no way I'd believe something like that.
