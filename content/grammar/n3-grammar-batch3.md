# Grammar — N3 Batch 3 (20 points)

## kesshite~nai — never; by no means
**JLPT:** N3

Pairs 決して with a negative predicate for a strong, absolute denial.

**JP:** 彼は決して嘘をつきません。
**Reading:** Kare wa kesshite uso o tsukimasen.
**EN:** He never lies.

## kirenai — unable to; too much to finish
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + 切れない to describe an action that's impossible to complete fully due to quantity or difficulty.

**JP:** 一人では食べきれない量です。
**Reading:** Hitori dewa tabekirenai ryou desu.
**EN:** It's too much to finish eating alone.

## kiri — only; just; since (and nothing since)
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches after a noun or verb + きり to describe a single occurrence with no repetition since.

**JP:** 去年会ったきりです。
**Reading:** Kyonen atta kiri desu.
**EN:** I haven't seen him since we met last year.

## kiru — to do something completely
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + 切る to emphasize doing an action thoroughly or to its conclusion.

**JP:** マラソンを走りきりました。
**Reading:** Marason o hashirikirimashita.
**EN:** I ran the marathon all the way to the end.

## kke — a casual tag to confirm a memory
**JLPT:** N3

A casual sentence-ending particle used when trying to recall or confirm something uncertain.

**JP:** 今日は何曜日だっけ？
**Reading:** Kyou wa nan youbi dakke?
**EN:** What day is it today, again?

## komu — to do for a long time; to move inward
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + 込む to describe an action done deeply, persistently, or into an enclosed space.

**JP:** 彼はすっかり考え込んでしまいました。
**Reading:** Kare wa sukkari kangaekonde shimaimashita.
**EN:** He fell deep into thought.

## koso — precisely; for sure; especially
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches after a word to add strong emphasis, singling it out from alternatives.

**JP:** 今度こそ成功します。
**Reading:** Kondo koso seikou shimasu.
**EN:** This time, for sure, I will succeed.

## koto — (must) do (indirect command)
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the dictionary or negative form of a verb + こと to state a rule or requirement, often in written notices.

**JP:** 教室では静かにすること。
**Reading:** Kyoushitsu de wa shizuka ni suru koto.
**EN:** Be quiet in the classroom. (rule)

## koto kara — from the fact that
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a clause + ことから to explain a reason or origin based on an observed fact.

**JP:** 声が高いことから、彼女だと分かりました。
**Reading:** Koe ga takai koto kara, kanojo da to wakarimashita.
**EN:** From the fact that the voice was high, I could tell it was her.

## koto ni natteiru — it is decided/expected that
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a clause + ことになっている to describe an established rule, custom, or standing arrangement.

**JP:** 会議は月曜日に行うことになっています。
**Reading:** Kaigi wa getsuyoubi ni okonau koto ni natte imasu.
**EN:** It's been decided that the meeting will be held on Monday.

## koto wa nai — there is no need to; never happens
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + ことはない to reassure that something isn't necessary or doesn't occur.

**JP:** 心配することはないですよ。
**Reading:** Shinpai suru koto wa nai desu yo.
**EN:** There's no need to worry.

## koto wa~ga — although; but
**JLPT:** N3

Repeats a verb/adjective with こと sandwiched, then contrasts it with が, conceding one point while adding a "but."

**JP:** 行くことは行きますが、遅くなります。
**Reading:** Iku koto wa ikimasu ga, osoku narimasu.
**EN:** I will go, but I'll be late.

## kurai / gurai — approximately; to the extent that
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches after a quantity or clause to indicate an approximate amount or degree.

**JP:** 一時間ぐらい待ちました。
**Reading:** Ichijikan gurai machimashita.
**EN:** I waited for about an hour.

## kuse ni — and yet; though; despite
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a clause + くせに to criticize a contradiction between someone's status/claim and their action.

**JP:** 知っているくせに教えてくれません。
**Reading:** Shitte iru kuse ni oshiete kuremasen.
**EN:** Even though he knows, he won't tell me.

## maru de — as if; as though; just like
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb, often paired with みたいだ or ようだ, to describe a vivid comparison.

**JP:** まるで夢のようです。
**Reading:** Marude yume no you desu.
**EN:** It's just like a dream.

## masaka — there's no way; that's impossible
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb expressing strong disbelief that something could be true.

**JP:** まさか彼が犯人だとは思いませんでした。
**Reading:** Masaka kare ga hannin da to wa omoimasen deshita.
**EN:** I never imagined he could be the culprit.

## metta ni~nai — hardly; rarely; seldom
**JLPT:** N3

Pairs めったに with a negative predicate to describe something that almost never happens.

**JP:** 彼にはめったに会いません。
**Reading:** Kare ni wa metta ni aimasen.
**EN:** I rarely see him.

## mo~ba~mo — and; also; either/or
**JLPT:** N3

A structure that lists multiple items or qualities together using も and ば.

**JP:** 頭もよければ、性格もいいです。
**Reading:** Atama mo yokereba, seikaku mo ii desu.
**EN:** He's both smart and has a good personality.

## moshika shitara — perhaps; maybe; by any chance
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb introducing a tentative possibility, often followed by a speculative statement.

**JP:** もしかしたら、雨が降るかもしれません。
**Reading:** Moshika shitara, ame ga furu kamo shiremasen.
**EN:** It might possibly rain.

## moshimo~tara — if; in the case that
**JLPT:** N3

An emphatic version of もし, pairing もしも with a たら-conditional to introduce a hypothetical scenario.

**JP:** もしも宝くじが当たったら、何をしますか。
**Reading:** Moshimo takarakuji ga atattara, nani o shimasu ka.
**EN:** If you won the lottery, what would you do?
