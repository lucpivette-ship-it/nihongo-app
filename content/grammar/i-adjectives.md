# い-adjectives — descriptive adjectives ending in い

**JLPT level:** N5

Adjectives ending in い that describe a noun directly or predicate a sentence by conjugating on their own (past: 〜かった, negative: 〜くない), without needing だ/です to negate.

## Example
- **JP:** この本は面白いです。
- **Reading:** kono hon wa omoshiroi desu.
- **EN:** This book is interesting.
