# だ／です — to be (am, is, are)

**JLPT level:** N5

The copula that links a noun or な-adjective to the subject of a sentence. です is the polite form; だ is the plain/casual form.

## Example
- **JP:** これは辞書です。
- **Reading:** kore wa jisho desu.
- **EN:** This is a dictionary.
