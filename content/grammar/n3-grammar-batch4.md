# Grammar — N3 Batch 4 (20 points)

## muke — intended for; aimed at
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + 向け to describe a product or service designed for a specific audience.

**JP:** これは子供向けの本です。
**Reading:** Kore wa kodomo muke no hon desu.
**EN:** This is a book aimed at children.

## muki — suitable for
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + 向き to describe something that naturally suits or faces a particular group or direction.

**JP:** この服は夏向きです。
**Reading:** Kono fuku wa natsu muki desu.
**EN:** These clothes are suitable for summer.

## mushiro — rather; instead; better
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb expressing a preference for one option over another, often reversing an expectation.

**JP:** 休むより、むしろ働きたいです。
**Reading:** Yasumu yori, mushiro hatarakitai desu.
**EN:** Rather than rest, I'd prefer to work.

## nagara mo — but; although; despite
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb or an adjective + ながらも to concede a contrasting fact.

**JP:** 小さいながらも、立派な会社です。
**Reading:** Chiisai nagara mo, rippa na kaisha desu.
**EN:** Despite being small, it's an admirable company.

## nai koto wa nai — is not impossible to; can do
**JLPT:** N3

A double-negative construction meaning something is possible, just with some hesitation or difficulty.

**JP:** 行けないことはないですが、大変です。
**Reading:** Ikenai koto wa nai desu ga, taihen desu.
**EN:** It's not impossible to go, but it'd be tough.

## naito — must do; unless/if you don't
**JLPT:** N3

A casual, shortened form of ないといけない, expressing obligation.

**JP:** もう帰らないと。
**Reading:** Mou kaeranaito.
**EN:** I have to head home now.

## nakanaka — quite; rather; just not (with negative)
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb emphasizing that something is surprisingly good/difficult, or with a negative, that it isn't happening easily.

**JP:** このパズルはなかなか難しいです。
**Reading:** Kono pazuru wa nakanaka muzukashii desu.
**EN:** This puzzle is quite difficult.

## nanka / nante / nado — things like; such as (belittling)
**JLPT:** N3

Casual particles used to give an example while expressing modesty, disdain, or understatement.

**JP:** 私なんかには無理です。
**Reading:** Watashi nanka ni wa muri desu.
**EN:** Something like this is impossible for someone like me.

## naosu — to do again; to redo
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + 直す to describe correcting or redoing an action.

**JP:** 間違えたので、書き直しました。
**Reading:** Machigaeta node, kakinaoshimashita.
**EN:** I made a mistake, so I rewrote it.

## naru beku — as much as possible
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb requesting maximum effort toward a goal, similar to できるだけ.

**JP:** なるべく早く来てください。
**Reading:** Narubeku hayaku kite kudasai.
**EN:** Please come as early as possible.

## nazenara — because; the reason is
**JLPT:** N3

A formal conjunction introducing a reason for a previously stated claim, often followed by ...からです.

**JP:** 彼は来ませんでした。なぜなら、病気だったからです。
**Reading:** Kare wa kimasen deshita. Nazenara, byouki datta kara desu.
**EN:** He didn't come. The reason is, he was sick.

## ndatte — I hear that; heard that
**JLPT:** N3

A casual, spoken form reporting information heard from someone else, similar to んだって.

**JP:** 明日は雨なんだって。
**Reading:** Ashita wa ame nan datte.
**EN:** I heard it's going to rain tomorrow.

## ni chigai nai — I'm sure; no doubt that
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a plain-form clause + に違いない to express strong confidence in a conclusion.

**JP:** これは彼のミスに違いない。
**Reading:** Kore wa kare no misu ni chigai nai.
**EN:** This must be his mistake.

## ni hanshite — against; contrary to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + に反して to describe something opposite to expectations or a stated rule.

**JP:** 予想に反して、試合に負けました。
**Reading:** Yosou ni hanshite, shiai ni makemashita.
**EN:** Contrary to expectations, we lost the match.

## ni kakete — through; concerning
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun (often time or place) + にかけて to describe a broad range or area of expertise.

**JP:** 料理にかけては彼女が一番です。
**Reading:** Ryouri ni kakete wa kanojo ga ichiban desu.
**EN:** When it comes to cooking, she's the best.

## ni kan suru / ni kan shite — about; regarding
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + に関する/に関して to introduce the topic being discussed or described.

**JP:** この件に関して、質問があります。
**Reading:** Kono ken ni kanshite, shitsumon ga arimasu.
**EN:** I have a question regarding this matter.

## ni kawatte / ni kawari — instead of; on behalf of
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + にかわって to describe acting as a substitute or representative for someone.

**JP:** 社長にかわって、部長が挨拶しました。
**Reading:** Shachou ni kawatte, buchou ga aisatsu shimashita.
**EN:** The department head gave the greeting on behalf of the president.

## ni kurabete — compared to; in comparison to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + に比べて to set up a comparison between two things.

**JP:** 去年に比べて、今年は暑いです。
**Reading:** Kyonen ni kurabete, kotoshi wa atsui desu.
**EN:** Compared to last year, this year is hot.

## ni nareru — to be used to; to get used to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + に慣れる to describe becoming accustomed to a new situation or environment.

**JP:** 新しい生活に慣れました。
**Reading:** Atarashii seikatsu ni naremashita.
**EN:** I've gotten used to my new life.

## ni oite / ni okeru — in; at; regarding
**JLPT:** N3

A formal, written equivalent of で, marking a location, field, or context for an action or state.

**JP:** この分野における研究は進んでいます。
**Reading:** Kono bun'ya ni okeru kenkyuu wa susunde imasu.
**EN:** Research in this field is progressing.
