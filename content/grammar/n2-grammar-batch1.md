# Grammar — N2 Batch 1 (20 points)

## ageku — to end up; in the end; finally; after all
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a plain past-tense clause + あげく to describe a negative or troublesome final outcome after a long process.

**JP:** さんざん悩んだあげく、会社を辞めることにしました。
**Reading:** Sanzan nayanda ageku, kaisha o yameru koto ni shimashita.
**EN:** After agonizing over it for a long time, I ended up deciding to quit the company.

## aruiwa — or; either; maybe; perhaps; possibly
**JLPT:** N2

A conjunction connecting two alternatives, or an adverb expressing uncertainty about a possibility.

**JP:** 電車あるいはバスで行くつもりです。
**Reading:** Densha aruiwa basu de iku tsumori desu.
**EN:** I plan to go by either train or bus.

## bakari — about, approximately
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a quantity + ばかり to express an approximate amount, similar to くらい.

**JP:** 三十分ばかり待ちました。
**Reading:** Sanjuppun bakari machimashita.
**EN:** I waited for about thirty minutes.

## bakari da — continue to (go in negative direction)
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + ばかりだ to describe a situation continuously worsening.

**JP:** 会社の業績は悪くなるばかりです。
**Reading:** Kaisha no gyouseki wa waruku naru bakari desu.
**EN:** The company's performance just keeps getting worse.

## bakari ka — not only... but also; as well as
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + ばかりか to add an even more surprising or extreme point on top of a stated fact.

**JP:** 彼は英語ばかりか、フランス語も話せます。
**Reading:** Kare wa eigo bakari ka, furansugo mo hanasemasu.
**EN:** He can speak not only English but also French.

## bakari ni — simply because; on account of (negative result)
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + ばかりに to attribute an unfortunate result to a single specific cause.

**JP:** 少し油断したばかりに、大失敗をしてしまいました。
**Reading:** Sukoshi yudan shita bakari ni, dai shippai o shite shimaimashita.
**EN:** Simply because I let my guard down a little, I ended up making a huge mistake.

## chinamini — by the way; in this connection; incidentally
**JLPT:** N2

A conjunction used to add related, supplementary information to what was just said.

**JP:** ちなみに、明日は休みです。
**Reading:** Chinamini, ashita wa yasumi desu.
**EN:** Incidentally, tomorrow is a day off.

## chitto mo~nai — (not) at all; (not) in the least
**JLPT:** N2

Pairs ちっとも with a negative predicate for emphatic denial, mostly in casual speech.

**JP:** この問題はちっとも分かりません。
**Reading:** Kono mondai wa chitto mo wakarimasen.
**EN:** I don't understand this problem at all.

## dake atte — being the case; precisely because; as expected from
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + だけあって to note that a result matches, and is explained by, an impressive fact.

**JP:** さすが有名なレストランだけあって、料理がおいしいです。
**Reading:** Sasuga yuumei na resutoran dake atte, ryouri ga oishii desu.
**EN:** As expected of a famous restaurant, the food is delicious.

## dake mashi da — it's better than; one should feel grateful for
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + だけましだ to say a situation, while not ideal, is better than a worse alternative.

**JP:** 遅刻したけど、来ただけましです。
**Reading:** Chikoku shita kedo, kita dake mashi desu.
**EN:** He was late, but it's better than not coming at all.

## dake ni — being the case; precisely because; as one would expect
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + だけに to explain a result as a natural consequence of a preceding fact.

**JP:** 長く準備しただけに、大成功でした。
**Reading:** Nagaku junbi shita dake ni, dai seikou deshita.
**EN:** Precisely because we prepared for a long time, it was a great success.

## dake no koto wa aru — no wonder; as expected of; not ... for nothing
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + だけのことはある to affirm that a result justifies the effort or reputation behind it.

**JP:** 三年間留学しただけのことはあって、日本語が上手です。
**Reading:** Sannenkan ryuugaku shita dake no koto wa atte, nihongo ga jouzu desu.
**EN:** No wonder his Japanese is good — he studied abroad for three years.

## dake wa — to do all that one can
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches after a noun/clause + だけは to emphasize doing at least the minimum expected action.

**JP:** 結果はどうであれ、努力するだけはしました。
**Reading:** Kekka wa dou de are, doryoku suru dake wa shimashita.
**EN:** Whatever the result, I at least did everything I could.

## datte — because; but; after all; even; too
**JLPT:** N2

A casual conjunction used to give an excuse or reason, or a particle meaning "even" attached to a noun.

**JP:** だって、時間がなかったんだもん。
**Reading:** Datte, jikan ga nakatta n damon.
**EN:** But there wasn't any time!

## de shika nai — merely; nothing but; no more than; there is only
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + でしかない to dismissively state that something amounts to no more than a limited category.

**JP:** それはただの言い訳でしかありません。
**Reading:** Sore wa tada no iiwake de shika arimasen.
**EN:** That is nothing but an excuse.

## dokoro dewa nai — not the time for; not the place for; far from; anything but
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + どころではない to say a situation makes some action completely inappropriate or impossible.

**JP:** 忙しくて旅行どころではありません。
**Reading:** Isogashikute ryokou dokoro dewa arimasen.
**EN:** I'm so busy that a trip is out of the question.

## dokoro ka — far from; anything but; let alone; not to mention; much less
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + どころか to strongly deny an expected outcome and replace it with a more extreme opposite.

**JP:** 貯金どころか、借金だらけです。
**Reading:** Chokin dokoro ka, shakkin darake desu.
**EN:** Far from having savings, I'm covered in debt.

## dou yara — possibly; apparently; seems like; somehow; barely
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb expressing a tentative judgment based on incomplete evidence.

**JP:** どうやら明日は雨が降るようです。
**Reading:** Dou yara ashita wa ame ga furu you desu.
**EN:** It seems like it's going to rain tomorrow.

## douse — anyhow; in any case; at any rate; after all; no matter what
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb expressing resignation that the outcome won't change regardless of effort.

**JP:** どうせ失敗するなら、挑戦してみます。
**Reading:** Douse shippai suru nara, chousen shite mimasu.
**EN:** If I'm going to fail anyway, I'll give it a try.

## enai — unable to; cannot; it is not possible to
**JLPT:** N2

A literary suffix attaching to the ます-stem of a verb + 得ない to state something is impossible.

**JP:** その結果には納得し得ません。
**Reading:** Sono kekka ni wa nattoku shi enai.
**EN:** I cannot accept that result.
