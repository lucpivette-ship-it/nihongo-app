# Grammar — N3 Batch 1 (20 points)

## ageru — to finish (doing something)
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + 上げる to describe completing a task, often one that took effort or time.

**JP:** 三日でレポートを書き上げました。
**Reading:** Mikka de repooto o kakiagemashita.
**EN:** I finished writing the report in three days.

## amari — so much that; (not) very
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb of degree; with a positive predicate it emphasizes an extreme result, with a negative predicate it softens to "not very."

**JP:** 嬉しさのあまり、泣いてしまいました。
**Reading:** Ureshisa no amari, naite shimaimashita.
**EN:** I was so happy that I ended up crying.

## amari ni mo — too much; so much that; excessively
**JLPT:** N3

A stronger, more emphatic version of あまり, used before an adjective or verb to stress an excessive degree.

**JP:** あまりにも忙しくて、休む時間もありません。
**Reading:** Amari ni mo isogashikute, yasumu jikan mo arimasen.
**EN:** I'm so busy that I don't even have time to rest.

## au — to do together; to do mutually
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + 合う to describe an action done reciprocally between two or more people.

**JP:** 二人はお互いに助け合っています。
**Reading:** Futari wa otagai ni tasukeatte imasu.
**EN:** The two of them help each other out.

## ba ii — should; it would be good if
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ば-conditional form + いい to suggest a desirable action or outcome.

**JP:** もっと早く起きればいいです。
**Reading:** Motto hayaku okireba ii desu.
**EN:** You should wake up earlier.

## ba yokatta — should have; would have been better if
**JLPT:** N3

The past form of ばいい, expressing regret about something that didn't happen.

**JP:** 傘を持ってくればよかった。
**Reading:** Kasa o motte kureba yokatta.
**EN:** I should have brought an umbrella.

## ba~hodo — the more... the more
**JLPT:** N3

Pairs the ば-conditional form of a verb/adjective with ほど to describe a proportional relationship.

**JP:** 練習すればするほど上手になります。
**Reading:** Renshuu sureba suru hodo jouzu ni narimasu.
**EN:** The more you practice, the better you get.

## ba~noni — would have; should have; if only
**JLPT:** N3

Combines ば with のに to express a hypothetical condition that didn't come true, often with regret or wishfulness.

**JP:** お金があれば、旅行に行けるのに。
**Reading:** Okane ga areba, ryokou ni ikeru noni.
**EN:** If only I had money, I could go on a trip.

## bakari de — only; just (negative nuance)
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun or verb + ばかりで to criticize someone for doing nothing but one thing.

**JP:** 彼は文句を言うばかりで、何もしません。
**Reading:** Kare wa monku o iu bakari de, nani mo shimasen.
**EN:** He does nothing but complain.

## bakari denaku — not only... but also
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/clause + ばかりでなく to add an additional point, similar to だけでなく.

**JP:** 彼は英語ばかりでなく、フランス語も話せます。
**Reading:** Kare wa eigo bakari denaku, furansugo mo hanasemasu.
**EN:** He can speak not only English but also French.

## beki da — should do; must do
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + べきだ to express a strong moral obligation or recommendation.

**JP:** 約束は守るべきです。
**Reading:** Yakusoku wa mamoru beki desu.
**EN:** You should keep your promises.

## beki dewa nai — should not do; must not do
**JLPT:** N3

The negative form of べきだ, expressing that something ought not to be done.

**JP:** 人の悪口を言うべきではありません。
**Reading:** Hito no waruguchi o iu beki dewa arimasen.
**EN:** You shouldn't speak ill of others.

## betsu ni~nai — not really; not particularly
**JLPT:** N3

Pairs 別に with a negative predicate to downplay or dismiss something casually.

**JP:** 別に怒っていません。
**Reading:** Betsu ni okotte imasen.
**EN:** I'm not particularly angry.

## buri ni — for the first time in (period of time)
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches after a time expression + ぶりに to describe something happening again after a long interval.

**JP:** 三年ぶりに国に帰りました。
**Reading:** San-nen buri ni kuni ni kaerimashita.
**EN:** I returned to my home country for the first time in three years.

## chuu / juu — during; throughout; currently
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches after a noun to mean either "in the middle of" (read ちゅう) or "throughout the entire" (read じゅう) depending on context.

**JP:** 今、会議中です。
**Reading:** Ima, kaigichuu desu.
**EN:** I'm in a meeting right now.

## dake — as much as
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a quantity or clause + だけ to describe doing something to the fullest extent possible.

**JP:** 好きなだけ食べてください。
**Reading:** Suki na dake tabete kudasai.
**EN:** Please eat as much as you like.

## dake de naku — not only... but also
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/clause + だけでなく to add an extra point on top of what's already mentioned.

**JP:** この店は安いだけでなく、味もいいです。
**Reading:** Kono mise wa yasui dake denaku, aji mo ii desu.
**EN:** This shop isn't just cheap, the taste is good too.

## dakedo — but; however; although
**JLPT:** N3

A casual conjunction contrasting a preceding statement, similar to だけれども.

**JP:** 行きたい。だけど、お金がない。
**Reading:** Ikitai. Dakedo, okane ga nai.
**EN:** I want to go. But I don't have money.

## darake — full of; covered with (something undesirable)
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + だらけ to describe something covered in or full of an unwanted thing.

**JP:** 部屋はほこりだらけです。
**Reading:** Heya wa hokori darake desu.
**EN:** The room is covered in dust.

## donna ni~temo — no matter how (much)
**JLPT:** N3

Pairs どんなに with a ても-form clause to express that no degree of something changes the outcome.

**JP:** どんなに頑張っても、うまくいきません。
**Reading:** Donna ni ganbattemo, umaku ikimasen.
**EN:** No matter how hard I try, it doesn't go well.
