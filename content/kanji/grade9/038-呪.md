# 呪 — curse, spell

**Strokes:** 8
**On-yomi (音読み):** ju
**Kun-yomi (訓読み):** noro.u

## Example: On-yomi (ju)
- **JP:** 魔法使いが呪文を唱えた。

- **EN:** She is cursing him.
