# けど — but / however (casual)

**JLPT level:** N5

Casual conjunction meaning "but" or "although," attached to the end of a clause. More common in speech than けれども.

## Example
- **JP:** 高いけど、買います。
- **Reading:** takai kedo, kaimasu.
- **EN:** It's expensive, but I'll buy it.
