# も — also / too particle

**JLPT level:** N5

Replaces は or を/が to mean "also," "too," or (with negative verbs) "neither." Attaches directly to a noun.

## Example
- **JP:** 私も学生です。
- **Reading:** watashi mo gakusei desu.
- **EN:** I am also a student.
