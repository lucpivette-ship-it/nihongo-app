# Grammar — N3 Batch 5 (20 points)

## ni shitagatte — as; therefore; according to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/clause + にしたがって to describe a change that happens proportionally, or to indicate following a rule.

**JP:** 時間が経つにしたがって、痛みが和らぎました。
**Reading:** Jikan ga tatsu ni shitagatte, itami ga yawaragimashita.
**EN:** As time passed, the pain eased.

## ni shite mo — even if; even though
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/clause + にしても to concede a point while still asserting a claim.

**JP:** 忙しいにしても、返事ぐらいはできるはずです。
**Reading:** Isogashii ni shitemo, henji gurai wa dekiru hazu desu.
**EN:** Even if you're busy, you should at least be able to reply.

## ni shite wa — for; considering
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + にしては to note that something is surprising given a stated standard.

**JP:** 初心者にしては上手です。
**Reading:** Shoshinsha ni shite wa jouzu desu.
**EN:** For a beginner, you're quite good.

## ni taishite — towards; against; in contrast with
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + に対して to mark the target of an action, attitude, or comparison.

**JP:** 彼は先生に対して失礼な態度を取りました。
**Reading:** Kare wa sensei ni taishite shitsurei na taido o torimashita.
**EN:** He behaved rudely towards the teacher.

## ni totte — to; for; as far as ... is concerned
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun (usually a person) + にとって to establish a perspective from which a statement holds true.

**JP:** 私にとって、家族が一番大切です。
**Reading:** Watashi ni totte, kazoku ga ichiban taisetsu desu.
**EN:** For me, family is the most important thing.

## ni tsuite — concerning; regarding; about
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + について to introduce the topic being discussed.

**JP:** 日本の歴史について調べています。
**Reading:** Nihon no rekishi ni tsuite shirabete imasu.
**EN:** I'm researching Japanese history.

## ni tsurete — as; in proportion to; with
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/clause + につれて to describe two changes happening together over time.

**JP:** 年を取るにつれて、体力が落ちます。
**Reading:** Toshi o toru ni tsurete, tairyoku ga ochimasu.
**EN:** As one gets older, physical strength declines.

## ni wa — for the purpose of; in order to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + には to introduce what's required to achieve a goal.

**JP:** 上手になるには、練習が必要です。
**Reading:** Jouzu ni naru ni wa, renshuu ga hitsuyou desu.
**EN:** In order to get good at it, practice is necessary.

## ni yoru to / ni yoreba — according to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun (often a source of information) + によると/によれば to cite where information comes from.

**JP:** 天気予報によると、明日は晴れるそうです。
**Reading:** Tenki yohou ni yoru to, ashita wa hareru sou desu.
**EN:** According to the weather forecast, it'll be sunny tomorrow.

## ni yotte / ni yoru — by means of; due to; because of
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + によって/による to mark a cause, method, or agent of an action, especially in passive sentences.

**JP:** 地震によって多くの建物が壊れました。
**Reading:** Jishin ni yotte ooku no tatemono ga kowaremashita.
**EN:** Many buildings were destroyed due to the earthquake.

## no deshou ka — I wonder; asking politely
**JLPT:** N3

A polite, softened way to ask a question, adding a nuance of genuine curiosity or hesitation.

**JP:** これはどうやって使うのでしょうか。
**Reading:** Kore wa dou yatte tsukau no deshou ka.
**EN:** I wonder how this is used?

## o chuushin ni — focused on; centered on
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + を中心に to describe something arranged or organized around a central point.

**JP:** 東京を中心に活動しています。
**Reading:** Toukyou o chuushin ni katsudou shite imasu.
**EN:** I'm based (centered) in Tokyo for my activities.

## o hajime — for example; starting with
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + をはじめ to introduce a representative example within a larger group.

**JP:** 社長をはじめ、全社員が参加しました。
**Reading:** Shachou o hajime, zenshain ga sanka shimashita.
**EN:** Starting with the president, all employees participated.

## o komete — filled with; full of
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun (often an emotion) + を込めて to describe doing something with strong feeling behind it.

**JP:** 心を込めて手紙を書きました。
**Reading:** Kokoro o komete tegami o kakimashita.
**EN:** I wrote the letter with all my heart.

## o tsuujite / o tooshite — through; via; throughout
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + を通じて/を通して to describe a means, channel, or period through which something happens.

**JP:** 友達を通じて、彼女に出会いました。
**Reading:** Tomodachi o tsuujite, kanojo ni deaimashita.
**EN:** I met her through a friend.

## okage de — thanks to; owing to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/clause + おかげで to attribute a positive outcome to someone or something's help.

**JP:** 先生のおかげで、試験に合格しました。
**Reading:** Sensei no okage de, shiken ni goukaku shimashita.
**EN:** Thanks to my teacher, I passed the exam.

## ppanashi — leaving (something) on/in use
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + っぱなし to criticize leaving something in an ongoing, often careless, state.

**JP:** 電気をつけっぱなしにしないでください。
**Reading:** Denki o tsukeppanashi ni shinaide kudasai.
**EN:** Please don't leave the lights on.

## ppoi — -ish; seems like; easily does
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun, adjective stem, or verb stem + っぽい to describe a resemblance or tendency toward a quality.

**JP:** 彼は子供っぽいです。
**Reading:** Kare wa kodomoppoi desu.
**EN:** He's childish (kid-like).

## sae — even; so much as
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + さえ to give an extreme example implying "even this basic thing," often with negatives.

**JP:** 彼は挨拶さえしませんでした。
**Reading:** Kare wa aisatsu sae shimasen deshita.
**EN:** He didn't even say hello.

## sae~ba — if only; as long as
**JLPT:** N3

Pairs さえ with a ば-conditional to say that one condition alone is sufficient for an outcome.

**JP:** これさえあれば大丈夫です。
**Reading:** Kore sae areba daijoubu desu.
**EN:** As long as I have this, I'll be fine.
