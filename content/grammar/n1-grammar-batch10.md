# Grammar — N1 Batch 10 (20 points)

## tara~tade — if / in the case... of course / should
**JLPT:** N1

Repeats たら after the same verb in different forms to describe accepting whichever outcome occurs, adapting either way.

**JP:** お金があったらあったで、悩みは尽きない。
**Reading:** Okane ga attara atta de, nayami wa tsukinai.
**EN:** Even if you have money, the worries never end (there's always something).

## tara~tokoro da — if... (counterfactual condition), then would be
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a hypothetical past-conditional clause + ところだ to describe an outcome that would have occurred under different circumstances.

**JP:** もう少し遅かったら、事故になっていたところだ。
**Reading:** Mou sukoshi osokattara, jiko ni natte ita tokoro da.
**EN:** If it had been just a little later, it would have resulted in an accident.

## tari tomo — not even; not any
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a counter (usually "one") + たりとも with a negative predicate to emphatically deny even a single instance.

**JP:** 一瞬たりとも気を抜けない。
**Reading:** Isshun tari tomo ki o nukenai.
**EN:** I can't let my guard down even for a single moment.

## taru mono / taru — (those) who are; (that) which is; in the capacity of ... should
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + たるもの to describe the ideal standard expected of someone in a given role or position.

**JP:** リーダーたるもの、責任を持つべきだ。
**Reading:** Riidaa taru mono, sekinin o motsu beki da.
**EN:** Anyone who is a leader should take responsibility.

## te kanawanai — can't bear to; unable to; troublesome to; can't stand to
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the て-form of an adjective/verb + 敵わない to express that a feeling or sensation is unbearably intense.

**JP:** 暑くて敵わない。
**Reading:** Atsukute kanawanai.
**EN:** It's unbearably hot.

## te kara to iu mono — ever since
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + からというもの to mark a point after which a new, ongoing state began.

**JP:** 結婚してからというもの、生活が一変した。
**Reading:** Kekkon shite kara to iu mono, seikatsu ga ippen shita.
**EN:** Ever since I got married, my life has completely changed.

## te miseru — I'll do my best; I'll definitely do; I'll show you
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + みせる to express strong determination to accomplish something, proving it to others.

**JP:** 必ず合格してみせる。
**Reading:** Kanarazu goukaku shite miseru.
**EN:** I will definitely pass, just watch.

## te shikaru beki da — should; appropriate; it is natural to do
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the て-form of a verb/adjective + しかるべきだ to state that a certain response is the fitting, appropriate one.

**JP:** 努力は評価されてしかるべきだ。
**Reading:** Doryoku wa hyouka sarete shikaru beki da.
**EN:** Effort should rightfully be recognized.

## te sumu koto dewa nai — ~ is not enough to solve the problem; cannot be resolved by
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + 済むことではない to state that a minimal response is insufficient to resolve a serious matter.

**JP:** 謝って済むことではない。
**Reading:** Ayamatte sumu koto dewa nai.
**EN:** This isn't something that can be resolved just by apologizing.

## te yamanai — always; never stop; can't help but
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + やまない to describe a strong feeling that continues without end, formal/literary register.

**JP:** ご健康とご多幸を願ってやみません。
**Reading:** Gokenkou to gotakou o negatte yamimasen.
**EN:** I sincerely and continuously wish you health and happiness.

## temae — considering; before; in front of; one's standpoint
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + 手前 to describe an action or feeling constrained by concern for how it looks to someone.

**JP:** 部下の手前、弱音は吐けない。
**Reading:** Buka no temae, yowane wa hakenai.
**EN:** With my subordinates watching, I can't show weakness.

## temo dou ni mo naranai — it's no use; can't do anything
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + もどうにもならない to state that an action would be completely futile.

**JP:** 今さら焦ってもどうにもならない。
**Reading:** Ima sara asette mo dou ni mo naranai.
**EN:** There's no use panicking about it now.

## temo sashitsukaenai — can ~; it's okay if ~ (indicates permission or compromise)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + も差し支えない to grant formal permission, stating no problem would result.

**JP:** ここに座っても差し支えございません。
**Reading:** Koko ni suwattemo sashitsukae gozaimasen.
**EN:** It's perfectly fine if you sit here.

## temo shiranai — if (you) continue... you'll end up / I don't care about it
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + も知らない as a warning about a bad consequence that will follow if a behavior continues.

**JP:** 無理をしても知らないよ。
**Reading:** Muri o shitemo shiranai yo.
**EN:** If you push yourself too hard, don't say I didn't warn you.

## to aimatte — together with..., more; because of the influence of..., more
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + と相まって to describe two factors combining to intensify an overall effect.

**JP:** 円安と相まって、輸出が伸びた。
**Reading:** Enyasu to aimatte, yushutsu ga nobita.
**EN:** Combined with the weak yen, exports increased.

## to areba — if it is the case that; if
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun/clause + とあれば to state that given a certain special circumstance, a strong action naturally follows.

**JP:** 子供のためとあれば、何でもする。
**Reading:** Kodomo no tame to areba, nan demo suru.
**EN:** If it's for my child, I'll do anything.

## to atte — due to the fact that; because of
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun/clause + とあって to give a reason for a notable, often crowded or lively, situation.

**JP:** 連休とあって、道路は混雑していた。
**Reading:** Renkyuu to atte, douro wa konzatsu shite ita.
**EN:** Because it was a long holiday, the roads were congested.

## to bakari ni — as if to say; as though
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a quoted phrase + とばかりに to describe an action performed as if silently declaring the quoted sentiment.

**JP:** 待っていましたとばかりに、彼は駆け寄ってきた。
**Reading:** Matte imashita to bakari ni, kare wa kakeyotte kita.
**EN:** As if to say "I've been waiting for this," he came rushing over.

## to ie domo — even if; even though; despite
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a plain-form clause + といえども to concede a point formally while asserting it doesn't change the conclusion.

**JP:** 子供といえども、責任がある。
**Reading:** Kodomo to iedomo, sekinin ga aru.
**EN:** Even though he's a child, he still has responsibility.

## to ienaku mo nai — one can even say that; it can also be said that
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a plain-form clause + と言えなくもない to hedge a statement as a plausible, if understated, description.

**JP:** これは奇跡と言えなくもない。
**Reading:** Kore wa kiseki to ienaku mo nai.
**EN:** You could even call this a miracle.
