# Grammar — N2 Batch 6 (20 points)

## ni sakidachi — before; prior to
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/verb + に先立ち to describe an action taken before a more significant upcoming event, in formal contexts.

**JP:** 開店に先立ち、内覧会が行われました。
**Reading:** Kaiten ni sakidachi, nairankai ga okonawaremashita.
**EN:** Prior to the opening, a preview event was held.

## ni seyo/ ni shiro — even if; regardless; whether... or
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a plain-form clause + にせよ/にしろ to concede a point while asserting it doesn't change the overall conclusion.

**JP:** 冗談にせよ、それは言い過ぎです。
**Reading:** Joudan ni seyo, sore wa iisugi desu.
**EN:** Even if it was a joke, that's going too far.

## ni shiro~ni shiro — whether… or
**JLPT:** N2

Repeats にしろ after two contrasting options to say a conclusion holds regardless of which applies.

**JP:** 行くにしろ行かないにしろ、連絡してください。
**Reading:** Iku ni shiro ikanai ni shiro, renraku shite kudasai.
**EN:** Whether you go or not, please let me know.

## ni shitara / ni sureba — from one's perspective; from the point of view of
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun (usually a person) + にしたら/にすれば to introduce a judgment made from that person's standpoint.

**JP:** 子供にしたら、それは大きな問題です。
**Reading:** Kodomo ni shitara, sore wa ookina mondai desu.
**EN:** From a child's perspective, that's a big problem.

## ni shitemo~ni shitemo — regardless of whether
**JLPT:** N2

Repeats にしても after two contrasting options to state the outcome is the same either way.

**JP:** 賛成するにしても反対するにしても、理由が必要です。
**Reading:** Sansei suru ni shitemo hantai suru ni shitemo, riyuu ga hitsuyou desu.
**EN:** Whether you agree or disagree, you need a reason.

## ni sotte — along with; in accordance with
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + に沿って to describe following a set path, plan, or set of guidelines closely.

**JP:** マニュアルに沿って作業を進めます。
**Reading:** Manyuaru ni sotte sagyou o susumemasu.
**EN:** We proceed with the work in accordance with the manual.

## ni soui nai — without a doubt; certain; sure
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a plain-form clause + に相違ない to express formal, confident certainty about a conclusion.

**JP:** この筆跡は彼のものに相違ありません。
**Reading:** Kono hisseki wa kare no mono ni soui arimasen.
**EN:** This handwriting is without a doubt his.

## ni suginai — no more than; just; merely; only
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + に過ぎない to dismissively limit the significance of a fact.

**JP:** これはほんの一例に過ぎません。
**Reading:** Kore wa hon no ichirei ni suginai.
**EN:** This is merely one example.

## ni tomonatte — as; due to; with; along with; following; accordingly
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + に伴って to describe a change that occurs in step with another change.

**JP:** 人口の増加に伴って、住宅不足が深刻化しています。
**Reading:** Jinkou no zouka ni tomonatte, juutaku busoku ga shinkokuka shite imasu.
**EN:** Along with population growth, the housing shortage is becoming more serious.

## ni tsuke — every time; whenever; as; whether; in (either case)
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a verb + につけ to describe a recurring reaction triggered every time a certain situation arises.

**JP:** この歌を聞くにつけ、昔を思い出します。
**Reading:** Kono uta o kiku ni tsuke, mukashi o omoidashimasu.
**EN:** Every time I hear this song, it reminds me of the old days.

## ni tsuki — due to; because of; per; each
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + につき to state a reason (formal) or a per-unit rate.

**JP:** 一人につき千円かかります。
**Reading:** Hitori ni tsuki sen'en kakarimasu.
**EN:** It costs a thousand yen per person.

## ni watatte — throughout; over a period of
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun (usually a span of time or place) + にわたって to describe something extending broadly across it.

**JP:** 三年間にわたって、この研究を続けています。
**Reading:** Sannenkan ni watatte, kono kenkyuu o tsuzukete imasu.
**EN:** I've continued this research over a period of three years.

## nimo kakawarazu — despite; in spite of; nevertheless; although
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a plain-form clause + にも関わらず to introduce a surprising or contrasting result.

**JP:** 雨にもかかわらず、多くの人が集まりました。
**Reading:** Ame ni mo kakawarazu, ooku no hito ga atsumarimashita.
**EN:** Despite the rain, many people gathered.

## nite — in, at, with, by (formal particle)
**JLPT:** N2

A formal, written equivalent of で marking location, means, or cause, used in official documents and announcements.

**JP:** 会議は本社にて行われます。
**Reading:** Kaigi wa honsha nite okonawaremasu.
**EN:** The meeting will be held at the head office.

## no mo motto mo da — no wonder; …is only natural
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a plain-form clause + のももっともだ to affirm that a reaction or conclusion is completely reasonable.

**JP:** 彼が怒るのももっともです。
**Reading:** Kare ga okoru no mo mottomo desu.
**EN:** It's only natural that he's angry.

## no moto de — under; with
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + の下で to describe an action happening under someone's guidance, authority, or a given condition.

**JP:** 先生の指導の下で、研究を進めました。
**Reading:** Sensei no shidou no moto de, kenkyuu o susumemashita.
**EN:** I carried out the research under my professor's guidance.

## no ue de wa — according to; from the viewpoint of
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + の上では to specify a formal or technical standpoint from which a statement is true.

**JP:** 数字の上では、景気は回復しています。
**Reading:** Suuji no ue de wa, keiki wa kaifuku shite imasu.
**EN:** According to the numbers, the economy is recovering.

## nomi — only; nothing but
**JLPT:** N2

A formal, written equivalent of だけ attaching to a noun + のみ, meaning "only."

**JP:** 成功への道はただ一つ、努力あるのみです。
**Reading:** Seikou e no michi wa tada hitotsu, doryoku aru nomi desu.
**EN:** There's only one road to success: effort, and nothing else.

## nominarazu — not only; besides; as well as
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + のみならず to add an even broader point beyond what was already mentioned, formal register.

**JP:** 彼は日本のみならず、世界的にも有名です。
**Reading:** Kare wa nihon nominarazu, sekaiteki ni mo yuumei desu.
**EN:** He is famous not only in Japan, but around the world.

## nu — negative verb conjugation; traditional version of ない
**JLPT:** N2

A classical negative suffix attaching to the negative stem of a verb, still seen in set phrases and formal/literary writing.

**JP:** 予期せぬ出来事が起こりました。
**Reading:** Yoki senu dekigoto ga okorimashita.
**EN:** An unexpected event occurred.
