# Grammar — N1 Batch 1 (20 points)

## aete — dare to; daringly; deliberately; purposely
**JLPT:** N1

An adverb marking an action taken on purpose despite difficulty, risk, or being unnecessary — often to make a point.

**JP:** あえて厳しいことを言わせてもらいます。
**Reading:** Aete kibishii koto o iwasete moraimasu.
**EN:** I'll dare to say something harsh.

## akumade mo — to the end; persistently; absolutely; is still very
**JLPT:** N1

An adverb emphasizing that a stance or state is maintained firmly and without wavering, to the very end.

**JP:** 彼はあくまでも自分の意見を主張した。
**Reading:** Kare wa akumade mo jibun no iken o shuchou shita.
**EN:** He persistently insisted on his own opinion.

## an no jou — just as one thought; as usual; sure enough
**JLPT:** N1

An adverbial phrase confirming that an anticipated (often negative) outcome occurred exactly as predicted.

**JP:** 案の定、電車は遅れた。
**Reading:** An no jou, densha wa okureta.
**EN:** Sure enough, the train was late.

## arakajime — beforehand; in advance; previously
**JLPT:** N1

A formal adverb describing preparation completed prior to some event.

**JP:** あらかじめ予約しておいてください。
**Reading:** Arakajime yoyaku shite oite kudasai.
**EN:** Please make a reservation in advance.

## atte no — which can exist solely due to the presence of
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + あっての to assert that something only exists or has value because of a prior condition.

**JP:** お客様あっての商売です。
**Reading:** Okyakusama atte no shoubai desu.
**EN:** It's a business that only exists because of its customers.

## ba koso — only because
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the conditional ば form + こそ to emphasize that something is true precisely and only for the stated reason.

**JP:** 愛すればこそ、厳しく叱るのです。
**Reading:** Aisureba koso, kibishiku shikaru no desu.
**EN:** It's precisely because I love him that I scold him strictly.

## ba sore made da / tara sore made da — if… then it's over
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a conditional clause + それまでだ to state that if a certain thing happens, everything ends there — nothing more to be done.

**JP:** 死んでしまえばそれまでだ。
**Reading:** Shinde shimaeba sore made da.
**EN:** If you die, that's the end of everything.

## bekarazu / bekarazaru — must not; should not; do not
**JLPT:** N1

A classical negative imperative suffix attaching to the dictionary form of a verb, seen on formal signs and notices.

**JP:** 芝生に入るべからず。
**Reading:** Shibafu ni hairu bekarazu.
**EN:** Do not enter the lawn.

## beku — in order to; for the purpose of
**JLPT:** N1

A literary suffix attaching to the dictionary form of a verb + べく to state the purpose behind an action, formal register.

**JP:** 目標を達成すべく、努力を続けた。
**Reading:** Mokuhyou o tassei subeku, doryoku o tsuzuketa.
**EN:** In order to achieve the goal, he kept up his efforts.

## beku mo nai — cannot possibly be
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + べくもない to state that something is utterly impossible, formal/literary register.

**JP:** 昔の生活には戻るべくもない。
**Reading:** Mukashi no seikatsu ni wa modoru beku mo nai.
**EN:** There's no way we can possibly return to the old way of life.

## beku shite — as it is bound to (happen); following the natural course
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + べくして to describe an outcome that occurred as the inevitable, natural result of preceding causes.

**JP:** 彼の成功は成功すべくして成功したものだ。
**Reading:** Kare no seikou wa seikou subeku shite seikou shita mono da.
**EN:** His success was bound to happen — it was inevitable.

## biru / bite / bita — to seem to be; to appear; to behave as
**JLPT:** N1

A literary verb-forming suffix attaching to a noun stem to describe taking on the appearance or manner of something.

**JP:** 大人びた態度で話す。
**Reading:** Otonabita taido de hanasu.
**EN:** She speaks with a mature, adult-like manner.

## buri / ppuri — style; manner; way
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun or verb stem + ぶり/っぷり to describe the particular manner or style in which something is done.

**JP:** 彼の話しぶりはとても丁寧だ。
**Reading:** Kare no hanashiburi wa totemo teinei da.
**EN:** His manner of speaking is very polite.

## buru / butte / butta — assuming the air of; behaving like; to pretend / act like
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun stem + ぶる to describe affecting or putting on an air of being a certain way.

**JP:** 彼は偉ぶっている。
**Reading:** Kare wa erabutte iru.
**EN:** He's acting like he's important.

## dani / dani shinai — even; not even
**JLPT:** N1

A formal emphatic particle attaching to a noun, used with a negative predicate to stress that not even the smallest instance occurs.

**JP:** 想像だにしなかった出来事だ。
**Reading:** Souzou dani shinakatta dekigoto da.
**EN:** It was an event I hadn't even imagined.

## dano~dano — and; and the like; and so forth
**JLPT:** N1

Repeats だの after listed items to give an informal, somewhat critical-sounding list of examples.

**JP:** 高いだの、まずいだの、文句ばかりだ。
**Reading:** Takai dano, mazui dano, monku bakari da.
**EN:** All he does is complain — it's too expensive, it tastes bad, and so on.

## darou ni — surely..., but; although... is likely, ~; should have (regret)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a plain-form clause + だろうに to express a hypothetical regret or contrast: something surely would have been true, but wasn't.

**JP:** もっと早く言ってくれれば良かっただろうに。
**Reading:** Motto hayaku itte kurereba yokatta darou ni.
**EN:** You should have told me sooner (implying regret that you didn't).

## de are / de arou to — whoever; whatever; however; even
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + であれ/であろうと to concede that regardless of the specific case, the conclusion still holds.

**JP:** どんな理由であれ、暴力は許されない。
**Reading:** Donna riyuu de are, bouryoku wa yurusarenai.
**EN:** Whatever the reason, violence is not permitted.

## de are~de are — whether [A] or [B]
**JLPT:** N1

Repeats であれ after two contrasting nouns to state that the conclusion applies regardless of which case is true.

**JP:** 男であれ女であれ、平等に扱われるべきだ。
**Reading:** Otoko de are onna de are, byoudou ni atsukawareru beki da.
**EN:** Whether male or female, everyone should be treated equally.

## demo ari~demo aru — to also be; both… and
**JLPT:** N1

Repeats でもある after two nouns/adjectives to state that a subject fulfills both descriptions simultaneously.

**JP:** 彼は先生でもあり、友人でもある。
**Reading:** Kare wa sensei demo ari, yuujin demo aru.
**EN:** He is both my teacher and my friend.
