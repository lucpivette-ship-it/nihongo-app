# Grammar — N1 Batch 9 (20 points)

## omoi o suru — to think; to feel
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to an adjective + 思いをする to describe personally experiencing a particular feeling or state.

**JP:** 恥ずかしい思いをした。
**Reading:** Hazukashii omoi o shita.
**EN:** I felt embarrassed.

## ori ni — when; at the time; on the occasion
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun/clause + 折に to mark a formal, fitting occasion for an action.

**JP:** 上京の折に、ぜひお立ち寄りください。
**Reading:** Joukyou no ori ni, zehi otachiyori kudasai.
**EN:** Please do drop by when you come up to Tokyo.

## oyoso — about; roughly; generally; approximately; completely
**JLPT:** N1

A formal adverb estimating an approximate amount, or with a negative, meaning "not at all."

**JP:** およそ百人が参加した。
**Reading:** Oyoso hyaku-nin ga sanka shita.
**EN:** Roughly a hundred people participated.

## sa — ending particle; indicates assertion
**JLPT:** N1

A casual sentence-final particle asserting a point plainly, often in a slightly nonchalant or emphatic tone.

**JP:** そんなの簡単さ。
**Reading:** Sonna no kantan sa.
**EN:** That's easy, you know.

## samo — really (seem, appear, etc.); truly; as if
**JLPT:** N1

An adverb intensifying a comparison, often with かのように, to describe something appearing very convincingly a certain way.

**JP:** さも本当のことのように話す。
**Reading:** Samo hontou no koto no you ni hanasu.
**EN:** He talks as if it were really true.

## samonaito — otherwise; or else; if not
**JLPT:** N1

A conjunction warning of a negative consequence if a preceding instruction isn't followed.

**JP:** 急ぎなさい、さもないと遅刻するよ。
**Reading:** Isoginasai, samo naito chikoku suru yo.
**EN:** Hurry up, or else you'll be late.

## sazo — surely; certainly; no doubt; indeed
**JLPT:** N1

A formal adverb expressing confident empathetic speculation about someone else's feelings.

**JP:** さぞお疲れでしょう。
**Reading:** Sazo otsukare deshou.
**EN:** You must be very tired indeed.

## shimatsu da — end up; in the end; as a result (negative)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a clause + 始末だ to describe reaching an unfortunate, often self-caused, conclusion.

**JP:** 挙句の果てに、喧嘩する始末だ。
**Reading:** Ageku no hate ni, kenka suru shimatsu da.
**EN:** And in the end, it resulted in a fight.

## soba kara — as soon as; right after
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + そばから to describe a second action repeatedly undoing or following right after the first.

**JP:** 覚えるそばから忘れてしまう。
**Reading:** Oboeru soba kara wasurete shimau.
**EN:** I forget it just as soon as I learn it.

## sobireru — to miss a chance; to fail to do
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + そびれる to describe missing the opportunity to do something intended.

**JP:** 忙しくて、電話をかけそびれた。
**Reading:** Isogashikute, denwa o kakesobireta.
**EN:** I was busy and missed the chance to call.

## sokonau / sokoneru / sonjiru — to do something wrong; by mistake; to miss a chance; to fail to
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + 損なう to describe failing at an action or narrowly missing doing it.

**JP:** 大事な書類を見損なった。
**Reading:** Daiji na shorui o misokonatta.
**EN:** I failed to notice the important document.

## sube ga nai — there is no way / means; cannot do
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + 術がない to state that there's no available method to accomplish something.

**JP:** どうする術もない。
**Reading:** Dou suru sube mo nai.
**EN:** There's nothing I can do about it.

## sura / de sura — even ~ (with emphasis)
**JLPT:** N1

A formal emphatic particle attaching to a noun to state that even the most surprising case applies.

**JP:** 専門家ですら分からない問題だ。
**Reading:** Senmonka de sura wakaranai mondai da.
**EN:** It's a problem that even experts can't figure out.

## ta hazumi ni / ta hyoushi ni — the moment [A], unintentionally / inadvertently caused something to happen
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the past-tense form of a verb + はずみに to describe an unintended consequence triggered by a sudden movement or event.

**JP:** 転んだ拍子に、足を捻挫した。
**Reading:** Koronda hyoushi ni, ashi o nenza shita.
**EN:** I sprained my ankle the moment I fell.

## ta koto ni suru / ta koto ni naru — pretend to; see things as untrue; contrary to the truth
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the past-tense form of a verb + ことにする to describe deliberately treating a false premise as if it were true.

**JP:** 見なかったことにしよう。
**Reading:** Minakatta koto ni shiyou.
**EN:** Let's pretend we didn't see that.

## ta tokoro de — even if; no matter (who, what, when, where, why, how)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the past-tense form of a verb + ところで to concede a hypothetical action while denying it would change the outcome.

**JP:** 今さら謝ったところで、許されない。
**Reading:** Ima sara ayamatta tokoro de, yurusarenai.
**EN:** Even if you apologize now, you won't be forgiven.

## ta tsumori wa nai — have no intention to / didn't mean to
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the past-tense form of a verb + つもりはない to deny that a certain intention was ever held.

**JP:** そんなつもりで言ったつもりはない。
**Reading:** Sonna tsumori de itta tsumori wa nai.
**EN:** I didn't mean it that way.

## tada~nomi da — all we can do is; can do nothing but; only
**JLPT:** N1

Pairs ただ with のみだ to state that a single, limited course of action is all that remains.

**JP:** あとはただ結果を待つのみだ。
**Reading:** Ato wa tada kekka o matsu nomi da.
**EN:** All that's left now is to wait for the result.

## tameshi ga nai — is never the case; has never happened; I have never seen or heard of
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the past-tense form of a verb + ためしがない to state that something has never once occurred, based on experience.

**JP:** 彼が時間を守ったためしがない。
**Reading:** Kare ga jikan o mamotta tameshi ga nai.
**EN:** He has never once been on time.

## tara saigo / taga saigo — if you do... negative result
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the past-tense form of a verb + ら最後 to describe an irreversible negative consequence once a certain action occurs.

**JP:** 彼に借りたら最後、なかなか返してくれない。
**Reading:** Kare ni karitara saigo, nakanaka kaeshite kurenai.
**EN:** Once you lend him something, he won't easily give it back.
