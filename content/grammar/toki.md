# とき — when / at the time of

**JLPT level:** N5

Attaches after a verb, adjective, or noun + の to mean "when ~" or "at the time that ~."

## Example
- **JP:** 日本に行くとき、パスポートが必要です。
- **Reading:** nihon ni iku toki, pasupooto ga hitsuyou desu.
- **EN:** When you go to Japan, you need a passport.
