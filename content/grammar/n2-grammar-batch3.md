# Grammar — N2 Batch 3 (20 points)

## ka~nai ka no uchi ni — just as; right after; as soon as
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a verb repeated in affirmative and negative form + かないかのうちに to describe two events happening in near-instant succession.

**JP:** 電車のドアが閉まるか閉まらないかのうちに、走り出しました。
**Reading:** Densha no doa ga shimaru ka shimaranai ka no uchi ni, hashiridashimashita.
**EN:** The train started moving just as the doors were closing.

## kaette — on the contrary; rather; all the more; surprisingly
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb marking a result that is the reverse of what would normally be expected.

**JP:** 薬を飲んだら、かえって具合が悪くなりました。
**Reading:** Kusuri o nondara, kaette guai ga waruku narimashita.
**EN:** After taking the medicine, I actually felt worse instead.

## kagiri — as long as; while… is the case; as far as; while
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + 限り to set a condition or extent within which a statement holds true.

**JP:** 私が知る限り、彼は嘘をつきません。
**Reading:** Watashi ga shiru kagiri, kare wa uso o tsukimasen.
**EN:** As far as I know, he doesn't lie.

## kai ga aru — it's worth one's efforts to do something
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + 甲斐がある to say that an effort or experience was rewarding.

**JP:** 一生懸命練習した甲斐がありました。
**Reading:** Isshoukenmei renshuu shita kai ga arimashita.
**EN:** It was worth all the hard practice.

## kanenai — (someone) might do something; something might happen
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + かねない to express concern that an undesirable outcome could occur.

**JP:** そんな運転をしたら、事故を起こしかねません。
**Reading:** Sonna unten o shitara, jiko o okoshikanemasen.
**EN:** Driving like that, you might well cause an accident.

## kaneru — unable to do something; can't do something
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + かねる to politely state an inability to do something, often in formal contexts.

**JP:** その質問にはお答えしかねます。
**Reading:** Sono shitsumon ni wa okotae shikanemasu.
**EN:** I'm afraid I cannot answer that question.

## kara iu to — in terms of; from the point of view of
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + から言うと to introduce a judgment based on a particular standpoint.

**JP:** 経験から言うと、この方法が一番効果的です。
**Reading:** Keiken kara iu to, kono houhou ga ichiban koukateki desu.
**EN:** Speaking from experience, this method is the most effective.

## kara koso — precisely because
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + からこそ to emphasize that a specific reason, and only that reason, explains a result.

**JP:** あなたのことを思うからこそ、厳しいことを言います。
**Reading:** Anata no koto o omou kara koso, kibishii koto o iimasu.
**EN:** It's precisely because I care about you that I'm saying something harsh.

## kara miru to — from the point of view of; by the look of; judging from
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + から見ると to introduce a judgment made from a particular vantage point.

**JP:** 専門家から見ると、この作品は素晴らしいそうです。
**Reading:** Senmonka kara miru to, kono sakuhin wa subarashii sou desu.
**EN:** From an expert's point of view, this work is apparently wonderful.

## kara niwa — now that; since; so long as; because
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + からには to state a firm resolve or obligation that follows from a given fact.

**JP:** 引き受けたからには、最後までやり遂げます。
**Reading:** Hikiuketa kara niwa, saigo made yaritogemasu.
**EN:** Now that I've taken it on, I'll see it through to the end.

## kara shite — judging from; based on; since; from; even
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + からして to give a representative example that supports a broader judgment.

**JP:** その態度からして、彼はやる気がないようです。
**Reading:** Sono taido kara shite, kare wa yaruki ga nai you desu.
**EN:** Judging from his attitude alone, he doesn't seem motivated.

## kara suru to / kara sureba — judging from; considering; by the look of
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + からすると/からすれば to introduce an inference based on a given standpoint.

**JP:** 今の状況からすると、成功は難しそうです。
**Reading:** Ima no joukyou kara suru to, seikou wa muzukashisou desu.
**EN:** Judging from the current situation, success seems difficult.

## kara to itte — just because; even if; even though
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + からと言って to deny that a stated reason automatically justifies a conclusion.

**JP:** 若いからと言って、無理をしていいわけではありません。
**Reading:** Wakai kara to itte, muri o shite ii wake dewa arimasen.
**EN:** Just because you're young doesn't mean it's fine to overexert yourself.

## kkonai — no chance of; … is definitely not possible
**JLPT:** N2

A casual, contracted suffix attaching to the dictionary form of a verb + っこない to firmly deny any possibility.

**JP:** そんな高い物、買えっこないです。
**Reading:** Sonna takai mono, kaekkonai desu.
**EN:** There's no way I could afford something that expensive.

## koto da — should do (used for suggestions or giving advice)
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the dictionary or negative form of a verb + ことだ to give firm advice about what one ought to do.

**JP:** 心配なら、直接聞いてみることです。
**Reading:** Shinpai nara, chokusetsu kiite miru koto desu.
**EN:** If you're worried, you should just ask directly.

## koto dakara — because; since
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun known for a particular trait + ことだから to explain a conclusion based on that reputation.

**JP:** 彼のことだから、きっと時間通りに来るでしょう。
**Reading:** Kare no koto dakara, kitto jikandoori ni kuru deshou.
**EN:** Knowing him, he'll surely come right on time.

## koto ka — how…!; what…!
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a plain-form clause + ことか to express strong emotion or exclamation about a degree.

**JP:** どんなに嬉しかったことか。
**Reading:** Donna ni ureshikatta koto ka.
**EN:** How happy I was!

## koto naku — without doing something even once
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + ことなく to describe an action never once occurring during a period.

**JP:** 彼は一度も休むことなく働き続けました。
**Reading:** Kare wa ichido mo yasumu koto naku hataraki tsuzukemashita.
**EN:** He kept working without taking a single break.

## koto ni — emphasize speaker's feelings; to my (surprise/etc)
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to an adjective/verb + ことに to preface a statement with the speaker's emotional reaction to it.

**JP:** 驚いたことに、彼は試験に一番で合格しました。
**Reading:** Odoroita koto ni, kare wa shiken ni ichiban de goukaku shimashita.
**EN:** To my surprise, he passed the exam with the top score.

## koto niwa naranai — just because… doesn't mean
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + ことにはならない to deny that a given fact automatically leads to a certain conclusion.

**JP:** 謝ったからといって、許されることにはなりません。
**Reading:** Ayamatta kara to itte, yurusareru koto ni wa narimasen.
**EN:** Just because you apologized doesn't mean you'll be forgiven.
