# Grammar — N2 Batch 10 (17 points)

## tsutsu — while; even though; despite
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + つつ to describe two actions happening simultaneously, or to concede a contradictory fact, in formal register.

**JP:** テレビを見つつ、朝食を食べました。
**Reading:** Terebi o mitsutsu, choushoku o tabemashita.
**EN:** I ate breakfast while watching TV.

## tsutsu aru — to be doing; to be in the process of doing
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + つつある to describe an ongoing change or process still in progress, formal register.

**JP:** 経済は回復しつつあります。
**Reading:** Keizai wa kaifuku shitsutsu arimasu.
**EN:** The economy is in the process of recovering.

## ue wa — now that; since; as long as
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + 上は to state a firm resolve or obligation following a fixed circumstance, formal register.

**JP:** ここまで来た上は、最後までやります。
**Reading:** Koko made kita ue wa, saigo made yarimasu.
**EN:** Now that I've come this far, I'll see it through to the end.

## wa moto yori — also; let alone; from the beginning
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + はもとより to state an obvious base case before adding an even more notable point.

**JP:** 平日はもとより、休日も働いています。
**Reading:** Heijitsu wa moto yori, kyuujitsu mo hataraite imasu.
**EN:** Not to mention weekdays, I even work on holidays.

## wa tomokaku — anyhow; anyway; regardless; in any case; whether or not
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun + はともかく to set a topic aside temporarily before addressing another point.

**JP:** 味はともかく、見た目はきれいです。
**Reading:** Aji wa tomokaku, mitame wa kirei desu.
**EN:** Regardless of the taste, it looks beautiful.

## wazuka ni — slightly; only; barely; narrowly
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb describing a very small amount, degree, or margin.

**JP:** わずかに差で負けてしまいました。
**Reading:** Wazuka ni sa de makete shimaimashita.
**EN:** I lost by just a slight margin.

## yagate — before long; soon; almost; eventually
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb describing an event expected to happen naturally after some time passes.

**JP:** やがて桜の季節がやってきます。
**Reading:** Yagate sakura no kisetsu ga yatte kimasu.
**EN:** Before long, cherry blossom season will arrive.

## yara~yara — such things as A and B; A and B and so on
**JLPT:** N2

Repeats やら after listed items to give a non-exhaustive, somewhat chaotic-feeling list of examples.

**JP:** 部屋には本やらCDやらが散らかっています。
**Reading:** Heya ni wa hon yara CD yara ga chirakatte imasu.
**EN:** The room is a mess with things like books and CDs scattered around.

## yohodo / yoppodo — very; greatly; much; to a large extent
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb emphasizing that something is true to an unusually large degree.

**JP:** よほど疲れていたのか、すぐに眠ってしまいました。
**Reading:** Yohodo tsukarete ita no ka, sugu ni nemutte shimaimashita.
**EN:** He must have been really tired, since he fell asleep right away.

## yori [2] — from~ (a time, place, or person)
**JLPT:** N2

A formal, written equivalent of から marking a starting point in time, place, or source.

**JP:** 本日より、営業時間が変わります。
**Reading:** Honjitsu yori, eigyou jikan ga kawarimasu.
**EN:** Starting today, the business hours will change.

## yori hoka nai — to have no choice but
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + よりほかない to state that one course of action is the only option remaining.

**JP:** こうなったら、諦めるよりほかありません。
**Reading:** Kou nattara, akirameru yori hoka arimasen.
**EN:** If it's come to this, there's no choice but to give up.

## you dewa — if ~ (bad result)
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a plain-form clause + ようでは to introduce a hypothetical negative outcome as a warning or criticism.

**JP:** そんなに怠けているようでは、成功できません。
**Reading:** Sonna ni namakete iru you dewa, seikou dekimasen.
**EN:** If you keep slacking off like that, you won't succeed.

## you dewa nai ka — let's do (something); why don't we
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the volitional form of a verb + ではないか to make an enthusiastic suggestion or invitation, formal/literary register.

**JP:** みんなで力を合わせようではないか。
**Reading:** Minna de chikara o awaseyou dewa nai ka.
**EN:** Why don't we all join forces together?

## you ka~mai ka — whether or not; considering options
**JLPT:** N2

Pairs the volitional form of a verb + うか with the negative volitional + まいか to describe hesitation between doing something or not.

**JP:** 参加しようかしまいか、迷っています。
**Reading:** Sanka shiyou ka shimai ka, mayotte imasu.
**EN:** I'm torn about whether to participate or not.

## you suru ni — in short; in a word; to sum up; to put it simply
**JLPT:** N2

An adverbial phrase introducing a concise summary of a preceding, more complex explanation.

**JP:** 要するに、彼はやる気がないのです。
**Reading:** You suru ni, kare wa yaruki ga nai no desu.
**EN:** In short, he just isn't motivated.

## zaru o enai — cannot help (doing); have no choice but to
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb + ざるを得ない to describe being forced into an action against one's own preference.

**JP:** 状況を考えると、計画を変更せざるを得ません。
**Reading:** Joukyou o kangaeru to, keikaku o henkou sezaru o emasen.
**EN:** Considering the situation, we have no choice but to change the plan.

## zu ni sumu — get by without doing
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb + ずに済む to describe avoiding a necessary but undesirable action, a formal variant of なくて済む.

**JP:** 幸い、入院せずに済みました。
**Reading:** Saiwai, nyuuin sezu ni sumimashita.
**EN:** Fortunately, I managed to avoid being hospitalized.
