# でも — but / however

**JLPT level:** N5

A sentence-initial conjunction meaning "but" or "however" — softer and more casual than しかし.

## Example
- **JP:** 高いです。でも、買います。
- **Reading:** takai desu. demo, kaimasu.
- **EN:** It's expensive. But I'll buy it.
