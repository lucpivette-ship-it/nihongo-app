# Grammar — N3 Batch 10 (2 points)

## zuni wa irarenai — can't help but; must do
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb + ずにはいられない to describe an impulse or emotion so strong it can't be suppressed.

**JP:** その映画を見て、泣かずにはいられませんでした。
**Reading:** Sono eiga o mite, nakazu ni wa iraremasen deshita.
**EN:** After watching that movie, I couldn't help but cry.

## zutsu — apiece; each; at a time
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches after a quantity + ずつ to describe an equal distribution or a gradual, incremental pace.

**JP:** 一人に二枚ずつ配ってください。
**Reading:** Hitori ni nimai zutsu kubatte kudasai.
**EN:** Please hand out two sheets to each person.
