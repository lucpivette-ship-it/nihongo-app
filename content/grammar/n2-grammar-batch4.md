# Grammar — N2 Batch 4 (20 points)

## kuse shite — although; despite the fact that; even though
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + くせして to criticize a contradiction between someone's status/ability and their behavior, a blunter version of くせに.

**JP:** 知らないくせして、知ったかぶりをします。
**Reading:** Shiranai kuse shite, shittakaburi o shimasu.
**EN:** Even though he doesn't know, he acts like he does.

## madashimo — rather; better
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb used to say one option, while still not great, is preferable to another that's worse.

**JP:** 遅刻するくらいなら、まだしも休んだ方がいいです。
**Reading:** Chikoku suru kurai nara, madashimo yasunda hou ga ii desu.
**EN:** If you're just going to be late, it'd be better to take the day off instead.

## mai — will not; will probably not; intend not to; must not
**JLPT:** N2

A literary negative-volitional suffix attaching to the dictionary form of a verb + まい to express a firm intention not to do something.

**JP:** 二度と同じ失敗はするまいと誓いました。
**Reading:** Nido to onaji shippai wa surumai to chikaimashita.
**EN:** I vowed never to make the same mistake again.

## mama ni — as, to do as
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + ままに to describe an action carried out passively, following an outside influence.

**JP:** 言われるままに行動しました。
**Reading:** Iwareru mama ni koudou shimashita.
**EN:** I acted just as I was told.

## mattaku~nai — not at all
**JLPT:** N2

Pairs 全く with a negative predicate for strong, complete denial.

**JP:** その話は全く知りませんでした。
**Reading:** Sono hanashi wa mattaku shirimasen deshita.
**EN:** I didn't know that story at all.

## mo kamawazu — without caring; without worrying about
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + もかまわず to describe an action done without regard for a normally relevant concern.

**JP:** 雨が降るのもかまわず、外で遊びました。
**Reading:** Ame ga furu no mo kamawazu, soto de asobimashita.
**EN:** I played outside without caring that it was raining.

## mo touzen da — it's only natural; no wonder; might as well
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + も当然だ to affirm that a result is a completely reasonable, expected consequence.

**JP:** あんなに練習したのだから、上手になるのも当然です。
**Reading:** Anna ni renshuu shita no dakara, jouzu ni naru no mo touzen desu.
**EN:** Since he practiced that much, it's only natural that he got good.

## mono / mon — because; indicates reason/ excuse/ dissatisfaction
**JLPT:** N2

A casual sentence-ending particle giving a reason, often with a hint of complaint or self-justification.

**JP:** だって、疲れているんだもの。
**Reading:** Datte, tsukarete iru n da mono.
**EN:** Because I'm tired, that's why.

## mono da — describe feeling; express memories; state fact
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a plain-form clause + ものだ to express a general truth, a nostalgic memory, or an emotional realization.

**JP:** 子供はよく転ぶものです。
**Reading:** Kodomo wa yoku korobu mono desu.
**EN:** Children tend to fall down often (that's just how it is).

## mono dakara — so; therefore; the reason for something
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + ものだから to give an emotionally-charged reason or excuse for a result.

**JP:** 急に雨が降ってきたものだから、濡れてしまいました。
**Reading:** Kyuu ni ame ga futte kita mono dakara, nurete shimaimashita.
**EN:** It's because it suddenly started raining that I got wet.

## mono dewa nai — shouldn't do something; it's impossible to
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + ものではない to state a general moral prohibition or practical impossibility.

**JP:** 人の悪口を言うものではありません。
**Reading:** Hito no waruguchi o iu mono dewa arimasen.
**EN:** You shouldn't speak ill of people.

## mono ga aru — there is such a thing; to be the case that; feels like
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a plain-form clause + ものがある to affirm an emotional or qualitative impression, often with some nuance of admiration.

**JP:** 彼の演技には感動するものがあります。
**Reading:** Kare no engi ni wa kandou suru mono ga arimasu.
**EN:** There is something genuinely moving about his acting.

## mono ka / mon ka — as if (something untrue were actually true); there's no way
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a plain-form clause + ものか to strongly, emphatically deny a possibility.

**JP:** 二度とあんな店に行くものか。
**Reading:** Nido to anna mise ni iku mon ka.
**EN:** There's no way I'm ever going to that shop again.

## mono nara — if I/we could; if [A] is possible, then [B]
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the potential form of a verb + ものなら to introduce a hypothetical that's very unlikely or difficult to realize.

**JP:** できるものなら、もう一度やり直したいです。
**Reading:** Dekiru mono nara, mou ichido yarinaoshitai desu.
**EN:** If it were possible, I'd want to start over again.

## monono — but; although; even though
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a plain-form clause + ものの to concede a fact before introducing a contrasting reality.

**JP:** 約束したものの、結局行けませんでした。
**Reading:** Yakusoku shita mono no, kekkyoku ikemasen deshita.
**EN:** I promised to go, but in the end I couldn't.

## motto mo — but then; although; though
**JLPT:** N2

A conjunction adding a qualifying exception or reservation to a preceding statement.

**JP:** 彼は天才です。もっとも、努力もしていますが。
**Reading:** Kare wa tensai desu. Mottomo, doryoku mo shite imasu ga.
**EN:** He's a genius. Although, he does put in effort too.

## mou sukoshi de — almost; nearly, close to
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches before a clause + もう少しで to describe an event that nearly happened but was narrowly avoided or missed.

**JP:** もう少しで電車に乗り遅れるところでした。
**Reading:** Mou sukoshi de densha ni noriokureru tokoro deshita.
**EN:** I almost missed the train.

## nai dewa irarenai — can't help but feel; can't help but do
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the negative stem of a verb + ないではいられない to describe an urge too strong to resist.

**JP:** その映画を見て、泣かないではいられませんでした。
**Reading:** Sono eiga o mite, nakanai dewa iraremasen deshita.
**EN:** Watching that movie, I couldn't help but cry.

## nai koto niwa~nai — unless you
**JLPT:** N2

Pairs the negative form of a verb + ないことには with another negative to state a necessary precondition.

**JP:** 実際にやってみないことには分かりません。
**Reading:** Jissai ni yatte minai koto ni wa wakarimasen.
**EN:** Unless you actually try it, you won't know.

## naka o / naka dewa — in; on; in the midst of; when; while
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + 中を/中では to describe an action happening within an ongoing, often adverse, circumstance.

**JP:** 雨の中を、傘もささずに歩きました。
**Reading:** Ame no naka o, kasa mo sasazu ni arukimashita.
**EN:** I walked through the rain without even using an umbrella.
