# Grammar — N3 Batch 6 (20 points)

## sai ni — when; at the time of
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/verb + 際に to mark the occasion or moment when something takes place, a more formal version of とき.

**JP:** ご利用の際にはカードが必要です。
**Reading:** Goriyou no sai ni wa kaado ga hitsuyou desu.
**EN:** A card is required when using this service.

## saichuu ni — while; during; in the middle of
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/verb + 最中に to emphasize an action happening right in the middle of another ongoing event.

**JP:** 会議の最中に、電話が鳴りました。
**Reading:** Kaigi no saichuu ni, denwa ga narimashita.
**EN:** The phone rang right in the middle of the meeting.

## sara ni — furthermore; again; more and more
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb adding an additional point or intensifying degree, similar to さらなる ("further").

**JP:** さらに詳しく説明します。
**Reading:** Sara ni kuwashiku setsumei shimasu.
**EN:** I'll explain in even more detail.

## sate — well; now; then (conjunction)
**JLPT:** N3

A conjunction used to shift topics or transition to a new subject in conversation or writing.

**JP:** さて、次の議題に移りましょう。
**Reading:** Sate, tsugi no gidai ni utsurimashou.
**EN:** Well then, let's move on to the next topic.

## sei de — because of; due to (negative outcome)
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/clause + せいで to attribute a negative result to a cause, often blaming.

**JP:** 雨のせいで、試合が中止になりました。
**Reading:** Ame no sei de, shiai ga chuushi ni narimashita.
**EN:** The match was cancelled because of the rain.

## seizei — at the most; at best
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb setting an upper limit on a quantity or degree, implying it's not very much.

**JP:** せいぜい三日で終わるでしょう。
**Reading:** Seizei mikka de owaru deshou.
**EN:** It'll take three days at the most.

## shibaraku — for a moment; for a while
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb describing a short but indefinite period of time.

**JP:** しばらくお待ちください。
**Reading:** Shibaraku omachi kudasai.
**EN:** Please wait for a moment.

## shikanai — have no choice but to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + しかない to express that one option is the only remaining course of action.

**JP:** もう諦めるしかありません。
**Reading:** Mou akirameru shika arimasen.
**EN:** I have no choice but to give up now.

## sono tame ni — hence; for that reason
**JLPT:** N3

A conjunction linking a stated reason to its resulting action or plan.

**JP:** 試験があります。そのために、今週は勉強します。
**Reading:** Shiken ga arimasu. Sono tame ni, konshuu wa benkyou shimasu.
**EN:** There's an exam. For that reason, I'll study this week.

## soretomo — or; or else
**JLPT:** N3

A conjunction offering an alternative between two options, used only in questions.

**JP:** コーヒーにしますか。それとも紅茶にしますか。
**Reading:** Koohii ni shimasu ka. Soretomo koucha ni shimasu ka.
**EN:** Will you have coffee? Or would you rather have tea?

## sou mo nai / sou ni nai — very unlikely to
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + そうもない/そうにない to say there's no sign of something happening.

**JP:** 今日中に終わりそうもありません。
**Reading:** Kyoujuu ni owarisou mo arimasen.
**EN:** It doesn't look like it'll be finished by today.

## sude ni — already
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb indicating that something has already occurred or is already the case by the time of speaking.

**JP:** 会議はすでに始まっています。
**Reading:** Kaigi wa sude ni hajimatte imasu.
**EN:** The meeting has already started.

## sunawachi — in other words; namely
**JLPT:** N3

A formal conjunction used to restate or clarify a preceding statement.

**JP:** 彼は私の父の兄、すなわち伯父です。
**Reading:** Kare wa watashi no chichi no ani, sunawachi oji desu.
**EN:** He is my father's older brother, in other words, my uncle.

## suuryou + wa — at least
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches a quantity + は to emphasize that an amount is a minimum, often surprisingly large.

**JP:** 千人はこのイベントに参加しました。
**Reading:** Sen-nin wa kono ibento ni sanka shimashita.
**EN:** At least a thousand people took part in this event.

## ta mono da — used to do; would often do
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the plain past form of a verb + ものだ to recall a habitual action from the past with nostalgia.

**JP:** 子供の頃、よくここで遊んだものです。
**Reading:** Kodomo no koro, yoku koko de asonda mono desu.
**EN:** When I was a child, I used to play here often.

## ta totan — as soon as; just as
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the plain past form of a verb + とたん to describe something happening immediately after another event, often unexpectedly.

**JP:** ドアを開けたとたん、猫が飛び出しました。
**Reading:** Doa o aketa totan, neko ga tobidashimashita.
**EN:** The moment I opened the door, the cat jumped out.

## tabi ni — whenever; every time
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/verb + たびに to describe an action that consistently accompanies another.

**JP:** 彼女に会うたびに、元気をもらいます。
**Reading:** Kanojo ni au tabi ni, genki o moraimasu.
**EN:** Every time I see her, I get energized.

## tame ni — for; in order to; because of
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/verb + ために to express purpose, benefit, or cause depending on context.

**JP:** 健康のために、毎日運動します。
**Reading:** Kenkou no tame ni, mainichi undou shimasu.
**EN:** I exercise every day for the sake of my health.

## tashika ni — surely; certainly
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb affirming that a statement is true, often used to concede a point before adding a "but."

**JP:** 確かに、彼の言う通りです。
**Reading:** Tashika ni, kare no iu toori desu.
**EN:** Certainly, it's just as he says.

## tate — just done; freshly done
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + たて to emphasize that something was just completed and is still fresh.

**JP:** 焼きたてのパンはおいしいです。
**Reading:** Yakitate no pan wa oishii desu.
**EN:** Freshly baked bread is delicious.
