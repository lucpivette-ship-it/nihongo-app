# Grammar — N1 Batch 2 (20 points)

## demo nan demo nai / kumo nan tomo nai — not in the least; nothing like that
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches after a noun/adjective with でも何でもない or くも何ともない to flatly deny that something is even close to being the case.

**JP:** 友達でも何でもない。
**Reading:** Tomodachi demo nan demo nai.
**EN:** He's not my friend at all — nothing like that.

## denakute nan darou — must be; is definitely
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + でなくてなんだろう as a rhetorical question emphasizing that something can only be described one way.

**JP:** これが奇跡でなくてなんだろう。
**Reading:** Kore ga kiseki de nakute nan darou.
**EN:** If this isn't a miracle, what is?

## dewa arumai ka — isn't it; I wonder if it's not
**JLPT:** N1

A literary rhetorical construction expressing a tentative but confident guess, similar to ではないか but more formal.

**JP:** それは誤解ではあるまいか。
**Reading:** Sore wa gokai de wa arumai ka.
**EN:** Isn't that perhaps a misunderstanding?

## dewa arumai shi — it's not like; it isn't as if
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + ではあるまいし to dismiss a premise as clearly untrue before making a point.

**JP:** 子供ではあるまいし、自分で決めなさい。
**Reading:** Kodomo de wa arumai shi, jibun de kimenasai.
**EN:** You're not a child, so decide for yourself.

## dewa sumanai — it doesn't end with just; it will take more than
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun/clause + では済まない to state that a situation demands more than the stated minimal response.

**JP:** 謝るだけでは済まない問題だ。
**Reading:** Ayamaru dake de wa sumanai mondai da.
**EN:** This is a problem that won't be resolved just by apologizing.

## dou nimo~nai — not … by any means; no matter how hard one tries, cannot
**JLPT:** N1

Pairs どうにも with a negative predicate to emphasize complete helplessness despite effort.

**JP:** どうにも我慢できない。
**Reading:** Dou nimo gaman dekinai.
**EN:** I simply cannot bear it, no matter what.

## ga hayai ka — no sooner than; as soon as
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + が早いか to describe an immediate second action following the first almost instantly.

**JP:** ベルが鳴るが早いか、生徒たちは駆け出した。
**Reading:** Beru ga naru ga hayai ka, seito-tachi wa kakedashita.
**EN:** No sooner had the bell rung than the students ran out.

## ga/mo~nara, ~mo~da — negative connection/comparison (like father like son)
**JLPT:** N1

A parallel construction pairing two related nouns with なら/も to criticize both parties equally, in a "like X, like Y" pattern.

**JP:** 親が親なら子も子だ。
**Reading:** Oya ga oya nara ko mo ko da.
**EN:** Like father, like son (said critically of both).

## gamashii — look like; sound like; approximate; similar to; somewhat like
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun stem + がましい to describe something that resembles, in a slightly negative way, a certain quality.

**JP:** 言い訳がましい態度はやめなさい。
**Reading:** Iiwake gamashii taido wa yamenasai.
**EN:** Stop acting so much like you're making excuses.

## gatera — while; on the same occasion; at the same time; coincidentally
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + がてら to describe doing a secondary activity incidentally while performing a main one.

**JP:** 散歩がてら、買い物をした。
**Reading:** Sanpo gatera, kaimono o shita.
**EN:** I did some shopping while out for a walk.

## gotoki / gotoku / gotoshi — like; as if; the same as
**JLPT:** N1

A classical literary equivalent of ような/ように, attaching to a noun or clause to make a comparison.

**JP:** 光陰矢のごとし。
**Reading:** Kouin ya no gotoshi.
**EN:** Time flies like an arrow.

## gurumi — together (with); -wide
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + ぐるみ to describe something that includes an entire group or extent, "-wide" or "whole."

**JP:** 町ぐるみで祭りを盛り上げる。
**Reading:** Machi gurumi de matsuri o moriageru.
**EN:** The whole town comes together to liven up the festival.

## hame ni naru — to get stuck with; to end up with (something unpleasant)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + 羽目になる to describe ending up in an undesirable situation, often through one's own doing.

**JP:** 遅刻して怒られる羽目になった。
**Reading:** Chikoku shite okorareru hame ni natta.
**EN:** I was late and ended up getting scolded.

## hodo no koto dewa nai — it's not worth; no need to
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a clause + ほどのことではない to downplay the significance of a matter.

**JP:** 泣くほどのことではない。
**Reading:** Naku hodo no koto dewa nai.
**EN:** It's not something worth crying over.

## hou ga mashi da — better than; would rather
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a clause + ほうがましだ to say one option, though undesirable, is still preferable to an even worse alternative.

**JP:** 死んだほうがましだ。
**Reading:** Shinda hou ga mashi da.
**EN:** I'd rather die (than do that) — an expression of extreme reluctance.

## houdai — doing as one pleases; to one's heart's content; leaving uncontrolled
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + 放題 to describe an unrestrained, all-you-can-do action, often used for "all you can eat."

**JP:** このレストランは食べ放題だ。
**Reading:** Kono resutoran wa tabehoudai da.
**EN:** This restaurant is all-you-can-eat.

## ikan da / ikan dewa / ikan ni yotte wa — in accordance with; depending on; whether or not
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + 如何だ/如何では to describe an outcome that depends entirely on a certain factor, formal register.

**JP:** 結果は努力如何だ。
**Reading:** Kekka wa doryoku ikan da.
**EN:** The result depends on your effort.

## ikan ni kakawarazu / ikan ni yorazu / ikan o towazu — regardless of; whether or not; doesn't matter whether
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + 如何にかかわらず to state that a result is unaffected by a given factor, very formal register.

**JP:** 理由の如何にかかわらず、遅刻は遅刻だ。
**Reading:** Riyuu no ikan ni kakawarazu, chikoku wa chikoku da.
**EN:** Regardless of the reason, being late is being late.

## ikanaru — any kind of; every; whatsoever; whatever
**JLPT:** N1

A formal pre-noun adjectival meaning "any kind of," used with a negative or conditional clause for emphasis.

**JP:** いかなる困難にも負けない。
**Reading:** Ikanaru konnan ni mo makenai.
**EN:** I won't lose to any kind of difficulty.

## ikani — how; in what way; how much; to what extent
**JLPT:** N1

A formal, literary adverb equivalent to どんなに, used to emphasize a high degree in exclamatory or rhetorical statements.

**JP:** いかに困難であっても、諦めない。
**Reading:** Ikani konnan de attemo, akiramenai.
**EN:** However difficult it may be, I won't give up.
