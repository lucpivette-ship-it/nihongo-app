# Grammar — N1 Batch 4 (20 points)

## koto gotoku — altogether; entirely; completely
**JLPT:** N1

A formal literary adverb attaching to a noun + ことごとく to describe totality, "each and every one."

**JP:** 彼の予想はことごとく外れた。
**Reading:** Kare no yosou wa kotogotoku hazureta.
**EN:** Every single one of his predictions was wrong.

## koto kono ue nai / kono ue nai / kono ue naku — the most of all; the best; nothing is more ... than
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun/adjective + この上ない to state that something has reached the absolute highest degree possible.

**JP:** これ以上の喜びはこの上ない。
**Reading:** Kore ijou no yorokobi wa kono ue nai.
**EN:** There is no greater joy than this.

## koto mo atte — partly because; also because of; plus the reason for
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a clause + こともあって to name one of several contributing reasons for a situation.

**JP:** 天気が良かったこともあって、公園は賑わっていた。
**Reading:** Tenki ga yokatta koto mo atte, kouen wa nigiwatte ita.
**EN:** Partly because the weather was nice, the park was crowded.

## koto nashi ni — without doing something
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + ことなしに to state that an action was carried out without first doing another.

**JP:** 努力することなしに成功はない。
**Reading:** Doryoku suru koto nashi ni seikou wa nai.
**EN:** There's no success without effort.

## koto no nai you ni — so as not to; to not
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a clause + ことのないように to express a cautionary intention to prevent a certain outcome.

**JP:** 同じ間違いを繰り返すことのないようにしたい。
**Reading:** Onaji machigai o kurikaesu koto no nai you ni shitai.
**EN:** I want to make sure not to repeat the same mistake.

## koto tote — because; since
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a clause + こととて to state a formal reason, often mitigating or excusing a fault, literary register.

**JP:** 慣れぬこととて、失礼をお許しください。
**Reading:** Narenu koto tote, shitsurei o oyurushi kudasai.
**EN:** Since I'm not used to this, please forgive any rudeness.

## kurai nara — rather than (do ...)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a clause + くらいなら to state a strong preference for an alternative over the stated undesirable option.

**JP:** 謝るくらいなら、最初からしません。
**Reading:** Ayamaru kurai nara, saisho kara shimasen.
**EN:** Rather than apologize (later), I wouldn't do it in the first place.

## kurai no mono da — only (emphasis)
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + くらいのものだ to emphasize that only a very limited thing/person qualifies.

**JP:** それができるのは彼くらいのものだ。
**Reading:** Sore ga dekiru no wa kare kurai no mono da.
**EN:** He's about the only one who can do that.

## made da / made no koto da — only; just; nothing else
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the dictionary/plain form of a verb + までだ to state that a simple, minimal course of action is all that's needed.

**JP:** だめなら、また挑戦するまでだ。
**Reading:** Dame nara, mata chousen suru made da.
**EN:** If it doesn't work, I'll just try again.

## made mo nai / made mo naku — there's no need to; it's not necessary to
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + までもない to state that an action, while possible, isn't necessary given the obviousness of the situation.

**JP:** 言うまでもなく、健康が一番大切だ。
**Reading:** Iu made mo naku, kenkou ga ichiban taisetsu da.
**EN:** Needless to say, health is the most important thing.

## majiki — should not; must not
**JLPT:** N1

A classical negative-obligation suffix attaching to the dictionary form of a verb, formal/literary register.

**JP:** 許すまじき行為だ。
**Reading:** Yurusumajiki koui da.
**EN:** It's an act that must not be forgiven.

## makuru — to do over and over again; to do relentlessly
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + まくる to describe doing an action excessively or relentlessly.

**JP:** 一日中働きまくった。
**Reading:** Ichinichijuu hatarakimakutta.
**EN:** I worked relentlessly all day long.

## mamire — covered with; stained; smeared with
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + まみれ to describe being thoroughly covered in something messy or unpleasant.

**JP:** 汗まみれになって働いた。
**Reading:** Ase mamire ni natte hataraita.
**EN:** I worked until I was covered in sweat.

## marukkiri — completely; absolutely; totally; (not) at all
**JLPT:** N1

A colloquial, emphatic variant of まるで, used with an affirmative or negative predicate for total emphasis.

**JP:** 彼の話はまるっきり信じられない。
**Reading:** Kare no hanashi wa marukkiri shinjirarenai.
**EN:** I can't believe his story at all.

## meku — seems; show signs of; to have the appearance of
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + めく to describe something taking on the characteristic feel of a season, mood, or quality.

**JP:** 春めいてきた。
**Reading:** Harumeite kita.
**EN:** It's started to feel like spring.

## mo douzen da — just like; same as
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + も同然だ to state that something is practically equivalent to another thing, even if not literally identical.

**JP:** この試合は勝ったも同然だ。
**Reading:** Kono shiai wa katta mo douzen da.
**EN:** This match is as good as won.

## mo saru koto nagara — one may well say so, but; not only... but also
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a noun + もさることながら to acknowledge one point before shifting emphasis to an even more important one.

**JP:** 味もさることながら、盛り付けも美しい。
**Reading:** Aji mo saru koto nagara, moritsuke mo utsukushii.
**EN:** The taste is great, and what's more, the presentation is beautiful too.

## mo shinai de — without even doing
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb + もしないで to describe an action skipped entirely, often with a critical nuance.

**JP:** 挨拶もしないで帰ってしまった。
**Reading:** Aisatsu mo shinai de kaette shimatta.
**EN:** He left without even saying goodbye.

## mohaya — already; now; no longer; not anymore
**JLPT:** N1

A formal adverb marking that a previous state has definitively ended or reached a new stage.

**JP:** もはや後戻りはできない。
**Reading:** Mohaya atomodori wa dekinai.
**EN:** There's no turning back now.

## mono de — because; for that reason
**JLPT:** N1

Attaches to a plain-form clause + もので to give an explanatory reason, often as a mild excuse, in conversational speech.

**JP:** 急いでいたもので、失礼しました。
**Reading:** Isoide ita mono de, shitsurei shimashita.
**EN:** I was in a hurry, so please excuse me.
