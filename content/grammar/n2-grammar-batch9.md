# Grammar — N2 Batch 9 (20 points)

## te touzen da — natural; as a matter of course
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the て-form of a verb/adjective + 当然だ to affirm that a result is a completely expected, reasonable consequence.

**JP:** あんな態度をとれば、嫌われて当然です。
**Reading:** Anna taido o toreba, kirawarete touzen desu.
**EN:** If you act like that, it's only natural you'd be disliked.

## tewa / dewa — whenever; if; when; repetitive action
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + は to describe a repeated cycle of an action and its consequence.

**JP:** 食べては寝て、食べては寝ての生活です。
**Reading:** Tabete wa nete, tabete wa nete no seikatsu desu.
**EN:** It's a life of eating and sleeping, eating and sleeping.

## tewa irarenai — can't afford to; unable to
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + はいられない to say a situation makes continuing an action impossible or inappropriate.

**JP:** これ以上、黙ってはいられません。
**Reading:** Kore ijou, damatte wa iraremasen.
**EN:** I can't stay silent about this any longer.

## tewa naranai — must not; cannot; should not
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + はならない to express a strong, formal prohibition.

**JP:** ここでタバコを吸ってはなりません。
**Reading:** Koko de tabako o sutte wa narimasen.
**EN:** You must not smoke here.

## tewa~tewa — repetitive situations/actions
**JLPT:** N2

Repeats ては with two contrasting or cyclical verbs to describe an ongoing back-and-forth pattern.

**JP:** 消しては書き、消しては書きを繰り返しました。
**Reading:** Keshite wa kaki, keshite wa kaki o kurikaeshimashita.
**EN:** I kept erasing and rewriting, over and over.

## to douji ni — at the same time as; while; simultaneously
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a noun/clause + と同時に to describe two events occurring at exactly the same moment.

**JP:** ベルが鳴ると同時に、授業が始まりました。
**Reading:** Beru ga naru to douji ni, jugyou ga hajimarimashita.
**EN:** Just as the bell rang, class began.

## to itta — like; such as
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches after a noun + といった to give one or two representative examples of a broader category.

**JP:** 寿司や天ぷらといった日本料理が好きです。
**Reading:** Sushi ya tenpura to itta nihon ryouri ga suki desu.
**EN:** I like Japanese food such as sushi and tempura.

## to iu fuu ni — in such a way that
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches after a clause + というふうに to describe the specific manner in which something is done or perceived.

**JP:** こういうふうに考えれば、問題は簡単です。
**Reading:** Kou iu fuu ni kangaereba, mondai wa kantan desu.
**EN:** If you think about it in this way, the problem is simple.

## to iu koto wa — that is to say; so that means; in other words
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + ということは to draw a logical conclusion from a preceding fact.

**JP:** 返事がないということは、忙しいのでしょう。
**Reading:** Henji ga nai to iu koto wa, isogashii no deshou.
**EN:** The fact that there's no reply means he's probably busy.

## to iu mono da — something like; something called
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + というものだ to make a general, definitional statement about the nature of something.

**JP:** それが人生というものです。
**Reading:** Sore ga jinsei to iu mono desu.
**EN:** That's just what life is like.

## to iu mono dewa nai — there is no guarantee that…; not necessarily
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + というものではない to deny that a stated condition always guarantees a certain result.

**JP:** お金があれば幸せというものではありません。
**Reading:** Okane ga areba shiawase to iu mono dewa arimasen.
**EN:** Having money doesn't necessarily mean you're happy.

## to kangaerareru — one can think that; it is conceivable that; considered
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a plain-form clause + と考えられる to present a reasoned inference or hypothesis, common in academic writing.

**JP:** 原因は複数あると考えられます。
**Reading:** Gen'in wa fukusuu aru to kangaeraremasu.
**EN:** It is thought that there are multiple causes.

## toka (de) — I heard that
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + とか to report hearsay information casually and somewhat vaguely.

**JP:** 彼は来月結婚するとか。
**Reading:** Kare wa raigetsu kekkon suru toka.
**EN:** I heard he's getting married next month, or something like that.

## tokku ni — long ago; already; a long time ago
**JLPT:** N2

An adverb emphasizing that something happened well before the present, further back than すでに implies.

**JP:** その映画はとっくに見ました。
**Reading:** Sono eiga wa tokku ni mimashita.
**EN:** I already watched that movie a long time ago.

## tokoro datta — was just about to do something; something almost happened
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb + ところだった to describe an action or event that was on the verge of happening.

**JP:** もう少しで転ぶところでした。
**Reading:** Mou sukoshi de korobu tokoro deshita.
**EN:** I almost fell over.

## tokoro ni — at the time; just as I was
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + ところに to describe an event interrupting or coinciding with an ongoing action.

**JP:** 出かけようとしたところに、電話が鳴りました。
**Reading:** Dekakeyou to shita tokoro ni, denwa ga narimashita.
**EN:** Just as I was about to head out, the phone rang.

## tokoro o miru to — judging from; seeing that
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a clause + ところを見ると to draw a conclusion based on a directly observed piece of evidence.

**JP:** 電気が消えているところを見ると、留守のようです。
**Reading:** Denki ga kiete iru tokoro o miru to, rusu no you desu.
**EN:** Seeing that the lights are off, it looks like nobody's home.

## tomo — even if; no matter (who, what, when, where, why, how); though
**JLPT:** N2

A formal suffix attaching to the volitional/dictionary form of a verb or an adjective stem + とも to concede a point unconditionally.

**JP:** 何があろうとも、諦めません。
**Reading:** Nani ga arou tomo, akiramemasen.
**EN:** No matter what happens, I won't give up.

## toshite~nai — not at all; there is not even one
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a counter + として with a negative predicate to emphatically deny even a single instance.

**JP:** 一日として休んだ日はありません。
**Reading:** Ichinichi toshite yasunda hi wa arimasen.
**EN:** There hasn't been a single day I've taken off.

## toshitemo — assuming; even if
**JLPT:** N2

Attaches to a plain-form clause + としても to concede a hypothetical premise while pointing out it doesn't change the conclusion.

**JP:** 明日雨が降るとしても、試合は行われます。
**Reading:** Ashita ame ga furu to shitemo, shiai wa okonawaremasu.
**EN:** Even if it rains tomorrow, the match will be held.
