# Grammar — N3 Batch 7 (20 points)

## tatoe~temo — even if... is the case
**JLPT:** N3

Pairs たとえ with a ても-form clause to emphasize that even an extreme hypothetical wouldn't change the outcome.

**JP:** たとえ雨が降っても、行きます。
**Reading:** Tatoe ame ga futtemo, ikimasu.
**EN:** Even if it rains, I'm going.

## tatoeba — for example; for instance
**JLPT:** N3

An adverb introducing a concrete example to illustrate a preceding general statement.

**JP:** 例えば、日本には富士山という有名な山があります。
**Reading:** Tatoeba, Nihon ni wa Fujisan to iu yuumei na yama ga arimasu.
**EN:** For example, Japan has a famous mountain called Mt. Fuji.

## tatte — even if; even though; no matter how
**JLPT:** N3

A casual, contracted form of ても used in spoken Japanese to concede a point.

**JP:** 急いだって間に合いません。
**Reading:** Isoidatte maniaimasen.
**EN:** Even if I hurry, I won't make it in time.

## te bakari iru — only; nothing but
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + ばかりいる to criticize someone for repeatedly doing only one thing.

**JP:** 彼はゲームをしてばかりいます。
**Reading:** Kare wa geemu o shite bakari imasu.
**EN:** He does nothing but play games.

## te goran — (please) try to; (please) look
**JLPT:** N3

A gentle, encouraging imperative attaching to the て-form of a verb + ごらん, often used by parents/teachers.

**JP:** 自分でやってごらん。
**Reading:** Jibun de yatte goran.
**EN:** Try doing it by yourself.

## te hajimete — not until; only after
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + はじめて to describe realizing or experiencing something only after a specific event.

**JP:** 大人になってはじめて、親のありがたさが分かりました。
**Reading:** Otona ni natte hajimete, oya no arigatasa ga wakarimashita.
**EN:** It wasn't until I became an adult that I understood how grateful I should be to my parents.

## te kara de nai to — must first do; cannot do without first doing
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + からでないと to state a required prerequisite before another action can happen.

**JP:** 予約をしてからでないと、入れません。
**Reading:** Yoyaku o shite kara de nai to, hairemasen.
**EN:** You can't enter without making a reservation first.

## te shou ga nai / te shikata ga nai — can't help but; very
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the て-form of an adjective/verb + しょうがない/しかたがない to express an uncontrollable feeling or state.

**JP:** 眠くてしょうがないです。
**Reading:** Nemukute shou ga nai desu.
**EN:** I'm unbearably sleepy.

## te sumu — sufficient by; resolved by
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + 済む to describe a situation that can be resolved with a minimal action.

**JP:** 謝って済む問題ではありません。
**Reading:** Ayamatte sumu mondai dewa arimasen.
**EN:** This isn't a problem that can be resolved just by apologizing.

## te wa ikenai kara — in order to not; to prevent
**JLPT:** N3

Combines てはいけない with から to explain a reason rooted in avoiding a negative situation.

**JP:** 風邪をひいてはいけないから、暖かくしてください。
**Reading:** Kaze o hiite wa ikenai kara, atatakaku shite kudasai.
**EN:** So you don't catch a cold, please keep warm.

## teiru baai janai — this is no time to be doing
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the ている form of a verb + 場合じゃない to stress that the current action is inappropriate given the urgency of the situation.

**JP:** 今は休んでいる場合じゃありません。
**Reading:** Ima wa yasunde iru baai ja arimasen.
**EN:** This is no time to be resting.

## teki — -like; changes noun into na-adjective
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun + 的 to turn it into a な-adjective describing a quality or characteristic.

**JP:** それは個人的な意見です。
**Reading:** Sore wa kojinteki na iken desu.
**EN:** That's a personal opinion.

## temo hajimaranai — even if... it's no use
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + も始まらない to say that an action wouldn't lead anywhere productive.

**JP:** 今さら後悔しても始まりません。
**Reading:** Imasara koukai shitemo hajimarimasen.
**EN:** There's no point regretting it now.

## temo kamawanai — it doesn't matter if
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the て-form of a verb/adjective + も構わない to grant permission or express indifference to an outcome.

**JP:** 遅れても構いません。
**Reading:** Okuretemo kamaimasen.
**EN:** It's fine even if you're late.

## temo shou ga nai / temo shikata ga nai — there's no point to; it's no use
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to the て-form of a verb + もしょうがない/もしかたがない to say an action would be futile.

**JP:** 泣いてもしょうがないですよ。
**Reading:** Naitemo shou ga nai desu yo.
**EN:** There's no use crying about it.

## to ieba — speaking of; when you talk of
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/clause + と言えば to bring up a related topic that comes to mind.

**JP:** 日本料理と言えば、寿司ですね。
**Reading:** Nihon ryouri to ieba, sushi desu ne.
**EN:** Speaking of Japanese food, sushi comes to mind.

## to ii / tara ii — it would be nice if; I hope
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a plain-form clause + といい/たらいい to express a wish or hope for a favorable outcome.

**JP:** 明日晴れるといいですね。
**Reading:** Ashita hareru to ii desu ne.
**EN:** I hope it's sunny tomorrow.

## to ittemo — although I say; although one might say
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a clause + と言っても to soften a preceding statement by adding a qualifying nuance.

**JP:** 得意と言っても、少しだけです。
**Reading:** Tokui to ittemo, sukoshi dake desu.
**EN:** Although I say I'm good at it, it's only a little.

## to iu koto da — I heard; it means that
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a plain-form clause + ということだ to report secondhand information or summarize a conclusion.

**JP:** 彼は来月結婚するということです。
**Reading:** Kare wa raigetsu kekkon suru to iu koto desu.
**EN:** I heard that he's getting married next month.

## to iu nowa — this means; the meaning of ... is; because
**JLPT:** N3

Attaches to a noun/clause + というのは to introduce a definition or explanation for a term or reason.

**JP:** 「もったいない」というのは、無駄にするのが惜しいという意味です。
**Reading:** "Mottainai" to iu nowa, muda ni suru no ga oshii to iu imi desu.
**EN:** "Mottainai" means it's a shame to waste something.
